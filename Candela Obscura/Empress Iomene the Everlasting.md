Iomene the Everlasting, as she was known, is the subject of great debate. Official history, as espoused by the [[Ascendancy]], suggests a matriarchal line of daughters all named Iomene, but the writings I have studied strongly suggest this is a fabrication. She extended her life through the use of [[magick]] and alchemy. This would make her one of the Unabridged.

Her reign saw the widespread use of [[alchemy]], which became an integral part of daily life in [[Oldfaire]], much like our use of electricity today. Her council of alchemists built the Ziggurat to house and utilize these magickal phenomena, but they were ill-equipped to manage the "bleed" it produced. The disastrous misuse of this power is what led to the city's eventual fall.

A gradual decline into violence and paranoia, as hinted at in ancient writings, points to the corruption of bleed, which is a key characteristic of the [[Unabridged]]. I believe that Empress Iomene's downfall was not a sudden event, but the slow, corrosive effect of this magickal energy. The idea that she might still be "living"—or rather, preserved by her unnatural state—shudders my very soul. I must continue my investigation to determine her final resting place, or if she still exists somewhere within the buried city below. The search for her body, or the lack thereof, is crucial to understanding the full truth of [[Oldfaire]]'s demise.
