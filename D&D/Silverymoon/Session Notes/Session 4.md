#### Rachel's Recap
Alustriel approached the party with a more personal mission: a tenday ago, Drizzt had gone to make peace between a dwarven mining expedition and an emerald dragon, but she hadn't heard from him in two days and couldn't *scry* on him. She lent Vath a heart-shaped gold locket with portraits of herself and Drizzt inside, to help find him, since it's enchanted to warm in proximity to him.

The party set off on their tenday-long journey, and observed a foraging brown bear. Ned wanted to befriend it, but the rest of the party persuaded him not to approach. Neon found the dragon's lair first, marked by a volcano-shaped circle of boulders, and the party snuck in.

After using magic to sneak and poke around, Audire woke the emerald dragon and she conversed telepathically in the party's heads, dealing psychic damage though seemingly without intending to. She knew who Drizzt (a dragon slayer) and Alustriel (the child of a god) were, and seemed afraid of them. She agreed to leave the area and let the party take Drizzt back to Silverymoon, but said that he had been "damaged" by her long conversations with him.

Neon found a hidden tunnel, at the end of which Drizzt had been carefully laid out as if sleeping, with all his gear intact and Guen's onyx figurine in his hand. Vath cast *remove curse*, which helped a little but did not dispel the overwhelming psionic residue that Audire's *detect magic* showed around him.

The party traveled back to Silverymoon with Tem carrying Drizzt. En route, Ned spotted a goblin hunting camp, and suggested that Audire make them minions. Audire refused, saying he preferred to do his own work, while Neon remarked that minions could be useful. Due to Vath, Audire, and Tem having no interest in fighting goblins, the party did not engage.

When they arrived back in Silverymoon, Alustriel gave them the use of Sylvaris Manor, a home near the High Palace, saying that it was good to keep heroes close. She also gave them each 500 gp for discretionary spending. And then she focused on reviving Drizzt, who remained comatose ever since the party found him.
