A 22-year-old shepherd in [[Growm]].

- **Status**: "Not Dead." She is a walking anomaly who was sewed back together by [[The Apothecary (Rachel)]] after some kind of accident involving a hole in her midsection.
- **Occupation**: Tends to the [[Sheepdragons]].
- **Personality**: Quiet, whimsical, but highly responsible. She spends her time studying and collecting trinkets (beads, ribbons, pins) that jingle when she walks.
- **Connections**: Grew up alongside the son of [[Ewen Fen]].
