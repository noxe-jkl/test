The specific day when the dead are permitted to walk the land.

- **Frequency**: There is a "Great Tombs Day" held once a year. There is some debate if smaller occurrences happen weekly before the Holy Day.
- **Rites**: To prevent the dead from wandering off before funeral rites are finished, locals apply black tar to the feet of the deceased.
- **Significance**: Highly revered by the citizens of [[Growm]].
