Small, cold village located in the northern reaches of [[The Empire]]. Characterized by persistent rain and heavy mist.

### Layout & Architecture

- Built in a star-shaped configuration.
- Surrounded by the [[Saint's River]]. It is said fey and spirits cannot cross this sacred water.
- Houses typically have blue and orange roofs.
- Governance: Currently communal. The old Mayor lived in the [[Manor House]] on one of the star points, but no one replaced him after he died.

### Key Locations

- **[[The South Graveyard]]**: Suspended graves and mechanical tombs.
- **[[Manor House]]**: Former seat of the mayor.
- **[[Lighthouse of Growm]]**: Situated on a star point. No current body of water nearby, but legends say there used to be.
- **Saint's Chapels**: Places of local worship.
- **The Old Church of the Quiet-Light**: Gathering place for those following [[The Quiet Lord]].
- **Overgrown Gardens**: Managed by specialized gardeners to appease the local flora.

### Culture & Economy

- People are superstitious but quiet. Common phrases: "Growmth luck" and "Foul Fair tonight."    
- **Currency**: Silver is often valued as highly as gold ($1 sp$ for complex tools). Gold is rare and primarily used to repel [[Fey]].
- **Livestock**: Known for herding [[Sheepdragons]].
- **Mines**: Located to the North-West. Currently collapsed due to a "mad miner's folly."
