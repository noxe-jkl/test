The local healer of [[Growm]], though some whisper she is a witch.

- **Home**: An old stone tower with a "suspicious" basement. She claims she used to have a much grander, palatial tower elsewhere.
- **Skills**: Expert in herbalism and healing magic. She is responsible for sewing [[Autumn]] back together and maintaining her "Not Dead" state.
- **Magic Style**: Uses magic to enhance plant growth, making them more potent for potions.
