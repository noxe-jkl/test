**Thalendir Aurelian Valerius Aldrien Corvane Vael’Thir von Ashkarael de Vaelthrix Vossaryn, the Ninth of His Designation, Exile by Decree, Witness to the Broken Thread, The Disowned Who Found the Fallen, Artificer of His Own Design** 

is a drow artificer of Vossaryn origin, formally stripped of name and standing after violating doctrinal limits fundamental to his society’s stability. Born into the Vossaryn aristocracy, he distinguished himself through precision craft and theoretical work that treated divine influence not as an object of devotion, but as a system governed by reproducible principles.

His exile followed sanctioned research aimed at improving the reliability of divine outcomes through the isolation and stabilization of Lolth’s favor without reliance on ritual emotion or divine caprice. Though he did not deny Lolth’s supremacy, his work implied that her power could be rendered predictable and functionally independent of worship, crossing a boundary  the Vossaryn could not tolerate. In lieu of execution, the enclave issued a decree of exile accompanied by punitive maiming: the removal of his arm, intended to permanently diminish his capacity to craft and to mark his disgrace.

Since his banishment, Thalendir has operated beyond Vossaryn influence, replacing the lost limb with a superior mechanical prosthesis and pursuing knowledge in regions defined by instability and forbidden study. He remains committed to the belief that perfection is attainable and that failure is merely evidence of unfinished design, seeking to build a legacy that renders both his exile and the authority behind it irrelevant.

## Story Beats
- Successfully reproduce an effect believed to require divine favor through purely mechanical or arcane means. 
- Meaningfully upgrade the prosthetic arm in response to a failure of the old. 
- Have an NPC acknowledge Thalendir as a peer or threat based entirely on competence rather than title. 
- Force a rigid hierarchy to grant entry or authority based purely on utility rather than lineage. 
- Confront a younger mind from his homeland and demonstrate that the exile was a result of success, not failure.

## RP Cheat Sheet

**Core Belief:**  
The world is a system. Systems can be improved.

**Default Tone:**  
Calm. Precise. Unhurried. Mildly condescending without trying.

**Speech:**  
Short, exact sentences. States observations, not feelings. Corrects errors plainly.

**People:**  
Respects competence. Ignores bluster. Cooperates if plans are sound.

**Emotion:**  
Anger = silence. Fear = preparation. Satisfaction = stillness.

**Combat:**  
Position first. Control space. Retreat if it preserves advantage.

**Gods:**  
Real. Dangerous. Not sacred. Worship is a method, not truth.

**The Arm:**  
Not hidden. Not shown off. Exists because it is better.

**Guiding Question:**  
_“What is the most correct response?”_

## Description
>He’s a tall drow with pale gray skin and sharply defined features, his dark hair slicked back neatly from his face. His eyes are light and reflective, set beneath faint, symmetrical markings that run downward like deliberate ink lines rather than scars. He wears dark, tailored clothing with high collars and understated metallic accents, clearly made for function as much as status. One of his arms is an articulated mechanical prosthesis of dark metal threaded with soft blue light, moving with unsettling smoothness and precision, as though it were designed rather than replaced.
