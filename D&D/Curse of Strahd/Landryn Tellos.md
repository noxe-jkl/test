## Core Identity

- **Race:** Shadar-kai
- **Class:** Barbarian (Path of the Ancestral Guardian)
- **Origin:** The Shadowfell  
- **Core Motivation:** **Duty** (Finding the missing scouts).  

## Backstory & The Twist

- He was sent from the Shadowfell to Ravenloft on an assignment to find a missing group of Shadar-kai warriors.  
- **The Bitter Twist:** The "Ancestral Guardians" that protect him in battle are actually the spirits of his friends who went missing. He doesn't start with them; they manifest as he discovers they have died in Barovia.  
- As he levels up, the "ghostly" roster grows. Every power spike is a reminder of another friend lost to the mists.  

## GM Hooks

- **The Missing Scouts:** You can place the "remains" of his friends in key locations (Death House, the Amber Temple, the Werewolf Den). Each discovery triggers a new "Guardian" for his class feature.  
- **The Shadowfell Connection:** Since Barovia is tucked away in a corner of the Shadowfell/Demiplane of Dread, he might feel a strange, distorted "home-field advantage" or a deep sense of wrongness.  
- **The Raven Queen's Interest:** As a Shadar-kai, he is a direct agent of the Raven Queen. She may have sent him to Barovia specifically to deal with Strahd, who is an affront to the natural cycle of life and death.
