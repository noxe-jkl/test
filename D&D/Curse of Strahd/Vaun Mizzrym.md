## Core Identity

- **Name:** Zamon’vaun Mizzrym (Vaun)
- **Race:** Drow (Dhampir, but doesn't know it)
- **Class:** Blood Hunter
- **Background:** Noble (House Mizzrym)  
- **Core Motivation:** **Justice** or **Faith** (serving the Raven Queen and seeking redemption through action).  

## Backstory & Personality

- **The Ignored Son:** As the second boy of House Mizzrym with no magical talent, Vaun lived in the shadows of his prominent siblings: Pharaun (a mage) and Greyanna (a prickly high priestess). He learned to stay neutral during their constant infighting.  
- **The Experiment:** His Blood Hunter abilities are not natural; he was a test subject for Pharaun. He was forced to drink an elixir of draegloth blood and illithid ichor, which transformed him into a "monstrous" dhampir/blood hunter.  
- **The Exile:** During a training spar, Vaun was overcome by bloodlust and nearly murdered an heiress of House Baenre. He was spared execution only at her request, but was exiled to the surface—a fate intended to be slower and crueler than death.  

## Regrets & Motivations

- **The Death of Sabal:** Vaun witnessed Greyanna poisoning her twin and rival, Sabal. He purposefully delayed telling Pharaun until it was too late to intervene, believing it "wasn't his place" to step in.  
- **Current Drive:** The guilt of his past passivity has turned him into a man of action. He now strives to save lives and act decisively, refusing to let evil happen simply because he is "staying out of it."  

## Social Dynamics

- **Matriarchal Conditioning:** Despite his exile, he still holds a deep-seated respect for female magic-wielders (like Triss).  
- **The Noble Exile:** He is intimately familiar with drow politics and high-ranking figures, though they likely view him as a failed experiment or a dead man.  

## GM Hooks

- **The Baenre Heiress:** Who was she, and why did she spare him? Her influence might reach further than Vaun realizes, or she may have planted a "seed" of debt she intends to collect.  
- **Sibling Rivalry:** Pharaun and Greyanna are powerful drow. If word reaches Menzoberranzan that their "failed experiment" is hunting monsters in Barovia, they might send agents to retrieve their property or tie up loose ends.  
- **Decisiveness vs. Bloodlust:** The ichor in his veins makes him dangerous when he acts. Contrast his desire to "save lives" with the "maddening bloodlust" that got him exiled.
