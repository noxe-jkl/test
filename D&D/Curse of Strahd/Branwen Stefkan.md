## Core Identity

- **Race:** Vistani (Wood Elf)
- **Class:** Bard (Mechanically), but flavored as a **Warlock** with a secret patron.
- **Background:** Acolyte
- **Core Motivation:** **Family** (Masked as Ambition/Power).
- **Secret Patron:** **Strahd von Zarovich** ("The Master of Birds").

## Backstory & Secret Role

- **The Vistani Spy:** Branwen is not a stranger to Barovia; she was born there. Her mother, Aranrhod, was a famed servant of Strahd who lured adventurers to their doom.
- **The Deal:** Her mother is dying of a "psychic illness." Branwen made a pact with Strahd: Bring him "the ones who shine brightest" (the party) to satisfy his boredom, and he will grant her mother "peace" and make Branwen famous.
- **The Mask:** She plays the part of a naive, ambitious "Talent Scout" or "Seer." She uses music to harness emotions and lure the party toward the mists.  

## Lore & Imagery

- **Raven Imagery:** Her name means "Raven." Strahd calls her "Little Bird."  
- **The Song:** "Long Road After Death Row" — a haunting melody she uses to "mark" her targets.  
- **The Trinket:** A gaudy locket (provided by her father, Cadfan) which she believes is blessed, but likely acts as a conduit for her patron.  

## GM Hooks

- **The "Peace" Clause:** Strahd's promise of "peace" for her mother is almost certainly a double entendre for death/undeath.  
- **Redemption Arc:** She is set up for a massive fall when she realizes the party offers the genuine family she never had.  
- **The Evil Eye:** As a Vistana, she has unique magic. Ensure she knows when/how to use her Curse/Evil Eye abilities discreetly.
