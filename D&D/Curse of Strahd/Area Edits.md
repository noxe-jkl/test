# Area C: The Svalich Woods

### The Atmosphere

_Read the following as the players enter the woods:_

> Towering trees, whose tops are lost in heavy gray mist, block out all but a death-gray light. The tree trunks are unnaturally close to one another, and the woods have the silence of a forgotten grave, yet exude the feeling of an unvoiced scream.

### The Discovery

_If the characters are following the road on foot, the party member who has the highest **passive Wisdom (Perception)** score notices a change in the air:_

> The damp, earthy smell of the woods is suddenly broken. You catch the sharp, cloying scent of death on the air.

_If they follow the stench to its source, read:_

> The foul scent leads you to a human corpse half-buried in the underbrush about fifteen feet from the road. The young man appears to be a commoner. His muddy clothes are torn and shredded, as if he had been sprinting through thick brambles and thorns.
> 
> Oddly, there are no visible wounds on his body—no blood, no claw marks, and no signs of a struggle. His face is frozen in a mask of pure, crystalline exhaustion. In one hand, he clutches an old, tarnished copper compass. Even though the man is dead and his hand is still, the compass needle trembles violently.

**DM Note:** * **Medicine Check:** A successful **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine)** check confirms the man, Dalvan Olensky, died of pure physical exhaustion.

- **The Compass:** If a player holds the compass, the needle continues to tremble and spin erratically. This is because there is no "magnetic north" within the Mists.
    
- **The Clue:** Dalvan’s hand is splayed out, pointing toward a nearby tree.
    

---

### The First Tree

_When the players inspect the tree Dalvan is pointing toward, read:_

> The bark of this gnarled tree has been hacked away to create thirteen horizontal tally marks. Below them, a crude arrow has been carved, pointing deeper into the woods along what appears to be a well-traveled footpath.

---

### Following the Path: The Second Tree

_If the players follow the footpath in the direction of the arrow, read:_

> The path leads you deeper into the gloom. You soon encounter a second tree. Like the first, it bears thirteen tally marks and an arrow pointing further along the trail.
> 
> Near the base of the trunk lies the carcass of a horse. Its coat is matted and its frame is skeletal; it appears to be in a similar state of decay to the young man back on the road.

---

### The Third Tree and the Mist

_As the players continue to follow the arrows, read:_

> A third tree appears, marked with the same thirteen tallies and a pointing arrow. However, just past this tree, the footpath vanishes into a wall of impenetrable, roiling white fog.
> 
> If you hold Dalvan’s copper compass now, the needle no longer trembles—it spins wildly in a blurred circle.

**DM Note (The Mists):** If the players enter the fog, they are within the Mists of Barovia.

- **Travel Time:** They wander for 2d4 minutes.
    
- **The Toll:** Each player must make a **DC 5 Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, they take **1 level of exhaustion**.
    
- **Escalation:** If they turn back and try again, the DC increases by 5 each time. It resets after a long rest.
    

---

### The Fourth Tree: The Loop

_After the players pass through the fog, they emerge into an unfamiliar portion of the woods. Read:_

> You emerge from the choking mist, your lungs burning and your limbs heavy. Ahead stands a fourth tree. It bears the thirteen tally marks, but here, the bark tells a darker story.
> 
> An old dagger has been driven deep into the trunk. Beside it is a rough carving of a skeletal figure atop a horse. Below the image, two phrases have been hacked into the wood:
> 
> **"THE HORSEMAN RIDES."** **"THE SEER SPOKE TRUE."**

**DM Note:** If the players follow the arrow from this fourth tree, the path eventually crosses the Svalich Road and leads them directly back to Dalvan’s corpse.

**The Disappearance:** If the players leave the area and return later, both Dalvan’s body and the horse’s remains have vanished completely.s
