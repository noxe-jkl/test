# Castle Ravenloft: The Final Assault

**Estimated Playtime:** 7 Hours (Two 3.5-hour sessions) **Party Level:** 8 (Milestone to 9 at end of Session 1, Milestone to 10 before final boss) **Prerequisites:** Party has the Sunsword, Ezmerelda d'Avenir, and just lit the Beacon of Argynvostholt.

## The Setup & Ezmerelda's Strategy

Because the party lit the beacon, Strahd knows they are coming. The castle is on high alert. Ezmerelda has scouted the perimeter and proposes a highly tactical route.

**Ezmerelda's Briefing:** _"Strahd knows we're coming. The main grand staircase is a death trap, and he has the high ground. We will enter through the front, bypass the grand landing by taking the South Tower stairs, raid his accountant's office for a key to his secret treasury, and then hit him in the Audience Hall from the side. Stay quiet, stay fast, and don't touch anything I don't tell you to."_

# Session 1: The Ascent & The Chamberlain (3.5 Hours)

## 1. The Entryway (Areas K1, K7, K8)

**SCENE CHANGE:** Ravenloft

The party approaches the castle. The drawbridge is down, but the weather is worsening into a violent storm.

**Area K1. Front Courtyard**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** 90-foot-high walls enclose the courtyard. It is dark, lit only by sporadic lightning and the warm light spilling from the open keep doors.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "Thick, cold fog swirls in this courtyard. Dismal rain falls in a torrent. Sporadic flashes of lightning lance the weeping clouds overhead as thunder shakes the ground. Through the drizzle, you see torch flames fluttering on each side of the keep's open main doors. Warm light spills out of the entrance, flooding the courtyard. High above the entrance is a round window with shards of broken glass lodged in its iron frame."

**Area K7. Entry**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Bright light from torches. Vaulted ceiling.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "The ornate outer doors of the castle hang open, flanked by fluttering torches in iron sconces. Twenty feet inside the castle is a second set of doors... As you approach, the doors in front of you suddenly swing open, revealing a grand hall filled with the sound of organ music. Overhead, in the vaulted entry foyer, four statues of dragons glare down, their eyes flickering in the torchlight."

- **DM Note:** Ezmerelda urgently whispers to the party _not_ to look up or linger, as the four dragon statues are actually **Red Dragon Wyrmlings**. If they keep moving forward into K8, the dragons remain stone.
    

**Area K8. Great Entry & The Gargoyle Ambush**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Dim light from sputtering torches. Domed ceiling.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "Cobwebs stretch between the columns that support the vaulted ceiling of a great, dusty hall dimly lit by sputtering torches in iron sconces. The torches cast odd shadows across the faces of eight stone gargoyles squatting motionlessly on the rim of the domed ceiling. Cracked and faded ceiling frescoes are covered by decay. Double doors of bronze stand closed to the east. To the north, a wide staircase climbs into darkness. A lit hallway to the south contains another set of bronze doors, through which you hear sad and majestic organ tones."

**The Encounter:** As they cross the room, the turbulence of the storm blows the doors to the courtyard completely shut. The **8 Gargoyles** (Statblock: _Monster Manual_) animate and swoop down.

- _Combat Tactics:_ The gargoyles use the turbulence from their wings to extinguish the feeble torches in the sconces, plunging the hall into darkness for characters without darkvision. Ezmerelda fights alongside the party using her silvered weapons.
    

## 2. The Illusory Host & Triss's Wound (Area K9 & K10)

After the gargoyles are reduced to rubble, Ezmerelda points to the southern hallway (K9).

**Area K9. Guests' Hall**

- **Read Aloud:** > "Torchlight flutters against the walls of this vaulted hall. A suit of armor, oiled and glistening, stands at attention in a shallow alcove. To the west, large double doors hang slightly open, and a steady bright light escapes through the opening. Swells of organ music come from behind the doors, spilling their melody of power and defeat into the hall."
    
- **DM Note:** The armor is non-magical and harmless.
    

**Area K10. Dining Hall**

**SCENE CHANGE:** Ravenloft Dining Hall

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Brilliant bright light from crystal chandeliers.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "Three enormous crystal chandeliers brilliantly illuminate this magnificent chamber. Pillars of stone stand against dull white marble walls, supporting the ceiling. In the center of the room, a long, heavy table is covered with a fine white satin cloth. The table is laden with delectable foods: roasted beast basted in a savory sauce, roots, sweet fruits, and vegetables. Places are set with fine china and silver, each with a crystal goblet filled with an amber liquid with a tantalizing fragrance.
> 
> At the center of the far west wall, between floor-to-ceiling mirrors, stands a massive organ. Its pipes blare out a thunderous melody that speaks of greatness and despair. Seated at the organ, facing away from you, a single caped figure pounds the keys in raptured ecstasy. The figure suddenly stops, and as a deep silence falls over the dining hall, it slowly turns toward you."

**What Happens:** This is an illusion of Strahd. He applauds slowly and acts the gracious host. _"You light the beacon of a dead dragon, thinking you bring hope to my valley? You bring only your own doom. Eat, drink, for tomorrow you join my crypts."_

- **Triss's Moment:** Before the illusion fades, Strahd's eyes lock onto **Triss**. The grand organ music twists, warping into a familiar, painful melody from her past. The illusion's face flickers, melting away the vampire's aristocratic features to reveal the face of the man she has been searching for—the former lover whose ring she still carries.
    

> _The illusion's voice twists into a cruel sneer, speaking directly into Triss's mind: "Still clutching that ring, Triss? Searching the world for a man you thought you had merely lost? I took him. I broke him. I drank him dry. And then I plucked him from your mind so you wouldn't even have the comfort of mourning him properly. You're chasing a ghost I left behind."_

- _Action:_ After this psychological blow, the illusion vanishes with a mocking laugh.
    
- _Atmospheric Shift:_ The moment he disappears, a fierce, bone-chilling wind rises up and roars through the hall, putting out all open flames. The characters hear the screech of ancient hinges and the solid thud of many heavy doors slamming shut into the distance. All torches in K7, K8, and K9 have gone out.
    
- _Ezmerelda's cue:_ _"Mind games. Don't let him get into your head. Come on, the South Tower stairs are just outside."_
    

## 3. The King's Accountant (Area K21 to K30)

**SCENE CHANGE:** Ravenloft

They backtrack into K9 and find the dark spiral stairs to the east.

**Area K21. South Tower Stair**

- **Read Aloud:** > "Fluttering torches in iron sconces illuminate this spiral staircase. A chill wind rushes down the circling stairway, seeming to kill the very heat of the torches."
    
- **DM Note:** They climb the stairs (past K61 and K9) and emerge on the next level up into Area K30.
    

**Area K30. King's Accountant**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Unlit except for the party's light sources.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "Dusty scrolls and tomes line the walls of this room. More scrolls and books lie scattered on the floor, around four heavy wooden chests fitted with sturdy iron locks. The only unobstructed floor space is directly in front of the doors on the east and west walls.
> 
> In the center of this clutter stands a great black desk. A figure crouches atop a tall stool, scratching on a seemingly endless scroll of paper with a dry quill pen. Nearby a tasseled rope hangs from a hole in the ceiling."

**What Happens:** This is **Lief Lipsiege** (CE human commoner, Statblock: _Monster Manual_), Strahd's accountant. He is chained to his desk and is incredibly grumpy.

- _Interaction:_ Lief has no interest in the characters. If threatened, he will pull the tasseled rope, which sounds a massive gong and summons 1d4 Vampire Spawn or Wraiths. Ezmerelda warns against threatening him.
    
- _Bribery/Persuasion:_ If treated kindly and told they are here to kill Strahd, Lief admits he hates the vampire because Strahd hides his treasures from him. He tells them the key to the four chests in the room is hidden in a hollowed-out book with a bloodstained leather cover. He also warns them: _"The Chamberlain is pacing in the King's Hall. He knows you are here."_
    
- _Looting the Chests:_ Once the party finds the hollowed-out book (Investigation DC 15 or Lief tells them), they can open the four chests.
    
    - Chest 1 & 2: 10,000 cp each.
        
    - Chest 3: 1,000 gp.
        
    - Chest 4: 500 pp, an **Amulet of Health** (instant CON boost for the final fight), a **Necklace of Fireballs** (4 beads), and a **Cloak of Protection**.
        

## 4. Midpoint Boss: The Chamberlain & Vaun's Test (Area K27)

They exit west through the door in K30, passing through a brief corridor (K25) and heading north into the King's Hall (K27) to make their way to the Throne Room.

**Area K27. King's Hall**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** 20-foot-high vaulted ceiling. Total darkness.
    
- **Read Aloud (Entering the Hall):**
    

> "This twenty-foot-high hall has a dark, vaulted ceiling draped with cobwebs. A low moan seems to travel the length of the corridor as it rises and falls, intoning sadness and despair. The moaning seems to be only the wind..."

**What Happens (The Ambush):**

When the party reaches the midpoint of the hall, trigger the ceiling trap. **Read the following script verbatim to execute the ambush:**

- **DM Script (Read Aloud):**
    

> _"Suddenly, you hear a scraping sound of stone against stone from above, followed by the squeaking of rusty pulleys. Out of the darkness, the fanged visage, outstretched claws, and flapping black cape of a vampire bears down on you from the ceiling! A deep, throaty chuckle fills the hall._
> 
> _(Pause for a brief second to let them panic, then continue)_
> 
> _But as the figure flies past, you realize it's moving on a rope—a wooden mannequin dressed as Strahd. As your eyes follow the dummy gliding into the shadows, a cold, elegant voice echoes from the darkness behind you._
> 
> _'My master's throne room is beyond,' the voice says. A dusk elf with cold, dead eyes steps from the shadows, drawing a polished, wicked-looking scimitar. The air around him screams with the psychic wails of a thousand dying souls. He turns his black gaze directly to **Vaun**, taking in his unnaturally bright eyes and the alchemical scars carved into his flesh._
> 
> _'My master hears all the little secrets whispered in his valley,' the elf sneers. 'A boy from the Underdark, desperate to win back a family that threw him away like garbage. Do you truly believe claiming my master's head will restore your honor? That your Matron Mother will ever welcome back a failed experiment? Someone who barely deserves a Drow name anymore?'_
> 
> _Rahadin steps forward, his blade humming. 'You claim your life's purpose is duty. But duty requires the strength to act, to sacrifice! I slaughtered the women of my own race for my duty. But you? You just stood back and watched your own sister be poisoned because you were too cowardly to intervene. Let us see if that tainted blood of yours has finally found its courage!'"_

1. **The Distraction:** The wooden mannequin flies across the room on Initiative count 5.
    
2. **The True Threat:** The dusk elf is **Rahadin** (Statblock: _Curse of Strahd App. D_). He is accompanied by two **Shadow Demons** (Statblock: _Monster Manual_) that use the dummy as a distraction to ambush the party.
    

**Combat Tactics:** * Characters with a Passive Perception less than Rahadin's Stealth check are surprised.

- **Vaun's Challenge:** Rahadin's psychic wail (_Deathly Choir_ aura) is specifically flavored as the screams of the dusk elf women he murdered. This perfectly parallels the matriarchal society Vaun comes from. Rahadin directly attacks Vaun's core delusion—that a grand victory will make his abusive family love him again. Rahadin's taunts (fed to him by Strahd's scrying, or perhaps by Branwen's spy work) force Vaun to realize he must fight _now_, not for a family that mutilated him, but to protect the people actually standing beside him.
    
- The Shadow Demons target the backline casters, popping in and out of the total darkness.
    
- Rahadin uses his _Deathly Choir_ aura (10ft radius, DC 16 Wis save or 3d10 psychic damage) to shred melee fighters while making three scimitar attacks. He fights to the death to protect his master.
    

**End of Session 1:** Upon defeating Rahadin, the party reaches **LEVEL 9**. They may take a Short Rest here. (Loot Rahadin for a **Scimitar of Wounding**, a **Ring of Free Action**, and his **Boots of Elvenkind**).


# Session 2: The Treasury & The Vampire (3.5 Hours)

## 5. The Secret Treasury & Landryn's Guardians (Areas K31b, K39, K40, K41)

Ezmerelda stops them before they charge into the Audience Hall. _"Strahd is waiting in the throne room. We need more firepower. There is a secret treasury hidden above the belfry."_

**The Path:** Halfway down the hall of K27 on the south side is a narrow secret door. Ezmerelda points it out. They pull it open into **K31b (Shaft Access)**.

- **Read Aloud (K31b):** > "This ten-foot-square room overlooks a vertical shaft to the south that plunges into darkness and continues upward. You can feel a cold draft rushing from below."
    
- They bypass the shaft and take the door in the north wall into **K39 (Hall of Webs)**.
    

**Area K39. Hall of Webs**

- **Read Aloud:** > "This ancient hall is choked with spider webs broken by a single clear path down its center. The arched ceiling twenty feet overhead is completely hidden behind thick webbing."
    
- **DM Note:** Characters who stray from the clear path become restrained by the webs. They proceed east through arched bronze doors into K40.
    

**Area K40. Belfry & The Lost Scouts**

- **Read Aloud:** > "You can hear the rain and thunder outside, and the air here is cold and damp. Veils and curtains of webbing fill the room, making it hard to gauge its width and depth. A single, narrow path leads to the dark center of the room, where a thick rope dangles from high above."
    
- **Landryn's Moment:** As the party steps into the room, **Landryn** notices something horrific in the periphery. Suspended in the thickest webs are several desiccated corpses. Recognizing the spiked armor and the pale, tattooed skin beneath the webbing, Landryn realizes the grim truth: these are the missing Shadar-kai warriors he was sent to find. They were drained dry by the horrors of this castle.
    

> _As Landryn touches the webbing, a cold, shadowy energy surges from the corpses into him. The spirits of his fallen friends detach from their physical remains, their ghostly forms nodding to him in silent, somber duty. The Ancestral Guardians are now fully awakened, ready to finish their mission through him._

- **The Encounter:** The disturbance of the webs (or pulling the bell rope) summons five **Giant Spiders** (Statblock: _Monster Manual_) that drop from the ceiling to defend their larder. A quick, brutal fight, giving Landryn a chance to test his newly empowered guardians.
    

**Area K41. Treasury**

**SCENE CHANGE:** Ravenloft Treasury (Original / Alt)

They clear the webs at the west end of the north wall of K40 to find a secret door opening into K41.

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Dimly lit by crystal "stars".
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "This octagonal vault is free of dust and cobwebs. The domed ceiling forty feet above is painted black and coated in dry pitch. Embedded in the pitch are glowing shards of crystal that sparkle with a display of stars in unfamiliar constellations.
> 
> Barely contained within this vault is a square tower, twenty feet on a side and thirty feet high, with arrow slits on all sides and a battlemented roof. It is constructed entirely of seamless, black adamantine."

**What Happens:** This black adamantine tower is a _Daern's Instant Fortress_. Only Strahd knows the command word. However, Ezmerelda reveals she stole the command word from the Tome of Strahd (or extracted it from Lief). She speaks the word, and the sealed adamantine door at the base opens.

- **Epic Loot Drop:** Inside the ground floor, they find a hoard: 50,000 cp, 10,000 sp, 10,000 gp, 1,000 pp, and a **+2 Shield** emblazoned with a stylized silver dragon (the emblem of Argynvost).
    
- **Upper Floor Loot:** Up the internal ladder, they hit the jackpot.
    
    - A velvet sack with 10 pieces of jewelry (250 gp each).
        
    - A **Mace of Disruption** and **5x Arrows of Undead Slaying**.
        
    - A **Helm of Brilliance**.
        
    - A **Rod of the Pact Keeper +1** (or Wand of the War Mage +1).
        
    - A **Cape of the Mountebank** (perfect for escaping or chasing Strahd).
        
    - An unlocked wooden coffer containing **4x Potions of Greater Healing**, **2x Potions of Speed**, and **2x Potions of Invulnerability**.
        
- **Level Up:** Discovering the secret hoard and gearing up pushes the party to **LEVEL 10**.
    

## 6. The Final Battle & Branwen's Fall (Area K25 - Audience Hall)

**SCENE CHANGE:** Ravenloft Audience Hall

Fully geared, buffed with potions, and ready for war, the party backtracks through the webs, through K27, and enters the double doors into the Audience Hall.

**Area K25. Audience Hall**

- **Dimensions & Lighting:** Immense hall. Dim light from the courtyard falling through the broken glass window.
    
- **Read Aloud:**
    

> "Dim light from the courtyard falls into this great hall through the broken glass and iron latticework of a massive window in the west wall. Rain lashes against the floor. This immense room is a place of chilly, brooding darkness. Empty iron sconces dot the walls. Hundreds of dust-laden cobwebs drape the hall, hiding the ceiling from view.
> 
> At the far southern end of the hall, a large wooden throne stands atop a marble dais. Seated upon it is the devil himself—Strahd von Zarovich. He slowly stands, his eyes burning like red coals in the gloom.
> 
> 'You have broken my servants,' his voice echoes over the thunder. 'You have raided my home. But you will not leave my valley.'"

**The Encounter:**

- **Enemies:** **Strahd von Zarovich** (Statblock: _CoS App. D_). He is joined by 3 **Vampire Spawn** (Statblock: _Monster Manual_) clinging to the ceiling in the shadows, waiting for his command to drop.
    
- **Branwen's Moment:** Before initiative is rolled, Strahd looks past the party's weapons and locks eyes with **Branwen**.
    

> _"Ah, my Little Bird," Strahd says, a cruel smile touching his lips. "You have kept your end of our bargain. You brought me the ones who shine brightest. And as promised, I have granted your mother eternal peace."_ _Strahd gestures to the ceiling. One of the pale, monstrous vampire spawn drops from the shadows, landing with a sickening thud. As it rises, baring its fangs, Branwen recognizes the gaunt, twisted features. It is her mother, Aranrhod. Strahd's "peace" was the curse of undeath. The spy's game is over; Branwen must choose where her true family lies._

- **Strahd's Tactics:** * _Mobility is Key:_ Strahd does NOT stand still. He has 144 HP and will die quickly if cornered by a level 10 party with these magic items. He uses his Lair Action to phase through the walls into K26 (Guards' Post) or K13 (Turret Post Access Hall), regenerating health out of line-of-sight, and popping out to cast _Fireball_ or _Blight_ on squishy targets.
    
    - _The Sunsword:_ Strahd fears the sunlight from this weapon. He will command Aranrhod and the other Vampire Spawn to grapple and isolate the character holding it, pulling them into the dark corners of the room. He will attempt to use _Charm_ on weak-willed fighters to get them to turn off the blade.
        
    - _Ezmerelda:_ She will cast _Greater Invisibility_ on herself or the party's rogue, and focus on keeping the Vampire Spawn off the party so they can focus on Strahd.
        
- **The Finish:** When Strahd drops to 0 HP, his physical body collapses into mist. Because of your time constraints, you can alter the module here: as his body turns to mist, the radiant energy of the Sunsword flares, catching the mist in a vortex of sunlight and burning him to utter ash right there in the Audience Hall. The dark clouds outside immediately part, letting actual sunlight touch the castle for the first time in centuries.
    

## Appendix: Loot Handouts

_Grant these to your players as they progress so they are fully prepared for the final boss._

**From Lief's Chests (Area K30)**

- **Amulet of Health:** Requires Attunement. Your Constitution score is 19 while you wear this amulet.
    
- **Necklace of Fireballs:** Contains 4 beads. You can use an action to detach a bead and throw it up to 60 feet away. Each creature within a 20-foot radius sphere must make a DC 15 Dex save or take 8d6 fire damage (half on success).
    
- **Cloak of Protection:** Requires Attunement. Grants a +1 bonus to AC and Saving Throws.
    

**From Rahadin (Area K27)**

- **Scimitar of Wounding:** Requires Attunement. Hit points lost to this weapon's damage can be regained only through a short or long rest, rather than by regeneration, magic, or any other means. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack using this magic weapon, you can wound the target. At the start of each of the wounded creature's turns, it takes 1d4 necrotic damage for each time you've wounded it.
    
- **Ring of Free Action:** Requires Attunement. Magic can neither reduce your speed nor cause you to be paralyzed or restrained.
    
- **Boots of Elvenkind:** While worn, steps make no sound, regardless of the surface. Grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently.
    

**From the Treasury (Area K41)**

- **+2 Shield (Order of the Silver Dragon):** Grants a total of +4 to AC. The shield whispers warnings, granting a +2 bonus to initiative if the bearer isn't incapacitated.
    
- **Mace of Disruption:** Requires Attunement. When you hit a fiend or an undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 radiant damage. If the target has 25 hit points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed.
    
- **Arrows of Undead Slaying (x5):** If a creature belonging to the undead type takes damage from the arrow, the creature must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking an extra 6d10 piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much extra damage on a successful one.
    
- **Cape of the Mountebank:** Casts _Dimension Door_ as an action. Once used, cannot be used again until the next dawn.
    
- **Helm of Brilliance:** Requires Attunement. A crown of diamonds, rubies, fire opals, and opals. Grants fire damage to weapons, allows casting of _Daylight_, _Fireball_, _Prismatic Spray_, and _Wall of Fire_. Undead within 30 feet that start their turn taking radiant damage take an extra 1d6.
    
- **Rod of the Pact Keeper +1** (or _Wand of the War Mage +1_): Requires attunement. +1 to spell attack rolls and saving throw DCs.
    
- **Consumables:** * **4x Potions of Greater Healing:** Heals 4d4 + 4 HP.
    
    - **2x Potions of Speed:** Casts _Haste_ on the drinker for 1 minute (no concentration required).
        
    - **2x Potions of Invulnerability:** Grants resistance to _all_ damage for 1 minute.
