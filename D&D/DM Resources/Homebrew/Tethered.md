A human wakes up in an unfamiliar field, their mind a complete blank save for a lingering, echoing whisper in an alien tongue. Suddenly, a spectral tentacle bursts from the ground at their command, saving them from an approaching goblin.

An elf stares into a mirror, their pupils shifting into impossible geometric fractals as they read an ancient, encrypted tome without ever having studied the language.

A dwarf steps into a crowded tavern, suddenly feeling the weight of a celestial presence in their mind. They raise a hand, and a glowing light knits the wounds of a battered adventurer nearby.

The Tethered are individuals whose memories have been wiped completely clean, leaving behind an incredibly rare, perfectly empty slate. This absolute mental void makes their mind the perfect piece of cosmic real estate. Extraplanar entities—from fey lords and fiends to great old ones and cosmic anomalies—constantly buy, sell, and borrow the rights to inhabit the Tethered's mind. Through this ever-shifting contract, the Tethered gains access to bizarre abilities and potent utility magic, never quite knowing who will be holding their mental leash tomorrow.

### Creating a Tethered

When creating a Tethered, consider how your character feels about their amnesia. Are they desperately searching for clues to their past life, or have they embraced the chaotic, ever-changing nature of their present existence? How do they react when they wake up with the dark impulses of a vampire one day, and the rigid, unfeeling logic of a clockwork entity the next?

#### Quick Build

You can make a Tethered quickly by following these suggestions. First, Charisma should be your highest ability score, followed by Constitution or Dexterity. Second, choose the Hermit or Far Traveler background.

## Class Features

As a Tethered, you gain the following class features.

#### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per Tethered level **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per Tethered level after 1st

#### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor **Weapons:** Simple weapons **Tools:** None **Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma **Skills:** Choose two from Arcana, Deception, History, Insight, Investigation, and Religion

#### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- _(a)_ a light crossbow and 20 bolts or _(b)_ any simple weapon
- _(a)_ a component pouch or _(b)_ an arcane focus
- _(a)_ a scholar's pack or _(b)_ an explorer's pack
- Leather armor, any simple weapon, and two daggers

### Fractured Mind, Borrowed Magic

_1st-level Tethered feature_

You have no memory of your past, leaving your mind a blank slate that extraplanar entities find incredibly easy to write on. You do not know any standard cantrips. Instead, your magic is entirely fueled by your current Patron, shifting as your cosmic contract is bought, sold, or borrowed.

### The Planar Dial

_1st-level Tethered feature_

Whenever you finish a long rest, your current contract expires or is bought out. Roll a d20 to determine your Patron for the day. You gain that Patron's specific Utility Cantrips and Passive Feature until you finish your next long rest.

If a feature requires a saving throw, the DC is equal to your Tethered spell save DC.

### Resonance Casting

_1st-level Tethered feature_

Your spellcasting functions similarly to Pact Magic, but your mind can only hold utility spells. Your spell slots are always cast at the highest level available to you, and they recharge on a short or long rest.

#### Spell Slots

The Tethered table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st through 5th level. The table also shows what the level of those slots is; all of your spell slots are the same level. To cast one of your Tethered spells of 1st level or higher, you must expend a spell slot. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a short or long rest.

#### The Blind List (Spells Prepared)

You do not learn a set list of "Spells Known." Instead, you have access to the entire **Planar Utility Spell List**, which consists of any spell that **does not deal direct damage**, drawn from the Warlock, Wizard, or Cleric spell lists.

Whenever you finish a long rest, you choose a number of utility spells from those lists equal to your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier (minimum of one spell). Those chosen spells are the only ones your brain can "remember" and cast for the day. A spell you choose must be of a level no higher than what's shown in the table's Slot Level column for your level.

#### Spellcasting Ability

Charisma is your spellcasting ability for your Tethered spells, so you use your Charisma whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Charisma modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a Tethered spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.

**Spell save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier **Spell attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

### Planar Sensory Organs

_2nd-level Tethered feature_

Your exposure to constant cosmic shifts permanently alters your physical senses. You gain two Planar Sensory Organs of your choice. These represent permanent scars and gifts left behind by entities who have used your brain.

When you reach certain Tethered levels, you gain additional organs of your choice, as shown in the Tethered table.

- **The Translation Matrix:** Your pupils shift into geometric shapes. You can read all writing, no matter the language or cipher.
    
- **Aura Sight:** You can cast _Detect Magic_ at will, without expending a spell slot.
    
- **Chronological Echoes:** You can cast _Identify_ at will. However, instead of just learning its properties, you get a hazy, psychic vision of the last creature who held it.
    
- **Whisper Eater:** You can cast _Detect Thoughts_ at will, but only to read surface thoughts.
    
- **Vibrational Attunement:** You gain tremorsense out to a range of 30 feet.
    
- **Third Eye:** You can cast _See Invisibility_ at will, without expending a spell slot.
    
- **Cosmic Echolocation:** You gain blindsight out to a range of 10 feet.
    
- **Ghost Gaze:** As an action, you can see through solid objects up to 30 feet away. You can use this ability for up to 1 minute per short or long rest.
    

### Residual Memory

_3rd-level Tethered feature_

Because your patrons are constantly cycling through your mind, they accidentally leave behind data scraps from their own vast libraries of cosmic knowledge.

When you finish a long rest and roll your Planar Dial to determine your patron for the day, you also gain proficiency in one Intelligence-based skill of your choice (_Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, or Religion_). This represents the unique database your current patron uses, and the proficiency lasts until your next long rest.

### Extraplanar Tether

_3rd-level Tethered feature_

Your connection to the planes stabilizes slightly. You can choose to lock your Planar Dial for up to 3 days instead of rolling every morning, keeping the exact same patron, cantrip, and passive feature.

However, resisting the natural flow of the cosmic market gives you a splitting headache; while your Dial is locked in this way, you have disadvantage on Intelligence (History) checks regarding anything related to the Material Plane.

### Ability Score Improvement

_4th-level Tethered feature_

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Blank Slate

_6th-level Tethered feature_

Your mind is so thoroughly fractured and heavily guarded by conflicting cosmic contracts that it is incredibly difficult for outside forces to manipulate you. You gain advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

### Ethereal Slip

_7th-level Tethered feature_

You learn how to momentarily slide through the cracks in reality left behind by your shifting patrons. Once per short or long rest, you can cast _Blink_ or _Gaseous Form_ without expending a spell slot.

### Echoes of the Multiverse

_10th-level Tethered feature_

You have hosted so many powerful beings that you know exactly how to ring their cosmic doorbells. You can cast _Contact Other Plane_ as a ritual. When you cast the spell in this way, you automatically succeed on the Intelligence saving throw, as your mind is already perfectly accustomed to the maddening whispers of the void.

### Planar Anchor

_11th-level Tethered feature_

Your tether to the planes deepens, granting you access to potent utility magic called a Planar Anchor. Choose one 6th-level utility spell (such as _True Seeing_, _Find the Path_, or _Word of Recall_) from the Warlock, Wizard, or Cleric spell lists.

You can cast your chosen anchor spell once without expending a spell slot. You must finish a long rest before you can do so again.

At higher levels, you gain more Planar Anchors of your choice that can be cast in this way: one 7th-level spell at 13th level (such as _Plane Shift_, _Etherealness_, or _Teleport_), one 8th-level spell at 15th level (such as _Antimagic Field_, _Mind Blank_, or _Maze_), and one 9th-level spell at 17th level (such as _Astral Projection_, _Foresight_, or _Gate_). You regain all uses of your Planar Anchors when you finish a long rest.

### Tether Snap

_14th-level Tethered feature_

When you are reduced to 0 hit points, your current patron forcibly yanks your soul out of harm's way to protect their investment. You immediately teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see and regain 1 hit point.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Cosmic Vessel

_18th-level Tethered feature_

Your physical body has become a perfectly reinforced container for extraplanar energy. When you roll your Planar Dial at the end of a long rest, you can choose to roll twice (rerolling duplicates). You manifest the cantrips and passive features of BOTH patrons simultaneously for the day.

### The Akashic State

_20th-level Tethered feature_

For a brief, terrifying moment, the dam breaks and you remember absolutely everything. As an action, you enter the Akashic State for 1 minute.

During this time, you know every single utility and planar spell in existence from 1st to 5th level across all spell lists. You can cast any of them as an action without expending a spell slot. Furthermore, you gain Truesight out to a range of 120 feet for the duration.

Once you use this feature, your mind violently purges the memories to save itself from overloading, and you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.
