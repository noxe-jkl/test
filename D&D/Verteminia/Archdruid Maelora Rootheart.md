Maelora is the Archdruid of **flora, earth, growth, and the mountains themselves**. Before the Long Winter, her followers maintained mountain roads, waystations, seed repositories, and sanctuaries throughout her territory. She viewed civilization and nature as things that could coexist, provided people respected the land they depended upon. Her symbol is an **open hand beneath a spreading leaf**, and green marks her followers and works.

A thousand years ago, Maelora and the Copper Archdruid discovered the Red Archdruid's true nature and his attempt to open a planar rift atop the mountains. The two enacted the desperate spell that froze the range and trapped all three Archdruids in magical stasis. With spring's sudden return, Maelora has awakened damaged and disoriented. Her fragmented memories and instinctive need to defend her territory make her dangerous even though she is ultimately one of the people who saved Verteminia.
