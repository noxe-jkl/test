# Session Prep
## 1. The Chase

Yseld warns the party that armed outsiders have pushed uphill. Alexis easily finds their trail, initiating a three-stage pursuit. Success closes the distance; failure costs resources or HP rather than halting progress.

**The Broken Crossing**

> The roar of whitewater fills the gorge ahead. A spring-fed river has swallowed the old mountain trail. Across the chasm, the shredded ends of hemp ropes dangle from an ancient piton. The Compass has crossed and cut the bridge behind them.

The party must improvise a crossing (Athletics, spells, or clever gear use). Failure results in minor bludgeoning damage from the rapids or lost equipment.

**The Thornwood**

> The vegetation thickens into a claustrophobic wall of green. The air smells of crushed sap and disturbed earth. Branches do not just grow here—they aggressively lean and weave across the uphill path, forming deliberate physical barricades.

Walthas recognizes that Green's domain is actively trying to block passage. The mountain is reacting to the intruders.

**The Abandoned Camp**

> A circle of flattened grass sits beneath massive pines, hurriedly abandoned. Discarded rations and botanical clippings litter the dirt.

The party finds a torn page from Sylvester's journal: _"Same structure again. Green masonry. The dreams were correct. There is something inside."_ 

## 2. The Celestial Statue (The Green Ward)

The pursuit breaks out of the treeline onto a high-altitude cliff edge.

> Before you stands a colossal statue gazing out over the sheer cliff edge. Carved directly from the mountain's living rock, the primeval figure's head is broken off at the jaw, but its remaining torso is draped in robes carved to resemble tightly woven vines and ancient bark. Its enormous, glowing hands rest flat against a sealed stone door at the statue's base. Carved into the door is an open hand beneath a leaf, and below that, a single thorn.

The Gilded Compass is here. Corvus and Jaspen linger near the back, allowing a brief parley before combat erupts.

- **Corvus (to Alexis):** Bitter and hostile. _"Of course. Of all the people who could have found me. You."_
    
- **Jaspen (to Walthas):** Condescending. _"Ah, the storyteller. Someone should document this for the taverns."_
    
- **Sylvester (to Tourmaline):** Flawlessly operating the ancient glowing hands of the statue. He is delighted to see her, completely oblivious to her hatred.
    
- **Kaelen (to Votrean):** Disgusted by Votrean’s reckless soul research.
    
- **Helga:** Slowly realizing she works for terrible people.
    

## 3. Combat & The Escape

**The Trigger:** Bart pries a stone mechanism loose. The cliff edge shudders, the statue's glowing hands flicker, and massive roots erupt from the stone floor as the Green Ward violently reacts. Sylvester shouts, _"That's our opening! Inside!"_

The Compass initially fights non-lethally to cover Sylvester. Sylvester and Bart spend Rounds 1 and 2 interacting with the door, leaving the PCs to fight Corvus, Jaspen, Kaelen, and Helga.

**The Escape (Round 3):** The heavy stone door grinds open, revealing an ancient vertical passage climbing up through the hollow center of the colossal statue. Realizing this is exactly what his dreams promised, Sylvester shouts, _"Jaspen! With me!"_

Jaspen abandons the fight to secure the ancient druidic knowledge inside. They rush into the dark vertical shaft. A moment later, the damaged Green mechanism violently snaps shut, slamming an impenetrable wall of thick roots across the entrance.

## 4. The Red Thread

# Victor's Dream:

_(Read this to Victor's player when the party takes a long or short rest near the sealed door, or at the start of the next session)_

The fires that usually accompany your dreams don't come tonight...

Instead, you feel the biting chill of the high mountain air, and smell the sharp, unmistakable metallic scent of fresh blood.

You open your eyes. You are standing on the stone terrace, high above the clouds. The sky isn't blue, or the black of night. It is a deep, bruised crimson.

There is someone standing a few feet ahead of you, right in front of the sealed stone door.

At first, you think it’s just the shadowed silhouette you've seen before. But as the figure shifts, you recognize the slump of the shoulders. The clothes. You recognize the catastrophic, fatal wound you put there just hours ago.

It's Bart. Or at least, something wearing his dead shape.

The thing wearing Bart doesn't speak. It steps right up to the stone.

It completely ignores the large, prominent carvings of the open hand and the thorn in the center of the door. Instead, its hands move to two small, easily overlooked glyphs flanking the centerpiece—one on the far left, and one on the far right.

The figure raises its left hand—slick with thick, dark blood—and presses it flat against the left glyph. It pushes inward. The stone gives way with a sickening, wet slide, sounding exactly like a blade sinking deep into flesh.

Then, it reaches over and grips the right glyph. It twists it violently clockwise. A sharp _CRACK_ echoes across the terrace, sounding exactly like snapping bone.

A searing red light bleeds from beneath the two depressed glyphs. The heavy stone door shudders, the writhing roots recoil and wither into ash, and the passage grinds open.

The figure steps onto the dark threshold. It pauses, slowly turning its head back toward you.

It has Bart's pale, dead face. But his eyes are hollow, burning pits of red embers.

From Bart's lifeless lips, you hear it.

_Heh._

It is the exact same quiet, echoing chuckle you heard on the mountain earlier today.

And then, your eyes snap open. You are awake.
