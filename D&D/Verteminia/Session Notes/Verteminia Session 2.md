# Session Prep
> "The first full sunrise of the Thaw breaks over the Wintergate Peaks not as a gentle dawn, but in a sudden flood of amber warmth that feels completely alien. All around your narrow, wind-scoured ledge, the mountain is in violent motion."
> "Roaring chutes of meltwater cascade down sheer basalt faces that have been locked in blue glacial ice for eleven centuries. The air smells sharply of damp pine needles, wet loam, and mineral dust waking from a thousand-year sleep. Below you, the trails back toward Sumistra are already disappearing beneath roaring torrents of slush and rockfall. Looking northeast, the colossal, mist-veiled silhouette of Mountain 1 towers against the horizon—a silent giant easily three to four weeks of brutal trekking through unmapped wilderness."
- Slush Chute AND/OR Ravine
	- Harvest $2\times$ bundles of Sun-Thaw Moss (which can be brewed into a tea during a short rest to grant $+1\text{d}4$ temporary hit points).
	- Identifying residual traces of ancient primordial druid magic pulsing through the meltwater streams, confirming that the Great Thaw is not purely seasonal, but the unraveling of a continent-wide enchantment.
	- Tourmaline (Cartographer's Tools / Perception): Charting newly exposed landmarks, ridge lines, and natural water channels to begin drafting the first true map of the post-winter frontier.
	- Encounter: Lair of the Silt-Water Mephits
		- The sudden surge of ancient meltwater and mineral dust has birthed minor elemental scavengers nesting inside the shelter.
		- Enemies: $3\times$ Mud Mephits (CR $1/4$ each) and $1\times$ Thaw-Silt Elemental (reflavored Ice/Dust Mephit with water traits, CR $1/2$).
		- Tactics: They erupt from the silted floor drains, attempting to blind characters with mud breath and push intruders back out into the freezing wind.
		- XP Budget: $200\text{ XP}$ total (an engaging, low-threat skirmish for five Level 2 PCs to stretch their combat abilities and establish team synergy).
