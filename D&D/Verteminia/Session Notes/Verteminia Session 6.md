# Session Prep
## Session 6 Overview: The First Seal

The campaign shifts from "weird ancient things in the mountains" to "a specific event occurred 1,000 years ago, and someone is disturbing it."
- **The Core Objective:** Reveal the concept of the "Seal," establish what Sylvester stole, and introduce Green’s lieutenant  
- **Do Not Reveal Everything:** Withhold the full containment story
- **Do Not Chase Sylvester Yet:** He is already gone.

## What Was Stolen: The Verdant Nail

- **Physical Description:** An 18-inch spike of petrified black wood, wrapped in living green roots.
- **Lore/Function:** One of three major ritual implements used by Maelora during the Long Winter to anchor the Material Plane around the Three Masters, stopping Vashara's planar wound from spreading.
- **The Three Implements:**
    - **Green:** The Verdant Nail (Anchor)
    - **Copper:** The Copper Bell (Prevented souls from crossing the rift)  
    - **Vashara (Red):** The third component (To be developed later)
- **Sylvester's Motive:** His patron told him where to find an extremely powerful Archdruid relic. He believes he just made the discovery of the century and has no idea what he actually unleashed.

## Scene 1 — Through the Door

Viktor knows how to reopen the passage from his dream (pressing and twisting the glyphs bypasses the Green mechanism; no literal blood/bone required).

> **Read Aloud:** The last of the roots withdraws with a sound like branches dragged across stone. Beyond them, the passage climbs steeply through the hollow interior of the ancient statue.

**Tracking Sylvester:** Let the party find evidence that Sylvester was in a frantic hurry _leaving_:

- Muddy footprints.
- Hacked and broken roots higher up the stairs.
- Emerald sap bleeding down the stone steps.
- Discarded gear (a piton, torn piece of Jaspen's cloak, broken brass surveyor's instrument).
- **Sensory cue:** Somewhere far above, something enormous moves.

## Scene 2 — The Empty Reliquary

Communicate immediately that the party is too late.

> **Read Aloud:** The stairway opens into a circular chamber carved from seamless green stone. Roots as thick as your arms converge upon a pedestal at its center, wrapping around it like the fingers of a closed hand. Except the hand has been forced open. The roots have been cut. The pedestal is empty. Around it, a ring of symbols glows weakly in the floor—green light sputtering through cracks where something long and narrow once rested. Carved into the stone beneath the empty space is a single word: **ANCHOR.**

**Keeper Affinity Check (Walthas):**

- Walthas instinctively recognizes Maelora's imagery.
- _DM Note to Ani (Walthas):_ "You don't recognize this room. But your body does. You know, with absolute certainty, that something is missing."

## Scene 3 — Meet Green's Lieutenant

**Brontide, the Rootbound Sentinel**

- **Concept:** A walking tree that views humanoids as an unfortunate administrative problem (heavy contrast to Caperkin). Mechanically based on a Treant, but **not** a combat encounter for a level 3 party.
- **Appearance:** 14–16 feet tall, granite embedded in bark, moss beard, dim green glowing eyes, huge split down his trunk held together by ancient metal bands.
- **Current State:** Asleep for 1,000 years. Woke up because Sylvester removed the Verdant Nail. 
- **Initial Assumption:** The party standing in the empty room stole the relic.

> **Read Aloud:** A noise like timber splitting under enormous weight rolls through the chamber. One of the walls moves. What you mistook for roots pulls itself free from the stone. An enormous wooden hand unfolds. Then an arm. Shoulders of bark and granite wrench themselves from the wall as centuries of moss cascade onto the floor. Two green lights open beneath a crown of twisted branches. The creature looks at the empty pedestal. Then at you. Its voice sounds like a tree falling somewhere very far away. "Return the Anchor."

## Scene 4 — “We Didn't Take It”

Brontide knows the Anchor is gone, outsiders are in the ward, Maelora is awake but confused, and the impossible-to-bypass defenses were bypassed. **He does not know Vashara betrayed them.** His memory ends right before the ritual.

**Brontide's Fractured Memories:**

- _"The Keepers gathered."_
- _"Something had opened."_
- _"Green commanded the roots descend."_
- _"Copper called every wandering spirit home."_
- _"The Anchor was planted. Then came winter."_
- **If asked why:** _"To seal."_
- **If asked 'Seal what?':** _"I do not know."_ (Major Reveal: The Long Winter was deliberate).

## Scene 5 — Brontide's Memory & Mission

Once convinced of their innocence, Brontide explains Maelora's final explicit instructions before the freeze: _"While the Anchor holds, the Seal holds."_

**The Memory Hole:** Brontide remembers Maelora, Thelar, and the Third Keeper working together. He does _not_ remember the freeze itself.
- **Maelora's Final Warning to Him:** _"If I wake before the Nameless does, do not trust what I remember."_

**The Local Containment Failure:** With the Nail gone, Green's portion of the seal is failing. Over coming sessions:

- Roots grow unpredictably.
- Earthquakes increase.
- Ancient Green guardians awake.
- Maelora's memories bleed into Walthas
- Reality strains physically near Green's mountain.

**The Party's New Job:** Brontide is **Rootbound** (tied to Maelora's mountain and weakens if he leaves). He cannot pursue Sylvester. He also feels Maelora is awake but fragmented, and he needs Walthas to reach her.
