# Session Prep
Most importantly, Walthas recognizes plants here that he knows only from old stories and descriptions. He came into the Peaks specifically to document newly returning plants, animals, relics, and texts. This isn't one extinct plant.
It's **dozens**.
For Walthas, this room is potentially one of the most significant natural discoveries of his lifetime.
After the battle, he finds a weatherproof leather field journal inside a stone cabinet.
It belonged to **Ilyan Mossquill, Waykeeper and Naturalist**.
Don't make Ilyan secretly important. He was just a guy who loved this place.

## Room 5 — Reward/Revelation: Names on the Wall

The final chamber sits immediately before the Greenway's exit.
**Read aloud:**

> The garden path eventually reaches a pair of enormous stone doors. Unlike everything behind you, these remain almost perfectly intact.
> 
> Thousands of names cover the surrounding walls.
> 
> Some are carved carefully in elegant script. Others are little more than scratches. Several have dates. Some include tiny pictures—a mountain, a flower, a crude goat, two hands joined together.
> 
> Between them stands a weathered relief of a robed figure kneeling beneath the branches of a tree. One hand rests against the earth. The other is extended toward a traveler.
> 
> Beneath it, a short inscription remains legible:
> 
> _“May all who pass beneath these mountains emerge beneath open sky.”_
> 
> Below the inscription is a stretch of bare stone.

These are the names of travelers who completed the Greenway.

Give the players a moment with it. Don't tell them to carve their names. The empty space makes the invitation obvious enough.

Nearby they find Ilyan's workroom. His journal confirms that the Greenway was maintained under the authority of the **Green Archdruid**, whose servants maintained roads, shelters, gardens, and safe passages through this region.

That's the entire lore revelation.

No ancient war. No suspicious Red figure. No magical winter explanation. No indication that Green is alive.

Just:

**The Green Archdruid's people once protected travelers here.**

Ilyan's journal mostly contains exactly what Walthas would write: sketches of plants, descriptions of animal behavior, weather observations, amusing stories about travelers, and notes about maintaining the trail. Walthas has essentially discovered a naturalist from a thousand years ago who shared his interests.

One unfinished entry can read:

> _“Spotted three red-crested finches near the southern spring this morning. First mating pair I've seen this high in years. If they're nesting, I should—”_

And that's where the surviving page ends.

Not everything unfinished needs to be ominous.

### Treasure

Keep the rewards practical: **2 Potions of Healing** stored in the waykeeper's emergency supplies, Ilyan's journal, several samples of rare plants, and the partial pre-winter trail map. I'd also let Walthas harvest enough unusual specimens to be genuinely valuable to scholars back home.

The biggest reward, though, is travel. Alexis's examination of the old map reveals a surviving route that gives the party **advantage on their next navigation check** and gets them meaningfully closer to Mountain 1.

When they finally push open the doors:

> Stone grinds against stone, and brilliant afternoon sunlight spills through the widening gap.
> 
> Beyond lies a valley untouched by any map you've ever seen. Forest blankets the slopes below. Streams glitter between stands of young trees, and distant herds move through fields of spring grass.
> 
> Far beyond them, rising above ridge after ridge, stands the first of the Three Masters.
> 
> The old Greenway has done exactly what its builders promised.
> 
> It has brought you safely through the mountain.

**DM purpose:** Room 1 establishes spring's returning wildlife; Room 2 teaches Green's symbols; Room 3 hooks Alexis through lost navigation knowledge; Room 4 hooks Walthas through the rediscovery of the natural world; Room 5 gives the Archdruid a small amount of positive characterization. Nobody learns anything important about the campaign's central mystery yet.
