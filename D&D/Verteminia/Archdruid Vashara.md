Vashara is remembered as the third Archdruid, supposedly governing some aspect of nature associated with **fire, heat, destruction, or renewal**. In reality, he is a powerful fiend—currently I'd keep him as a **Rakshasa**—who infiltrated the druidic order and successfully convinced the others that he belonged among them. His ultimate objective was to use the summit of the mountains to open a permanent planar rift to the Hells.

Maelora and Tharos stopped the ritual before it could be completed, but they couldn't safely kill Vashara or close what he had begun. Their solution was the Long Winter: freeze the mountains, the unfinished rift, Vashara, and themselves together. Vashara has now awakened before the others fully recovered and is quietly preparing to resume his work. For the early campaign, however, the players shouldn't know **any** of this. Red should remain almost entirely a historical mystery.
