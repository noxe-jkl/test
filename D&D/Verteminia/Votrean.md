## Character Overview

- Player: psychopump
- Class/Role: Arcane Scholar / Spellcaster (manipulator of life, death, and souls)
- Defining Features: Possesses natural wings that he conceals beneath heavy garments. Carries deeply cherished belongings of his deceased wife.
- Primary Motivation: Driven by grief, loss, and an obsession with the boundaries between life and death.
