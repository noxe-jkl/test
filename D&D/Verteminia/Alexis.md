> "Ensuring that kindness would be repaid and that no one would end up like she nearly did."

## Character Overview

- Player: BRIX
- Role/Class: Local Wilderness Guide / Survivalist
- Backstory Summary: Grew up as a wilderness nomad. At age 12, her family was ambushed by a wolf pack in the wastes, severely injuring her father. Locals from Sumistra rescued them and gave them a permanent home. Upon turning 18, Alexis became a guide to pay forward the life-saving generosity shown to her family.
