Thelar is the Archdruid of **fauna, spirits, migration, and the boundary between life and death**. His followers traditionally acted as wardens of animals and guides for spirits, treating death as a natural transition rather than something inherently tragic or evil. Copper marks his influence, often appearing alongside imagery of antlers, migrating animals, or spirits. Tourmaline was created directly by this Archdruid, giving the party a particularly personal connection to him.

Thelar joined Maelora in creating the great freezing spell when Red's plan was discovered. Like Maelora, he has only recently awakened after a millennium trapped in stasis, and the experience has fractured his mind. His connection to spirits may have made that imprisonment especially strange: while his body slept, some portion of him may have remained aware of the countless creatures and people dying throughout the thousand-year winter.
