## Rumors & Gossip

> [!success] Good Rumors
> 
> - Perfect Memory: You've heard that Walthas (Ani's character) can perfectly recall anything he reads after seeing it just once. Townsfolk say that's how he knows so many ancient tales word-for-word.
>     
> - Heavenly Cuisine: Word around town is that Walthas (Ani's character) is such a talented cook that eating a meal prepared by him gives you a temporary glimpse of heaven itself.
>     

> [!warning] Bad Rumors
> 
> - Awakened Trees: Whispers claim that the parents of Walthas (Ani's character) are actually massive living trees hiding out in the wild, waiting to awaken and wreak total havoc on Sumistra as soon as Spring fully takes hold.
>     
> - Archdruid Cult Spy: Anti-Spring traditionalists rumor that Walthas (Ani's character) is secretly an agent working for an old Archdruid cult, using his enchanting stories to charm the town's youth into leaving Sumistra so he can kidnap them.
>     

> [!quote] False Rumors
> 
> - Tree-Grafted Sterility: Gossip circulates that because Walthas (Ani's character) is half-tree, he is completely incapable of fathering children.
>     
>     - The Truth: He can have children, though the biological process is unique and strange.
>         

## NPC Connections & Dynamics

### Friends & Allies

- The Local Leatherworker: Walthas spent time during the long wait befriending Sumistra's local leatherworker to learn the trade, specifically honing his skills in crafting and repairing boots and armor for rugged mountain travel.

### Avoids & Friction

- Acacia (Mother): Walthas actively dodges his mother, Acacia. She is protective and deeply anxious about him risking his life out in the treacherous wild just to record stories of the coming Spring.
