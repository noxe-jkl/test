## 1. The Arrival (Read-Aloud Text)
*Wait until the party thinks they are completely alone in the wilderness, then read:*

> "The trail climbs through dense young forest until the trees abruptly give way to a ridge overlooking the valley below. For a moment, nothing seems unusual. 
> 
> Then you see the smoke. 
> 
> Thin gray columns rise between the trees—not wildfire, but chimney smoke. Beneath them are rooftops. Dozens of them. Stone cottages crowd around terraced fields along the valley floor, connected by narrow footpaths and wooden bridges. Someone has built a waterwheel across the stream. 
> 
> And somewhere below, a bell begins to ring."

### Initial Reactions
*   **The Villagers:** They are equally shocked. Nobody has crossed the mountains in generations. A group of armed strangers walking down from the pass will cause genuine panic and immediate defensive posturing. 
*   **The Vibe:** Isolated, but not primitive. They have local politics, marriages, laws, and skilled trades. 
*   **Economy:** Barter only. Metal and salt are incredibly precious.

## 2. Key NPC: Elder Yseld Bramblevein
**Role:** Briarhollow's "Rootspeaker" (Local leader/priestess)
**Stats:** Standard Druid (CR 2) - Firbolg. 
**Personality:** Elderly, warm, practical, unsentimental. Corrects people who worship Green as a god. *"She is the Keeper, not your god. Gods ask for worship. She asked us to tend the garden."*

**What She Will Share Freely:**
*   **History:** The old stories about the Archdruids fighting to cause the Winter are wrong. Green's servants evacuated the valley into shelters *before* the Winter hit. It was planned.
*   **Viktor’s Hook (Caperkin):** If Viktor mentions Caperkin: *"Caperkin? Then you've wandered farther into Copper's affairs than I'd recommend. Whatever grievance you have with the Horn, don't bring it to Green's doorstep. He is not hers."* 

**What She Is Hiding (Tier 1 - The Warning):**
*   Three nights ago, the ancient tree (Heartroot) spoke aloud for the first time in centuries: **"Do not let them climb."**
*   Tree roots have been moving under the village at night. She is terrified.
*   *Trigger:* She initially thinks "them" means the party. Once she realizes the timeline doesn't match (the party arrived too late), she will trust them with this secret.

**What She Is Hiding (Tier 2 - The Chamber):**
*   Beneath the Heartroot is a sealed chamber. Inside is an ancient stone relief of the Three Archdruids.
*   Green and Copper stand side-by-side (allies). Red stands apart.
*   **The Clue:** Red's face was deliberately gouged out of the stone right before the Winter.
*   *Trigger:* The party must earn massive trust, save the village, or catch her in a lie to see this.

## 3. Player-Specific Hooks
*   **Walthas:** The village herbalists have living specimens and knowledge of plants/creatures that Sumistra considers completely extinct. A treasure trove of lost botanical lore.
*   **Alexis:** The local hunters know this specific terrain better than she does. She can swap wilderness survival tactics with them, earning their respect.
*   **Viktor:** Establish that Copper (Caperkin's boss) and Green are distinct. Yseld's words prove that the Archdruids aren't one monolith, narrowing Viktor's vengeance specifically to Copper's territory.

---

## 4. Side Quest: The Disappearances (Mundane Threat)
*   **The Hook:** Three hunters and a shepherd have vanished. Livestock found abandoned but uneaten.
*   **The Misdirection:** Villagers think it's just a spring predator (or maybe Green's wrath). 
*   **The Truth:** A young **Ankheg** population has awakened beneath the terraced fields. (The burrowing explains why Yseld is hearing/feeling "roots" moving under the village—it's a mix of the Heartroot's agitation and literal bug monsters).
*   **Resolution:** Clearing the nest earns the village's absolute trust (unlocking Yseld's secrets and giving the party a permanent safe haven).

## 5. Main Plot: "Do Not Let Them Climb" (The Ashen Company)

**The Antagonists:** The Ashen Company (6-7 explorers). They aren't evil; they are treasure hunters whose leader is being manipulated by Red/Vashara via dreams to open a "treasure vault."

**The Clue Trail (Pace this out over the session/next session):**
1.  **The Footprints:** Local hunters tell Alexis they found bootprints a few days north. Nobody from the village went that way.
2.  **Yseld's Confession:** Once Yseld realizes the footprints match the timeline of the Heartroot's warning, she tells the party: *"Green wasn't talking about you. Someone else is up there."* Magic cast on the Heartroot reveals sensations: boots on stone, metal on rock, and *something red behind closed eyes*.
3.  **The Abandoned Camp:** The party finds the Ashen Company's old camp. 
    *   *Loot:* A torn journal page: *"Three more nights with the same dream. The door is real. I know it now. Whatever they buried up there, they never meant anyone else to find it."*
4.  **Nature Retreating:** As the party climbs higher, Walthas notices the ecosystem isn't dying—it's actively fleeing. Birds fly downhill, roots pull away, flowers snap shut. Green is acting like a wounded, cornered animal.

**The Confrontation (The Ward):**
*   The party catches up to the Ashen Company at a massive stone door.
*   The door is marked with Green's symbols: An open hand beneath a leaf, and the symbol for **Thorn (Danger)**.
*   The Ashen Company leader is friendly: *"You picked a hell of a route too, huh? Come look at this. Nobody's opened that thing in a thousand years."*
*   **The Dilemma:** The Company wants to open it. Yseld said don't let them climb. The door says danger. But no one knows *why*. 
*   **Outcomes:** 
    *   *If stopped:* The ward holds. Green's territory calms down.
    *   *If opened:* The ward (part of Red's prison) breaks. The Ashen Company might be killed by awakening ancient Green Guardians, and the mountain becomes violently hostile as Green's automated defenses activate.
