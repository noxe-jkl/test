# Viktor

> "It's like trying to find a needle in a haystack... except the needle is wearing a full-face helmet inside a tavern."

## Character Overview

- Player: Likan
- Ancestry: Human
- Class: Fighter
- Defining Quirk: Facial bandages completely obscuring his features, covered at all times by a helmet.
- Primary Motivation: Keeping his head low, keeping busy with odd jobs to pass the time until Spring arrives.

## Rumors & Gossip

> [!success] Good Rumors
> 
> - Master of the Blade: Townsfolk mention that Viktor (Likan's character) trains and fights with terrifying skill, swinging broad-bladed weapons like someone ready to kill at a moment's notice.
>     
> - Helpful Neighbor: Despite his intimidating look, locals note that Viktor (Likan's character) is always remarkably polite to vendors and frequently lends a hand unloading heavy cargo during his off time.
>     

> [!warning] Bad Rumors
> 
> - Fugitive Killer: Whispers around town say that Viktor (Likan's character) is a ruthless murderer who was forced to flee his homeland after leaving a trail of dead bodies behind him.
>     
> - Scarred Criminal: Rumor has it that beneath his helmet and wrappings, Viktor (Likan's character) hides a wretched, horribly scarred face solely to avoid being recognized for past crimes.
>     

> [!quote] False Rumors
> 
> - Soulless Monster: Gossip circulates that what hides beneath the helmet of Viktor (Likan's character) isn't human at all, possessing eyes far too fierce to belong to anything with a soul.
>     
>     - The Truth: Viktor is entirely human, though his face remains hidden behind facial bandages.
>         

## NPC Connections & Dynamics

### Friends & Allies

- The Town Medic / Nurse: The single individual in [[Sumistra]] Viktor trusts. This medic is the only person who has ever seen Viktor's actual face, as they assist him with changing his facial bandages in strict privacy.

### Avoids & Friction

- Virtually Everyone Else: Viktor actively avoids general socialization. He only speaks to locals when necessary for work or trade while waiting for the mountain passes to clear.

## Equipment & Playstyle

- Armor: Heavy/Medium Helmet (never removed in public).
- Combat Style: Devastating heavy weapon strikes (Great Weapon Master).
- Demeanor: Reticent, stoic, keeps his head low to the ground.
