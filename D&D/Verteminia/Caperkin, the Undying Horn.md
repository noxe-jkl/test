> _"You chop off a leg, it grows back out of pine needles. You throw it off a cliff, it trot-trots back up the wall behind you. It's not a goat—it's an insult with horns."_

## Lore & Encounter Context

Rolled as a random mountain encounter, **Caperkin** appears at first to be an ordinary, slightly smug mountain goat grazing on fresh thaw moss. In truth, Caperkin is a fey lieutenant bound to the impish Archdruid of Mountain 1. He acts as an immortal scout, sentry, and nuisance guarding the pass to the Archdruid's domain.

He cannot be slain by normal weapons—whenever reduced to zero hit points, he melts into a burst of spring petals and pollen, only to pop back up completely unharmed moments later to bleat mockingly at the party.

## Visual & Physical Description

### **Beast Form (Mountain Goat)**

At a distance, Caperkin looks like a sturdy, thick-furred mountain goat with shaggy cream and charcoal wool. Up close, however, his fey nature becomes glaringly obvious. His hide is coarse and woven with living pine needles, moss, and flexible strips of cedar bark rather than normal animal hair. His split hooves are polished like aged bronze, leaving faint, glowing copper leaf prints in snow, mud, or rock wherever he treads.

Curving backward from his skull are a pair of sweeping, ringed horns tipped with solid copper that faintly spark or hum when magic is cast nearby. His eyes are bright, amber spheres with horizontal slit pupils that sparkle with unmistakable impish intelligence.

### **Humanoid Form (Fey Satyr / Trickster)**

When dropping his beast façade to banter, bargain, or openly mock travelers, Caperkin shifts into a wiry, five-foot-tall satyr-like humanoid.

- **Attire & Features:** He wears an asymmetrical jacket woven from living pine needles, soft charcoal wool, and flexible cedar strips, fastened with polished copper clasps. His lower body retains shaggy goat legs terminating in bronze-hooved feet.
    
- **Face & Hair:** He has sharp, exaggerated features—a pointed chin, high cheekbones, and an arching brow framed by wild, dark hair woven with dried berries and small pinecones. The pair of copper-tipped horns crest out from his hairline, sweeping back over his head. His expression is locked in a perpetual half-smirk, and his amber slit eyes gleam with sharp, mischievous cunning.
    
- **Voice:** In this form, his voice is raspy, pitch-shifting, and theatrical—sounding like a sly carnival caller punctuated by snicker-bleats.
    

### **Sensory Details**

- **Sight:** When he moves or shifts forms, tiny copper sparklers and green leaves drift lazily from his body. When struck by weapons, he bleeds sweet green tree sap rather than red blood.
    
- **Sound:** His hooves sound like heavy wooden dice rattling on stone. His bleat (or chuckle) is loud, grating, and unnaturally resonant.
    
- **Smell:** He carries a sharp, crisp scent of crushed pine needles, fresh cedar resin, and ozone, like the mountain air right after a thunderstorm.
    

### **Mannerisms & Demeanor**

Caperkin strikes ridiculous, dramatic poses on top of narrow crags and high boulders, intentionally looking down his nose at travelers. He has a bad habit of chewing on things he shouldn't—leather straps, spellbook tassels, or potion satchels—while locking eye contact with his victims. When threatened, he doesn't flinch or display fear; he simply cocks his head, winks one amber eye, and waits for the incoming strike with smug satisfaction.

- **Skills:** $\text{Acrobatics } +4$, $\text{Deception } +4$, $\text{Stealth } +4$

- **Challenge:** $1$ ($200\text{ XP}$)
    

### **Traits**

- **Shapechanger:** Caperkin can use his action to polymorph into his goat beast form or back into his fey humanoid form. His statistics remain the same in both forms. Any equipment he is wearing or carrying transforms with him.
    
- **Archdruid's Immortality (The Goat That Won't Die):** When Caperkin drops to $0$ hit points, he does not die or fall unconscious. Instead, his body instantly explodes into a cloud of fragrant pine needles, green leaves, and copper sparklers. At the start of his next turn, he reappears at full hit points in an unoccupied space within $30\text{ ft.}$, shakes himself off, and resumes whatever he was doing.
    
    - _Bypassing Immortality:_ Caperkin can only be permanently dispersed, banished, or subdued if:
        
        1. A character succeeds on a $\text{DC } 12$ Charisma (Persuasion or Performance) check to offer him a riddle, high-quality offering (like rare berries or copper coin), or proper fey greeting.
            
        2. He is bound with cold iron or splashed with holy/vital spring water.
            
        3. The party enters the Archdruid's inner sanctum, where his protective glamour thins.
            
- **Charge (Goat Form Only):** If Caperkin moves at least $20\text{ ft.}$ straight toward a target and then hits it with a Ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra $3$ ($1\text{d}6$) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a $\text{DC } 12$ Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
    
- **Sure-Footed:** Caperkin has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock him prone or knock him off a ledge.
    

### **Actions**

- **Ram (Goat Form) / Headbutt (Humanoid Form):** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ $+4$ to hit, reach $5\text{ ft.}$, one target. _Hit:_ $5$ ($1\text{d}6 + 2$) bludgeoning damage plus $2$ ($1\text{d}4$) force damage.
    
- **Taunting Bleat (Recharge 5–6):** Caperkin emits an excruciatingly obnoxious, echoing fey bleat or mocking laugh. Each hostile creature within $30\text{ ft.}$ that can hear him must make a $\text{DC } 12$ Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes $4$ ($1\text{d}8$) psychic damage and has disadvantage on all attack rolls made against targets other than Caperkin until the end of its next turn.
    

### **Roleplay & Tactical Notes**

- **Tactics:** Caperkin doesn't fight to murder the party—he fights to frustrate them, knock them down slopes, eat their supplies out of their backpacks, and run up vertical cliff faces where they can't follow.
    
- **Clues to His True Identity:**
    
    - His hooves leave glowing copper leaf prints in the snow/mud.
        
    - When hit by a sword or spell, instead of bleeding red blood, he leaks green sap that smells like sweet spring blossoms.
        
    - If a player character speaks Sylvan or Druidic to him, he will drop the "goat act," shift forms, and banter with them in a sharp, impish voice.
