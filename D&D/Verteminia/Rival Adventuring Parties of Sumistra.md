As the 1,146-year winter fractures, competition among scavengers, mercenaries, and scholars scrambling out of Sumistra is fierce. Below are two fully fleshed-out rival parties active in the area, incorporating established lore figures and newly named rivals.

# The Gilded Compass

_Note: This encounter is designed so that Sylvester and Bart spend Rounds 1 and 2 interacting with the Green Ward rather than attacking, effectively making the opening rounds a 4-v-5 fight before the escape._

## Sylvester Vane-Gild (Expedition Leader)

_Medium humanoid (human), Neutral Evil_ **Armor Class:** 14 (Studded Leather) **Hit Points:** 65 (10d8 + 20) _(Upgraded to 6th-Level Fiend Warlock)_ **Speed:** 30 ft. **STR** 11 (+0) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 14 (+2) | **WIS** 13 (+1) | **CHA** 18 (+4) **Saving Throws:** Wis +4, Cha +7 **Skills:** Arcana +5, History +5, Persuasion +7, Deception +7 **Damage Resistances:** Fire (Fiendish Resilience - though thematic, strictly speaking a level 10 feature, but you can hint at his fiery patron) **Senses:** passive Perception 11 **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Sylvan, Infernal

**Spellcasting (Warlock):** Sylvester is a 6th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (Spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). He regains his expended spell slots when he finishes a short or long rest. He knows the following warlock spells:

- Cantrips (at will): _Eldritch Blast_ (2 beams), _Mage Hand_, _Prestidigitation_
    
- 3rd level (2 slots): _Command_, _Fireball_, _Hex_, _Hold Person_, _Misty Step_, _Scorching Ray_, _Vampiric Touch_
    

## Jaspen (The Condescending Scholar)
**AC 15** (natural bark armor)  
**HP 76**  
**Speed 30 ft.**  
**STR 10, DEX 14, CON 16, INT 13, WIS 18, CHA 11**  
**Saving Throws:** Con +6, Wis +7  
**Skills:** Nature +4, Perception +7, Survival +7  
**Spell Save DC 15, Spell Attack +7**

**Cantrips:** _druidcraft, produce flame, thorn whip_  
**3/day each:** _entangle, thunderwave, healing word_  
**2/day each:** _spike growth, moonbeam_  
**1/day:** _plant growth_

Rather than tracking normal spell slots, I'd use those uses. Much easier during combat.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Jaspen makes two attacks using Thorn Whip or Gnarled Staff in any combination.

**Gnarled Staff.** _Melee Spell Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: **1d10 force** damage.

**Thorn Whip.** _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +7 to hit, range 30 ft. Hit: **7 (2d6) piercing**, and Jaspen can pull the target up to 10 feet toward himself.

That pull becomes **really useful** with the sanctuary hazards.

### Bonus Action — Verdant Step

Jaspen magically moves up to **15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks**, provided he begins or ends the movement adjacent to vegetation.

Use this to make him incredibly annoying rather than incredibly lethal.

### Reaction — Briar Ward

When Jaspen is hit by an attack, roots briefly wrap around him, reducing the damage by **1d8 + 3**.
**3/day.**

## Bart (The Hired Muscle/Locksmith)

_Medium humanoid (dwarf), True Neutral_ **Armor Class:** 12 (Leather Armor) **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2) **Speed:** 25 ft. **STR** 11 (+0) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 12 (+1) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 10 (+0) **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish

**Cunning Action:** On each of his turns, Bart can use a bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

### Actions

- **Scimitar:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
    
- **Light Crossbow:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
    

> **Tactics:** Bart spends Rounds 1 and 2 prying at the stone door mechanism with his tools (using his action). If the party engages him, he uses _Cunning Action_ to Disengage and put a meat shield (Helga) between himself and the attacker. He surrenders the moment Sylvester escapes.

## Helga (The Underpaid Guard)

_Medium humanoid (human), True Neutral_ **Armor Class:** 16 (Chain Shirt, Shield) **Hit Points:** 22 (4d8 + 4) _(Increased HP)_ **Speed:** 30 ft. **STR** 14 (+2) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 12 (+1) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 11 (+0) | **CHA** 10 (+0) **Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +2 **Senses:** passive Perception 12 **Languages:** Common

### Actions

- **Spear:** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.
    

> **Tactics:** Helga is the frontline. She intercepts anyone trying to reach Sylvester or Bart at the door. However, she fights defensively and non-lethally. If her HP drops below half, or if it's clear the party has the overwhelming upper hand, she drops her spear and quits on the spot.   

## Party 2: The Ash-Carver Pack

- **Theme:** Ruthless mercenaries, cutthroat scavengers, desperate opportunists.
    
- **Motive:** Scraping money out of the thaw by any means necessary—looting ruins, ambushing other parties, and selling dangerous relics on the black market.
    

### Members (5)

1. **Jensen Vane (Human Battlemaster / Mercenary Captain)**
    
    - **Role:** Frontline Leader & Cutthroat Fighter
        
    - **Lore Connection:** Viktor's arch-nemesis.
        
    - **Description:** The smooth-talking, ruthless mercenary who attacked people in a Sumistra tavern, killed the owner, set the building on fire, and framed Viktor for the crime. Having escaped prosecution, Jensen now commands a cutthroat squad of scavengers seeking to grow rich off the thaw's unsealed treasures.
        
2. **Mirela Darkweaver (Human Necromancer)**
    
    - **Role:** Occultist & Soul-Scavenger
        
    - **Lore Connection:** Rival spellcaster to Votrean.
        
    - **Description:** A sinister mage obsessed with harvesting the lingering energies of Archdruids and dead beasts. She actively targets the same forbidden arcana as Votrean, though with zero ethical boundary or respect for the dead.
        
3. **Grakka Split-Shield (Half-Orc Barbarian)**
    
    - **Role:** Heavy Enforcer & Berserker
        
    - **Lore Connection:** Combat rival for Viktor.
        
    - **Description:** Jensen's primary muscle, wielding a massive greataxe. Grakka loves testing his strength against heavily armored opponents and takes particular pleasure in trying to crack open helmets.
        
4. **Saris "Viper" Khol (Tiefling Shadow Rogue)**
    
    - **Role:** Infiltrator & Assassin
        
    - **Description:** A quiet, tail-flicking assassin who specializes in setting roadside ambushes for weary adventuring parties returning from dungeons.
        
5. **Maren Frost-Eye (Human Ranger)**
    
    - **Role:** Tracker & Beast Harvester
        
    - **Lore Connection:** Survivalist rival for Alexis.
        
    - **Description:** A cold, pragmatic hunter who views the mountain thaw purely as a commercial slaughterhouse. Maren tracks high-value beasts (like drakes and rare fauna) to strip them bare for meat, hide, and glands before other guides can reach them.
