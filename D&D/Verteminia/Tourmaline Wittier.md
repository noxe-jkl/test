> "Created with a map in hand and a fire in her hearth... just don't touch her wings without asking."

## Character Overview

- Player: Ledi
- Ancestry/Nature: Insect-themed magical creation with copper-colored faerie fire aesthetic. Created directly by an Archdruid.
- Adoptive Family: Raised by the dwarven blacksmith Bertram Wittier.
- Primary Objective:
    1. Main: Map the unmapped world as the 1,146-year winter thaws.
    2. Sub-Objective: Deliver confidential, critical messages to other Archdruids she encounters across Riredin and Gran Lavania.

## Rumors & Gossip

> [!success] Good Rumors
> 
> - Protector of the Grain: You've heard that Tourmaline Wittier (Ledi's character) is exceptionally kind to the town's mice and rats, and locals credit her gentle behavior for why pests haven't raided Sumistra's food stores.
>     
> - Town Prankster: Common consensus in Sumistra is that if a harmless prank was recently pulled, Tourmaline Wittier (Ledi's character) was almost certainly behind it.
>     

> [!warning] Bad Rumors
> 
> - Short Fuse: People warn that Tourmaline Wittier (Ledi's character) has a violent short temper. When she loses her cool, you can immediately tell by the creative torrent of swearing that erupts from her.
>     
> - Anti-Druid Hostility: Word is that Tourmaline Wittier (Ledi's character) behaves cold, distant, or outright rude toward any druid who isn't connected to the specific Archdruid that created her.
>     

> [!quote] False Rumors
> 
> - Secret Romance: Gossip says that Tourmaline Wittier (Ledi's character) is secretly dating the elf researcher Sylvester, but she is too shy to go public while he wants to announce it to the world.
>     
>     - The Truth: She utterly despises Sylvester's boundary-stepping behavior and scales walls to cling to ceilings just to hide from him.
>         

## NPC Connections & Dynamics

### Friends & Family

- Bertram Wittier (Uncle): A dwarven blacksmith who found her in the mountains below Sumistra. He took her in, raised her as his niece, and taught her survival and merchant skills.

### Avoids & Friction

- Sylvester: An overly enthusiastic beanpole elf researcher. He touched her wings without her permission once and bombards her with relentless questions. Tourmaline will scale walls and cling to ceilings to avoid him.

## Equipment & Customizations

- Transforming Cartographer Tools: All her weapons (daggers, sidearms) have been custom-crafted using her own coin to mechanically transform into mapmaking and cartography instruments.
- Aesthetic: Copper faerie fire glow, insectile accents.
