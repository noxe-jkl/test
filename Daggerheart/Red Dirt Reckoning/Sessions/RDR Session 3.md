## Intro Script
### The Head & The Cleaver

_Telegraphs: Invulnerability, Godcleaver/Head Toss, and the "Broken" mechanic._

> "Where a horn should be, a colossal, jagged steel cleaver protrudes from its snout. The iron plating on its face is impossibly thick and pristine; striking it while the beast is mobile would be like throwing pebbles at a fortress wall.
> 
> But you notice a fatal flaw in its design: the sheer, unbalanced weight of that blade. When the beast rears back to deliver a truly devastating, earth-shattering blow, the momentum drives the cleaver deep into the bedrock. For a few desperate moments, its neck pistons scream and buckle as it tries to rip the blade free. **In that window, while it is stuck and straining, the armor plates along its neck and head separate, leaving its inner workings completely exposed.**"

### The Torso & The Steam Vents

_Telegraphs: Steam-Powered attacks, Unbreakable Ribs, Climbing Difficulty, and the Underbelly Weak Point._

> "The central boiler of its torso is caged by external, unyielding mineral plates that look like the ribs of a slain god. Scaling this beast while it’s moving looks like a death sentence—not just because of the slick armor, but because **pressure-release valves line its back and flanks, ready to erupt with geysers of scalding steam to boil anything clinging to it.**
> 
> However, if you look beneath those unbreakable ribs, the underbelly glows with exposed heat and fragile piping. It’s tucked away and protected right now, but **if the beast were anchored to the ground—say, with its heavy head wedged into the dirt—its posture would slump.** That slump would silence the steam vents along its sides, making it easy to climb, and completely expose that soft underbelly to your blades."

### The Legs & The Chassis

_Telegraphs: Stomp/Shockwaves, Backward Kicks, The Roll, and Collapsing the beast._

> "It is propelled by four massive, hydraulic legs ending in heavy train-wheel hooves. It rears up, ready to slam down with enough force to send a shockwave through the earth that will knock you off your feet, and its hind legs look primed to kick backward like a startled mule.
> 
> Yet, those piston-driven legs are bearing hundreds of tons of iron. **If you can shatter the hydraulics on just one of those legs, the entire towering chassis will buckle and collapse into the dirt,** creating a makeshift ramp straight up onto its armored back.
> 
> But be careful if you swarm it. The central boiler is perfectly cylindrical, and the deep, circular gouges scarred into the earth around it suggest that **if it feels overwhelmed, it can drop its immense weight and barrel-roll over everything in its path to shake you off.**"
