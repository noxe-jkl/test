**Player:** Anna **Class:** Warlock (Pact of the Endless) **Patron:** The Lovers **Ancestry:** Loreborne Infernis

## Background & Motivations

- **Isolation:** Always felt a sense of disconnect from her peers due to being an Infernis.
    
- **Tragedy:** She met her fiancé through their shared interest in studying the shadowblight. He was killed when a piece of a building collapsed on him, leading her to dive even deeper into her studies.
    
- **Obsession:** Received disciplinary action for researching ways to use the shadowblight to revive the dead, but it did not discourage her.
    

## The Pact

- **The Deal:** Her patrons ("The Lovers") grant her power in exchange for her reviving them. In return, they have promised the possible revival of her dead fiancé.
    
- **The Mark:** The magical contract is bound within her engagement ring, and the ritual caused her hair to turn white.
    

## Connections

- **Zeke (Zekrivash):** Met Zeke during field research, and they became fast friends over their mutual interest in the blight. Zeke introduced her to the ritual that resulted in contact with her patrons (though her memory of the ritual is fuzzy). She confides in Zeke about what her patrons tell her.
