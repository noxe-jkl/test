For one thousand years, the Seekers stalked the wastelands of Valloren. Though the party is unaware, these shades are the baleful spirits of the eleven fallen magi, starved of terrana and furious at the failure of their rebellion. Now, they finally have a chance to bring back their overlords—or take power for themselves.

In their natural form, the Seekers are tattered, vaguely humanoid shrouds of shadow-stuff with stretched and contorted proportions. Much as in life, they can draw terrana from the land to power their magic spells. Unlike in life, their doing so leaves a wound in the environment that fills with shadowblight.

After absorbing terrana, a Seeker’s appearance takes on aspects of the environment they devastated. For instance, after consuming terrana in a forest, their body might grow gray leaves and become covered in bark. A Seeker who devastates a snowy field might gain crystalline skin or ice-white patches of fur. This absorbed terrana doesn’t just change their appearance, it also grants them access to new abilities that reflect these altered states. When a Seeker is defeated, their body regenerates some time later—though a party could endeavor to find a special weapon or ritual that can destroy the Seeker for good.


# Stat Blocks
**Tier 2 Solo: [[Inner Demon]]** (_Archibald's Almanac of Adversaries_)

  

- **Mini Match:** The thorny, shadowy "Nightwalker" mini perfectly represents a Seeker operating in its natural, untethered shroud of shadow-stuff.
    
      
    
- **Thematic Fit:** Embodying the baleful, psychic weight of the magi's ancient failure, this adversary terrorizes the party before asserting physical dominance. It uses **Bitter Memories** to decrease the party's Hope dice and **Possess** to physically take control of a player character, simulating a magic-starved spirit leeching the party's minds and bodies.
    
      
    

**Tier 3 Solo: Archmage** (_Daggerheart SRD_)

  

- **Mini Match:** The cloaked, faceless "Revenant 2" mini captures the essence of a fallen magus who has consumed enough terrana to reassert their ancient arcane dominance and manifest a physical weapon.
    
      
    
- **Thematic Fit:** Operating as a corrupted master of magic, the Archmage uses **Orbiting Aegis** to deflect blows and **Counterspell** to shut down the party's abilities. You can flavor their **Force Salvo** and **Fireball** actions as blasts of pure, toxic shadowblight drawn directly from the wounded environment.
    
      
    

**Tier 4 Solo: Disfavored** (_Archibald's Almanac of Adversaries_)

  

- **Mini Match:** The "Wyvern Revenant" mini represents a Seeker that has gorged on massive amounts of corrupted terrana (perhaps from a draconic or mountainous environment), mutating its physical form into a winged monstrosity to take power for itself.
    
      
    
- **Thematic Fit:** This adversary operates with overwhelming, oppressive strength. The **Now I Take From You** action allows it to deal massive magic damage and lock away a PC's domain card, reflecting the Seeker actively draining the party's magical potential. Its **Hateful Aura** forces the entire party to use a d20 for their Fear die, mechanically cementing the world-ending threat of a fully restored overlord.
