# Session Plan
- Opening Scene (_In Media Res_):
 > For one thousand years, the people of Marrogate knew only the amber glow of the barrier above their heads. For thirty generations, your ancestors lived, loved, and died within this stone sanctuary, shielded by the dying sacrifice of the goddess Aurelia while the world outside burned in the Starfall.
> For the past two weeks, you've contracted to **High Priestess Saraya Rinn**, the highest authority of the Aurelian Order in Marrogate, and she vetted and hired each of you for your specific, lethal, and practical talents. She was the architect of an expedition to find newly discovered Soul Echos, a way to bring Aurelia back together to reconstruct Valloren from the destruction it has endrured. Your role was to act as her specialized escort, protect the **Vessel of Light** holding the goddess’s Soul Echo found in marrogate, a small seed of light, and get her safely across the continent to discover the others and Journey to Horizon.
> However, those plans seem to have gone up in smoke.
> You gathered at the inner shrine for the final staging check before departure, when her apprentice, **Tolren**, barred the heavy bronze doors behind you. Armed **Fellsworn Templars** of the Two-Headed Coin emerged from the shadows, turning the sacred hall into an enclosed kill-box designed to end Saraya's mission and seize the Vessel. Cornered, outmatched, and facing immediate slaughter, you had no tactical exit.
> Saraya took the only option left. Drawing upon the raw, unchecked terrana surging through the earth, she drove her staff into the flagstones of the temple. Colossal crystalline roots tore violently through the shrine floor, crushing the Templar vanguard, bringing down the massive stone rafters on the attackers, and blowing an emergency breach through the rear wall. Her suicide strike bought you the few precious seconds you needed: enough time to haul the Vessel off its pedestal, dive through the crumbling fissure, and vanish into the undercity before the room caved in completely.
> Now, the alarm bells of Marrogate are clanging in frantic, discordant strokes. Black smoke billows from the shattered temple dome, blotting out the morning sun. You are sprinting through the shadowed, rain-slicked cobblestones of the Lower Warrens. The Vessel in your arms is heavy, ancient, and pulsing with a radiant, heartbeat-like warmth that leaks golden light between your fingers.
> Behind you, iron boots strike stone as Templar hunting horns echo down the alleys. Ahead, the massive western gatehouse—where the old barrier boundary meets the city walls—is in utter disarray. Before you can reach the breach, a heavy masonry archway, cracks overhead and begins to collapse directly across your path.
