**Player:** Jack **Class:** Ranger (Wayfinder) **Ancestry:** Ridgeborne Faun

## Background & Motivations

- **The Attack:** While hunting, he was brutally attacked by a mysterious, deer-like monster (which he refers to as the "Forest King", inspired by the "Not Deer" cryptid) that shattered and tore out his ribs. As a result, he is terrified of deer.
    
- **The Quest:** He firmly believes that the goddess Aurelia is the one who rescued him and saved his life after the attack. His primary motivation for joining the quest is to find Aurelia and thank her.
    
- **Revenge:** His secondary goal is to track down and kill the "Forest King."
    
- **Personality:** He is highly superstitious.
