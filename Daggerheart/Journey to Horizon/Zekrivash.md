**Player:** Daniel (Archerdan) **Class:** Witch (Hedge) **Ancestry:** Freeborne Goblin

## Background & Playstyle

- **Personality:** Dangerously curious and oblivious, highly prone to walking into traps. He is a "silly goober."
- **Party Dynamics:** Functions as the group's "shadowblight drug supplier." Several party members owe him their lives, and he is setting up these debts to function as binding, traditional "fey deals."
- **The Shadowblight:** Daniel wants him to gain shadowblight corruption and mutations (mechanics inspired by Drakkenheim (I have this under control)).
- **Connections:** He became fast friends with Filia Lenue during field research over their mutual interest in the shadowblight. He introduced her to a blight ritual that connected her to her patrons. She confides in him.

## Companions & Mechanics

- **Pet:** Has a mechanically harmless feline pet. As Zekrivash gains shadowblight corruption, the cat mirrors this corruption and changes in appearance alongside him.
- **Self-Preservation:** He plans to narratively and mechanically burn his own resources to survive his antics so he doesn't become a burden to the party's healers.
