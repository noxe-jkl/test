A massive, labyrinthine cave system beneath [[Elmore]]. It's said to be endless, stretching for hundreds of miles. The caves are filled with strange creatures drawn from all over the region by [[The Lure]], creating a unique and dangerous ecosystem. The caves are not uninhabited by people; various camps and even small permanent settlements exist within. A communal culture has developed, with bartering, trade, and even a chain of restaurants called [[Berry's Restaurant]].

- **Access:** Follow the birdsong of piping plovers from the western road out of [[Elmore]]. These birds can navigate the tunnels freely.
- **Key Regions:** [[Brilliance]], [[Reflection of the Stars]], [[The Catacombs]]

The caves are stratified by depth, with each layer representing 200-500 vertical feet. Deeper layers host more dangerous creatures and more valuable treasures.

- **[[The Shallows]]:** The first and most commonly explored layer.
- **[[Twilight]]:** The second layer.
- **[[Abyss]]:** The third layer, a place of significant danger. The [[Catacombs]] are at its deepest point.
- **[[Hadral]]:** The fourth and deepest commonly known layer. [[The Lure]] is believed to be located here.
