- **The Aftermath:** Assuming the party sides with Kaelen and defeats or drives off the Open Hand, Kaelen will be immensely grateful but also deeply concerned. "They will be back in greater numbers. Their ignorance of this place will be its undoing."
- **Harvesting the Venom:** The party still needs the Ghost Scorpion venom for Carat's quest. Kaelen explains that the scorpions are not inherently hostile, but are protectors. Killing them would be a desecration. He can guide the party through a ritual of respect. This allows them to harvest the venom without harming the creatures, demonstrating the Secret-Keepers' way.
- **The Invitation:** Impressed by their willingness to listen and their prowess in battle, Kaelen extends an offer. "Our Elder must meet you. We thought we were alone in our struggle, but you... you listen. We are fighting to save this cave from the sickness the Open Hand spreads, the same sickness that makes The Lure fail. Please, come back with me to our home. To the Coenobium of Still Waters."

**Session End:** The session ends with the party having acquired the venom and now facing a choice: return to Elmore to complete their initial quest for Carat, or follow Kaelen deeper into the caves to meet the leader of the Secret-Keepers and learn the truth behind the failing Lure.
