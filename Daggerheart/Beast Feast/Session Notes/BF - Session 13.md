## 1. The Approach: The World Dissolves
As the GM, read or paraphrase the following to set the scene. This is the moment they enter the Traversal challenge.
> The rough-hewn, damp tunnels of the Plover Caves suddenly end. It’s not a wall, but an *absence*. The very concept of "tunnel" dissolves into a vast, nauseating chamber that defies mortal senses.
> Before you is a space of impossible geometry. Floating islands of jagged crystal drift on unseen currents. Waterfalls of pure, static-laced light flow *upwards* into swirling vortexes of shadow. The air itself feels thin, vibrating with a low, dissonant *thrum*.
> And in the center, embedded in what might be the "floor" or the "ceiling," is the heart of this madness. It is a massive, orb-like creature, pulsating with a sickly, radiant light. Its flesh is a fusion of crystal and raw, unformed magic. A gaping, luminous maw at its center cracks open, and you feel it—a tangible, spiritual, and physical *pull*. It is drawing all things toward it, a vacuum of annihilating power.
> This chamber *is* the Minor Chaos Realm. To reach the orb, you must cross this impossible space.

### 🧲 Mechanical Kick-in: Impossible Architecture

This is the central challenge. The moment they decide to move forward, activate the **Impossible Architecture** feature.  

> "To cross this alien landscape and reach the heart of the orb, you must overcome an **8-step Progress Countdown**. The difficulty for all rolls to navigate this space is **17**.
> "As you take your first step, gravity lurches. What feels like 'down' is now 'forward,' and the only path is a sliver of crystal no wider than your boot, hanging over a sea of swirling chaos.
> "To the first person moving: **What landmark or point do you fixate on to maintain your balance in this madness?**"

* *On a successful roll:* Mark 1 tick on the Progress Countdown.

* *On a failure:* "You stumble as the world reorients around you, the pull of the maw nearly tearing you from your footing. **Mark a Stress.**" Then, ask the follow-up: "**What bizarre landmark do you traverse?** A bridge made of frozen sound? A staircase that twists back on itself?"

## 2. The First Move & The Realm's Reaction

Let the first player attempt to cross. Whatever they roll, this is a perfect time for the Realm to react.
### Scenario A: The Roll Generates Fear
If the player's roll (success or failure) includes a Fear, immediately trigger **Disorienting Reality**.
> "As you find your footing or stumble, the Fear you generate allows the realm to *see* you. The very air shimmers, and the orb's light flashes. A vision slams into your mind.
> "**[Ask the PC]: What is one of your deepest fears?**
> "You see it, warped and magnified by this place. [Describe their fear, e.g., *'You see your hometown burning, but the flames are the same chaotic light as the orb.'*] The vision is so real, so visceral, it threatens to overwrite your real memory.
> "**You lose a Hope.** Is that your last one?" (If yes, you gain a Fear).


### Scenario B: The Realm Activates

  

Whether orA not the first roll generated Fear, the realm is now aware of them. It's time to use an Action. A great opener is **Everything You Are This Place Will Take from You**.

  

> "The orb pulses, and a psychic shriek echoes, not in your ears, but in your *talents*. It feels like a hook, pulling at the very thing that makes you *you*.

>

> "I am activating **'Everything You Are This Place Will Take from You.'**"

>

> *Roll 1d4 for the Countdown (let's say you roll a 3).*

>

> "A 3-step Countdown begins. This place is trying to *unmake* your legend. You feel it leaching at your skills, your power, your very essence."

  

This clock will now tick down, forcing the PCs to make a choice: lose their power or burn through their Stress.

## 3. The Escalation: Predators & Unmaking

  

The PCs are now juggling three things:

1. Making progress on the 8-step **Impossible Architecture** Countdown.

2. Dealing with the looming **Everything You Are...** Countdown.

3. The inherent danger of failing rolls (marking Stress).

  

Let another PC take an action. Perhaps they try to create a magical bridge, or use athletics to leap between crystal islands.

  

### Scenario C: The Realm Summons (Using Fear)

  

Let's assume you have a Fear (either from a roll or by starting the scene with one). This is the time to make the traversal a *fight*.

  

> "As you land on the next crystalline shard, the world *tears* open in defiance of logic. The space next to you simply ceases to be, and from that hole spills true horrors.

>

> "I am spending a Fear to activate **'Outer Realms Predators.'**

>

> *Roll 1d6 (let's say you roll 4).*

>

> "A hulking, many-angled **Outer Realms Corruptor** and **four skittering Outer Realms Thralls** materialize at Close range to you, [PC Name]. They are perched impossibly on a vertical wall, which is *also* the floor you're standing on.

>

> "They cast aside the half-consumed remnants of a shattered world—what looks like the petrified wing of a dragon fused with a church steeple. You catch a jagged reflection of a screaming, half-formed face in their oily carapaces before they lunge.

>

> "I am spending a *second* Fear. The Corruptor **automatically succeeds** on its standard attack against you, right now."

  

### Scenario D: The Realm Attacks (Using Fear)

  

Perhaps a PC tries to use a powerful ability—maybe a cleric calls on their god, or a warrior lets out a rallying cry. If they fail, or even if they succeed, you can spend a Fear to punish them.

  

> "Your power flares, a beacon of order in this sea of chaos. The realm *hates* it. It focuses its energy on you, [PC Name].

>

> "I am spending a Fear to use **'Unmaking.'** Make a **Strength Reaction Roll (Difficulty 17)**."

>

> * **On a failure:** "You are torn by invisible forces. The very atoms of your being feel like they are being ripped apart. You take **3d10 direct magic damage.** As this happens, what glimpse of another, terrible world do you catch? What core facet of your personality—your courage? your compassion?—does this unmaking try to erase?"

> * **On a success:** "You hold yourself together through sheer, agonizing will, but the effort is immense. **Mark a Stress.**"

## 4. The Climax: The Maw

  

The session is now in full swing. The PCs are trapped in a desperate, three-dimensional fight against summoned horrors, all while trying to solve the traversal puzzle of **Impossible Architecture**.

  

All your features are now in play:

* **Impossible Architecture** is the main objective.

* **Everything You Are...** is the looping "tax" on their abilities, forcing hard choices.

* **Outer Realms Predators** provides the immediate, physical threat.

* **Unmaking** is your high-damage "sniper shot" to punish actions or focus down a PC.

* **Disorienting Reality** is your tool for draining their Hope on every Fear-generating roll.

  

The goal is for them to reach the orb (completing the 8-step Countdown) before the Realm and its minions unmake them completely.
