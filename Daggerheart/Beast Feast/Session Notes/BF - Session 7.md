**SKEG DURASH** _Human-Orc, Male, 18 (Orc)_

**Description**: Skeg is a muscular but bandaged individual with blackened eyes and bloodstained clothing.

**Motive**: Survival, escape, avoid pain. 
**Difficulty**: 12 
**Hit Points**: 3 
**Stress**: 2 (all marked) 
**Attack Modifier**: +1 **Standard Attack**: Punch: Melee | 1d4 phy **Features**:

- **Injured**: Skeg is bruised and weakened. All his rolls have disadvantage. He cannot gain Hope.
    
- **Terrified**: Skeg will not willingly enter combat. He will attempt to flee or find cover if not actively restrained.
    
- **Sturdy**: He can endure a blow. When an attack against him succeeds, he can choose to mark a Stress instead of taking damage. This can only be done once per round.
