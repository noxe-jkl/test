**Session Start:** Begin with the party venturing onto the hunting path designated on their map from Lt. [[Guag Dhol]].

- **Atmosphere:** Describe the path. It's well-trodden, almost unnaturally so. The natural cave floor has been flattened, and the luminous moss that typically grows everywhere seems scraped away here. You can occasionally see discarded, broken traps and the picked-clean bones of common cave creatures. It feels less like a path and more like a scar.

**Scene 1: A Wasted Kill & A Puzzling Clue**

The party comes across a recently slain beast, a **Cave Pangolin**. Its armored plates are shattered.

- **The Scene:** The creature has been killed, but only a small, prime cut of meat has been taken. The rest is left to rot. This is wasteful and contrary to the "use everything" spirit of Elmore and the Beast Feast. Lem, the butcher, would find this particularly unprofessional and disrespectful.
    
- **The Clue:** Near the pangolin lies a broken spear with the mark of the Open Hand (a simple, open palm) burned into the shaft. A few feet away, almost hidden under a ledge, is a single, dark leather glove. If inspected, the players will find a symbol stitched on the inside: **a stylized eye shedding a single tear**. This is the mark of the Secret-Keepers.

### Possible Combat

#### For a 5-Player Party (17 BP)

- **3x [[Cave Pangolin]]** (using Armored Sentry stats) (4 BP each = 12 BP)
    
- **2x [[Juvenile Pangolin]]** (using Agile Scout stats, re-flavored) (2 BP each = 4 BP)
    
- **Total:** 16/17 BP. This leaves you 1 BP to spare, which you could use to add a group of Minions if you feel the fight needs more bodies, or simply run it as is for a solid, challenging fight.
    

#### For a 4-Player Party (14 BP)

- **2x Cave Pangolins** (using Armored Sentry stats) (4 BP each = 8 BP)
    
- **3x Juvenile Pangolins** (using Agile Scout stats, re-flavored) (2 BP each = 6 BP)
    
- **Total:** 14/14 BP. This provides a balanced encounter with a good mix of tough and nimble foes.
- **Beast Feast Opportunity:** The party can harvest the remaining **Cave Pangolin Plates**.
    
    - **Ingredient:** Cave Pangolin Plates (2 Ingredients)
        
    - **Flavor Profile:** Salty (2), Bitter (1).
        
    - **Interesting Property:** The plates are brittle and can be ground into a powder. (This could be a hint for a future recipe).
        

## **Scene 2: The Whispering Trap**

Further down the path, the party hears a low, rhythmic chanting in a language Zai might recognize as a dialect related to rites for Aiphysnone. They find a young **Halfling** caught in a sophisticated but non-lethal snare trap (a net of glowing, sticky threads). This is **Kaelen**, a Secret-Keeper acolyte.

- **The Encounter:** Kaelen is not injured, just stuck. He is immediately defensive and hostile, assuming the party are Open Hand aggressors. "You see? Your clumsy paths and loud marching have sprung my warding sigil! Begone, defilers!"
    
- **The Conflict:** Kaelen points out their map. "That map is a lie! This is not a hunting trail; it is a pilgrimage route, generations old. You are trespassing on sacred ground."
    
- **Revealing Nuance:**
    
    - If Zai mentions Aiphysnone (their shared patron), Kaelen will pause, confused. "Our patron grants us the wisdom to keep knowledge _safe_, not to exploit it for profit and sport. Why would a follower of the Shadowed Truth walk the path of the Clenched Fist?" This presents Zai with a direct conflict: two groups, both claiming allegiance to their patron, have completely opposite interpretations of their teachings.
        
    - Trashcan might sympathize with Kaelen, who is an outcast defending his territory from encroaching forces—much like Trashcan felt when he could no longer scrounge in Plover.
        
    - Lem, the protector, might see that Kaelen is not a "tormentor" but a defender of his ground.
        
- **The Goal:** Kaelen reveals that the "Reflection of the Stars" is a sacred pool vital to the cave's ecosystem. The Open Hand's presence is profaning it. The Ghost Scorpions aren't just beasts; they are the _guardians_ of the pool. He pleads with the party to turn back or to help him drive off an Open Hand patrol that he knows is heading for the pool.
    

**Scene 3: The Crystalline Guardians**

Whether they agree to help Kaelen or continue on their original quest, the path leads to the **Reflection of the Stars**.

- **The Location:** A vast cavern. The ceiling is coated in thousands of slowly pulsing, fist-sized crystals. They reflect perfectly in a large, unnaturally still pool of water below, creating the illusion of a starry night sky. The air hums with faint energy.
    
- **The Beasts:** Patrolling the edge of the pool are two **Ghost Scorpions**. They are translucent and shimmer with faint light. They are not immediately aggressive but will raise their stingers if the party gets too close. Mayor Doug Dougle's description of "crystalline creatures" might click here—perhaps the scorpions are not the source, but are also being affected by something.
    
- **The Twist:** Before the party can act (to get the venom, to observe, to leave), a group of four **Open Hand Hunters** arrive from another tunnel, led by a stern-faced officer named **Sergeant Valerius**.
    

**Session End Cliffhanger:** Sergeant Valerius sees the party, weapons drawn (or not), standing near the Ghost Scorpions. If Kaelen is with them, he scowls. "So, Lt. Dhol's mercenaries are consorting with the saboteurs. I knew he was too soft. Seize them! The Hand will claim this territory, by the Patron's will!"
