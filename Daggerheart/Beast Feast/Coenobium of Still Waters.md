### The Lazaretto of the Mothers Merciful

**Tier 2 Exploration**

_A secluded sanctuary where enigmatic healers treat the cosmos’s most profound physical and metaphysical ailments, operating on principles that defy conventional logic._ 1111

- **Impulses:** Offer solace to the suffering, confront uncomfortable truths, enforce a philosophy of radical acceptance.
    
- **Difficulty:** 14
    
- **Potential Adversaries:** Manifestations of Trauma (reskinned Chaos Skulls or Failed Experiments), Fold Guardians (reskinned Spectral Guardians), The Mothers Merciful (as Social adversaries)6.
    

#### FEATURES

**A New Direction - Passive:** The philosophy of the Lazaretto is one of irreversible action and forward momentum. Once per session, a character may mark a Stress after making an action roll with Fear to treat it as a roll with Hope instead. They must narrate how they accept a past failure and consciously choose a different path forward in this moment.

What past failure does this action remind you of, and how do you choose to act differently now?

**A Place of Healing - Passive:** The Lazaretto is a place of profound, if strange, healing. 10 During a rest within the Lazaretto, you can choose to undergo a unique treatment. If you do, you may clear all of either your marked Stress

_or_ your marked Hit Points. 11

What strange, unsettling, yet ultimately soothing treatment do the Mothers administer?

**Controlled Eclosion - Action:** A character suffering from a deep-seated psychological issue or a permanent scar may request eclosion therapy from the Mothers. 12If the GM agrees, this initiates a

**Progress Countdown (6)**. 13To advance the countdown, the character must make action rolls (typically using Instinct or Presence) to navigate a controlled "reverie" where they confront their inner turmoil. 14The countdown advances according to the standard dynamic countdown rules. 15When the countdown is completed, the character may either

**clear one permanent scar** or create a powerful **new Experience at +3** related to the truth they uncovered. 16

What core trauma, previously unrevealed, surfaces during this reverie? What truth do you finally accept?

**Unquantifiable Mercy - Reaction:** When a GM move would cause a PC to lose an important item permanently, you can **spend a Fear**. The item is instead badly damaged and requires a

**Progress Countdown (4)** to repair during downtime. 17This represents the Mothers' intervention, which mitigates the harshest consequences of fate but does not erase them entirely. 18

Which Mother intervenes on your behalf? What silent, non-judgmental lesson do they impart as they mitigate the consequence?

### How to Use This Environment

As a Co-DM, here's my advice for running the Lazaretto:

- **Emphasize the Atmosphere:** Describe the Lazaretto as serene but unnerving. It's clean and quiet, but the veiled, indistinguishable Mothers 19, the living ferry20, and the strange flora and fauna of the Fold make it feel alien. The conflict here shouldn't be a typical dungeon crawl, but a psychological and philosophical one.
    
- **Lean into Social and Exploration:** The primary challenge should be understanding the Mothers and their strange ways. Use the Mothers as Social adversaries with motives like "assess the patient's true need" or "gently guide toward a painful truth." A failed Presence roll might not result in a fight, but in being politely and firmly denied access to a certain area until the PC has "properly prepared their mind."
    
- **The "Controlled Eclosion" as a Centerpiece:** This feature is a perfect opportunity for a character-focused side quest within the location itself. Work with the player beforehand to identify a scar or backstory element they'd like to explore. The "reverie" can be a series of short, surreal scenes where the player's choices directly influence their rolls to advance the countdown.
    
- **Adversaries as Metaphors:** If combat does break out, it should be strange. The "Manifestations of Trauma" shouldn't just be monsters to be killed; they should be physical embodiments of a PC's or another patient's fears. Perhaps defeating one requires a PC to speak a comforting truth rather than just dealing damage.
    

This environment is designed to give your players a memorable and unique experience that challenges them to think about their characters in new ways, perfectly mirroring the function of the Mothers Merciful in their own universe. Good luck!
