A quiet, rustic village populated by bakers, weavers, farmers, and cobblers, with no real warriors or weapons. The villagers are more concerned with the yearly [[Stewpot Festival]] than with any dangers beyond their borders, thanks to the protection of [[The Lure]]. Recently, fear has gripped the village after an attack on [[Mayor Doug Dougle]], causing a halt to hunting and foraging in the nearby Plover Woods. Food is becoming precious. The PCs, as villagers, are the unlikely heroes stepping up to save their home.
