[[Goblin]] [[Sorcerer]] ([[Elemental Origin]])

Her village was accidentally burned down when she lost control of her powers. One day all was good and then she got a bit too excited and suddenly burst into flame... and burned her woodland home to the ground. This resulted in a lot of shame and she no longer felt comfortable at home with the determination to learn about her new powers (and how to control them) so she can return to her village in control.
