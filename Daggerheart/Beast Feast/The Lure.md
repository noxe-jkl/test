A powerful orb of concentrated magic created by [[Hylaeus the Forest Mage]]. It resides deep within the [[Plover Caves]] (likely in the [[Hadral]] layer) and its purpose is to draw dangerous creatures from the surrounding region into the caverns, thus protecting [[Elmore]]. Its power is now fading. Key campaign questions include:

- How was it made?
    
- What was sacrificed to create it?
    
- Why has its power started to fade?
    
- What must the PCs do to reactivate it?
