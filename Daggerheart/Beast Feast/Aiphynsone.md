**Domains:** Knowledge and Shadow

**Patronage:** Aiphysnone is the patron of those who seek to understand the world not by conquering it, but by listening to it. Its followers are scholars, mystics, and tenders of secrets. The deity's primary servants in the Plover Caves are the **Secret-Keepers**.

**Core Philosophy: The Sheltering Veil**

Aiphysnone teaches that knowledge is a living, fragile thing, intrinsically woven into the fabric of the world. It must be protected, nurtured, and kept from those who would exploit it for mere profit or power. The domain of Shadow is not a tool for aggression, but a sacred veil to conceal and protect these delicate truths from the brutish and the ignorant. Followers of Aiphysnone do not seek to claim and catalog, but to guard and understand.

- **The Secret-Keepers:** As the faithful followers of Aiphysnone, the Secret-Keepers are the tenders of the Plover Caves' deepest secrets. They believe the caves are a living entity and that locations like the "Reflection of the Stars" are sacred heart-nodes essential for the magic that protects Elmore. The rhythmic chanting Zai may hear is a rite of communion and warding, meant to soothe the cave's spirit. Their symbol, the eye shedding a single tear, represents the burden of protecting wisdom that others would destroy.
    

### The True Conflict: Faith vs. Industry

The struggle within the Plover Caves is not a schism, but a war between two opposing worldviews, likely backed by rival powers.

- **The Secret-Keepers (Faith):** They fight to protect the sacred, magical ecosystem of the caves to ensure the well-being of the entire region, including the village of Elmore above. They are defending their holy sites and their patron's domain.
    
- **The Open Hand (Industry):** This faction views the caves as a resource to be tamed, stripped, and controlled for material gain and security. Their talk of a "Patron" now points to a different, rival entity—one that perhaps values strength, conquest, or the subjugation of nature. Their goal is to eliminate the "saboteurs" who prevent their total control over the caves' bounty.
    

### Zai's Crisis of Loyalty

This new understanding places Zaivryx Zhyer in an incredibly difficult position, which will come to a head in Session 3.

- **Unwitting Betrayal:** Zai, a warlock of Aiphysnone, has been hired and guided by the Open Hand—the very enemies of their patron's faithful. The map from Lt. Dhol is not just a lie; it is a tool of desecration, leading Zai to trespass on their own sacred ground.
    
- **A Test of Allegiance:** When the Secret-Keeper acolyte, Kaelen, confronts Zai, the question "Why would a follower of the Shadowed Truth walk the path of the Clenched Fist?" is not a theological query. It is a direct accusation of treason.
    
- **The Choice:** Zai's conflict is no longer about interpreting their patron's will, but about choosing their loyalty. Do they honor the contract made with the Open Hand, or do they stand with the beleaguered followers of their own patron to defend a sacred site from invaders? The arrival of Sergeant Valerius and his patrol will force this choice into the open, with blades drawn and lives on the line.
