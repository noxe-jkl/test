The Open Hand is a well-established guild of hunters and trailblazers operating out of Plover. They have taken it upon themselves to chart and secure safe hunting paths within the dangerous Plover Caves. Their stated goal is to ensure a reliable flow of food and resources from the caves to support the town. The guild is organized with a clear hierarchy, including officers like Lieutenant Guag Dhol, though it's leader is known only as "Patron."

Currently, they are in an aggressive territorial conflict with a mysterious faction known as the Secret-Keepers, a group they claim has been tormenting their hunters on the paths. The Open Hand has tasked the party with driving off any trespassers and using lethal force against any Secret-Keepers found along their routes. To many in Elmore, they are seen as a force for order and prosperity, taming the wild dangers that lie beneath the town.
