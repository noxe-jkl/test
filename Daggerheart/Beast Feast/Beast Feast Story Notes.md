### Campaign Overview

This campaign follows a group of adventurers who answer the call from the village of Elmore to reactivate a magical Lure deep within the Plover Caves. The campaign is structured as a series of unfolding arcs, moving from a localized threat to a much larger, more complex problem. The initial plea from Elmore serves as the inciting incident, but as the party ventures deeper, they will uncover the true reason the Lure is failing and confront a threat that extends far beyond the small village.

### Arc 1: The Call of the Caves (Levels 1-4)

This initial arc focuses on the party's entry into the [[Plover Caves]] and their first steps toward understanding the scale of the problem. 3It serves as the introduction to the larger conflict. 4

- **A-Plot: Answer the Call.** The primary goal is to respond to [[Elmore]]'s plea. The party must travel from the village into the upper regions of the Plover Caves, guided by the promise of reactivating [[The Lure]] to save the town. This involves surviving the newly dangerous Plover Woods and navigating the initial, treacherous descent.
    
- **B-Plot: The Cave Dwellers.** The party discovers that the caves are not uninhabited. They encounter the communal society of people living and working within the caverns. 6 These residents are not panicked by the Lure's fading; instead, they are dealing with a different problem: a new, aggressive faction of cave dwellers, led by a mysterious figure, who are trying to seize control of key resources and territories. This B-plot will become the A-plot of the next arc. 7
    
- **C-Plot: Hylaeus's Legacy.** The party finds weathered journals and cryptic carvings left behind by Hylaeus the Forest Mage. 8 These clues hint that the Lure is more than a simple magical ward; it is a complex spell tied to a powerful magical entity bound deep within the caves. This seeds the main conflict of the final arc. 9
    

**Three-Act Structure:** 101010

- **Act One: Collision.** The party arrives in Elmore to find a frightened populace and the injured Mayor Doug Dougle. 11 They are briefed on the mission: enter the Plover Caves and reactivate the Lure. Their first task is to follow the piping plovers through the dangerous woods to find a cave entrance, culminating in an encounter with one of the newly escaped creatures. This gives them a taste of the conflict to come. 12
    
- **Act Two: Complications.** Deeper inside, the party finds the cave system is vast and complex, with its own ecosystem of residents, camps, and even small restaurants called Berry's. 13 They learn that just reaching the Lure is a major journey. They must contend with rival adventurers, hunt for their own food, and navigate the social dynamics of the cave-dwelling communities, some of whom see the Lure's failure as an opportunity, not a crisis. They also have their first run-in with the aggressive faction mentioned in the B-plot.
    
- **Act Three: Climax.** The party reaches a significant landmark deep in the caves—a large, established cave-dweller settlement under siege by the hostile faction. 14 To proceed deeper toward the Lure, they must help defend the settlement. In doing so, they learn from the settlement's elder that the Lure isn't just fading; its energy is being actively siphoned away. The arc ends with the party having secured a safe path forward and armed with the troubling knowledge that a simple "reactivation" may not be possible. 15This revelation creates the dramatic questions that drive them into the next arc. 16
    

### Arc 2: The Siphoning (Levels 5-7)

The focus shifts from a simple quest to a complex investigation. The party must now identify who is draining the Lure's power and why. 17

- **A-Plot: The Antagonist Revealed.** The party hunts for the faction that is siphoning the Lure's power. This investigation leads them through diverse and dangerous biomes within the caves. They discover this faction, known as the "Hollowed," is led by a former student of Hylaeus who believes the Lure is a cruel prison for the entity bound within it. The leader's goal is not to destroy Elmore but to shatter the Lure to free this entity, regardless of the consequences.
    
- **B-Plot: The Bound Entity.** As the party gets closer to the Lure, they begin to feel its influence. They experience strange visions and whispers—the voice of the magical entity bound by Hylaeus's spell. It shows them glimpses of the past and the immense power it wields. The party must decide if this entity is a prisoner that deserves freedom or a world-ending threat that must remain contained. 18This B-plot will become the central conflict of the final arc. 19
    
- **C-Plot: The Caves Weep.** The siphoning of the Lure's magic is causing the cave system itself to become unstable. 20 Tunnels collapse, strange magical phenomena manifest, and the very flora and fauna are becoming twisted and corrupted. This foreshadows the environmental catastrophe that will occur if the Lure is destroyed.
    

**Three-Act Structure:** 21

- **Act One: Introduction.** The party follows leads from the settlement they saved. They track the Hollowed through a series of plundered outposts and corrupted cave regions. They find evidence of strange rituals and arcane machinery being used to drain magical energy, confirming the elder's story.
    
- **Act Two: Trials and Tribulations.** The journey becomes more personal as the party learns more about the leader of the Hollowed and their connection to Hylaeus. 22They face tough choices, such as whether to destroy the siphoning machinery, which could cause a backlash of unpredictable magic, or to pursue the leader directly. 23 They also contend with the increasingly powerful whispers from the bound entity, which may try to sway them to its side.
    
- **Act Three: Climax.** The party confronts the leader of the Hollowed at a major siphoning nexus. 24The antagonist reveals their full motivation and connection to Hylaeus, perhaps through a dramatic twist (e.g., they are a descendant, or a magically preserved apprentice). 25 The party must fight the leader and their lieutenants. While they succeed in stopping the siphoning at this location, the leader escapes, but not before initiating a final, desperate gambit to shatter the Lure completely, launching the campaign into its final act. 26
    

### Arc 3: The Heart of the Matter (Levels 8-10)

This is the finale. 27The party must race against time to reach the Lure's core and decide the fate of Elmore, the Plover Caves, and potentially the entire region. 28

- **A-Plot: The Fate of the Lure.** The central plot is the final confrontation at the heart of the Plover Caves. 29 The Hollowed leader is trying to perform a ritual to shatter the Lure. The magical instability is now critical, causing widespread cave-ins and unleashing the most powerful creatures from the deepest caverns. The party must navigate this chaotic environment to intervene.
    
- **B-Plot: The World Outside.** The consequences are no longer contained. Reports from the surface (perhaps via returning adventurers or magical communication) describe ecological disasters spreading from the Plover Woods. The magical imbalance is affecting the weather, the wildlife, and the land itself. The fate of Elmore is now tied to the fate of the wider world. 30
    
- **C-Plot (Resolved): Hylaeus's Choice.** The party uncovers the final piece of Hylaeus's story. They learn why she created the Lure—not just to protect Elmore, but to contain the immensely powerful and chaotic entity she accidentally summoned. The Lure was her life's work and a terrible burden. This context frames the party's final decision.
    

**Three-Act Structure:** 31

- **Act One: Introduction.** The party follows the path of destruction left by the escaping antagonist. The Plover Caves are in chaos. They must overcome environmental hazards and the most dangerous creatures yet as they descend into the deepest, previously unreachable sections of the cave system.
    
- **Act Two: Complications.** The party finally reaches the chamber of the Lure, a massive, pulsating crystal containing a swirling vortex of raw magic—the bound entity. Here, they face the final guardians Hylaeus left behind. They also must confront the bound entity itself, which pleads its case, promising them power or knowledge in exchange for freedom. They also face a moral complication: they find evidence that Hylaeus herself was searching for a way to safely release or neutralize the entity, but ran out of time. 32
    
- **Act Three: Finale.** The final showdown with the leader of the Hollowed occurs on the precipice of the Lure itself. 33This should be a climactic and dangerous encounter. 34 At its conclusion, the party is left with a choice that will define the campaign's ending:
    
    - **Reignite the Lure:** They can repair the damage and strengthen the prison, saving Elmore and the surrounding region but condemning the entity to eternal imprisonment.
        
    - **Destroy the Lure:** They can side with the antagonist's goal and shatter the Lure, freeing the entity and unleashing unpredictable power upon the world, gambling that they can control or defeat it.
        
    - **Find a Third Way:** Drawing on Hylaeus's final research, they may attempt a difficult ritual to neutralize the entity or banish it to another plane, a risky option that offers a chance at redemption and resolution without destruction.
        

This final choice provides a satisfying conclusion to the campaign, directly resolving the central conflict that has been building since the first session. 35
