[[Wanderborne]] [[Faerie]] [[Guardian]] ([[Stalwart]])

He works as a butcher for the village of Elmore. He uses a very large and long greatsword (think like Guts from Berserk). He's 2 feet tall, strangely resilient for his thin build. He has a protector mindset, is very strong, but in his mind he doesn't think so d/t a difficult experience he had with his late wife. He wasn't strong enough to lift a very large boulder away from his wife (he was very close though), and in a panic, he had to let go and in a sense push himself away from the boulder as not to crush himself, killing his wife. He feels incredible remorse for it. However at this stage of his life, he's silently and slowly learning that mistakes happen and life goes on.
