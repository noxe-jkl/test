- Gods are around
- 8 of them, each with a curse on the land
	- Veythar (Guilded Maw)
		- Domain: Prosperity and Endless Heart
		- All grain rots and meats go bad faster
	- Nyxthene (Veilmother of Mournlight) (shrouded figure with a mask weeping black ichor)
		- Domain: Moonlight and Dreams
		- Curse: People stuck outside of Pyres were left to wander in labrynths of shadow forever, becoming bones on the road
	- Orrivash (Crown of Ash) 
		- Domain: Kingship and Law
		- Curse: Crowns turn to embers on their heads and rulers burn alive on their first night of reign
	- Zorathun (Harbinger of the Last Bell) (Cloaked figure with a open shadow between the cloak with a endlessly tolling bell inside)
		- Domain: Death's Passage
		- Curse (only one still lingering): No soul passes cleanly into death, linger until the bones are dust
	- Eryssael (Bloom that Devours) (woman with a multitude of petals with long hair dripping crimson sap)
		- Domain: Spring and Beauty
		- Curse: Flowers bloomed overnight, growing thorns the size of spears
	- Thoryn-Kael (The Storm Tyrant)
		- Domain: Sea and Sky
		- Curse: Rain fell as brine leading the ocean to swallow coastal towns
	- Morrakel (Keeper of the Crimson Ledger)
		- Domain: Bargain of Fate
	- Iskorr (The Emberscribe)
		- Domain: Knowledge
		- Curse: All books outside of the pyres burst into flame, leaving half-truths for the future of man
- There was a tree that had been destroyed, and all the pieces of the tree where they were when the curses fell brought the pyres into being. 
- King Othedias did not like Druids and where they came from, so he enslaved them.
