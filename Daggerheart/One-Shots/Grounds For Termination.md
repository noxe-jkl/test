### Custom Mechanic: The Buzz Track

This campaign uses a **Consequence Countdown (d6)** called the **Buzz Track** to represent the rising profile of the team's disastrous investigation.
- **Start:** Set a **5-step** countdown die.
- **Tick Down:** Whenever the party makes a roll with **Fear**, or the GM calls for it due to excessive property damage, breaking corporate policy, or public attention (e.g., a massive explosion).
- **Trigger (0):** If the Buzz Track reaches **0** before the confrontation ends, Manager Brenda learns the full scope of the team's antics. This locks in the **Partial Success** or **Total Failure** Debrief, regardless of the coffee machine's state.

## Intro:
- **The Scene:** The team is crammed into the mirrored elevator of the Angel City Dispatch office. It’s 9:58 AM.
    
- **The Hook:** The elevator music is a soulless synth version of "Holding Out for a Hero." Everyone knows Brenda called this emergency meeting at 10:00 AM sharp.
    
- **The Prompt:** Ask each player to describe their character as they stand in this awkward silence.
    - _Who is frantically checking their reflection to look presentable?_
    - _Who is holding a cheap, inferior coffee cup because the good machine is broken?_
    - _Who is already sweating because they know they’re in trouble?_

- **Transition:** As the doors ding open on the 14th floor, they see the chaos: weeping interns, managers running with clipboards, and Brenda already pacing in her glass office. This leads directly into **Scene 1: The Briefing**.

### **Scene 1: The Briefing (A.K.A. The Berating)**

Location: Manager Brenda's glass-walled office.

Time: 10:00 AM.

Goal: Establish the high stakes (the audit) and the urgent deadline (3:00 PM).

[[Manager Brenda]] points a trembling finger at the team.

> "Stop. Don’t speak. If you speak, you are wasting oxygen that I need to keep from screaming.
> Look at the clock. It is 10:00 AM. At 3:00 PM, three auditors from Corporate are walking through those doors to assess 'Regional Morale & Amenities.' The cornerstone of our amenity score—the Omni-Java 9000, a fifty-thousand-dollar piece of machinery that creates the only joy I have in this miserable existence—is gone.
> Security says it was taken at 3:00 AM. I don't care who took it. I don't care if a demon from the Nether-Realm needs a darker roast. I care that it is back, plugged in, and brewing a caramel macchiato before the auditors sit down.
> And listen to me closely: Do not throw cars. Do not blow up a city block. If I see _one_ headline about 'Super-Vandalism,' I will personally process your transfer paperwork to the Fresno branch. Fix it. Quietly. Now get out."

**Mechanics: Surviving Brenda (Difficulty 14)**

- **Action Roll (Social Check):** If a PC attempts to get more information (e.g., "Was anyone else questioned?"), call for a **Presence Roll (14)**.

### **Scene 3: Following the Lead (A.K.A. Harassing HR)**

Location: 10th Floor, [[Human Resources]]. Koboroku

Environment: Difficulty 15 (The Fortress of Bureaucracy).

Goal: Get Gary's last-known address from his employee file.

_Note: Any check not listed as a "Subtle Approach" is rolled with **Disadvantage** due to bureaucratic red tape._

**Mechanics: Approaches**

- **The Direct Approach:** Attempting to convince the HR rep this is "time-sensitive."
    
    - **Presence Roll (15)** with Disadvantage.
        
    - **Failure:** HR Rep calls their supervisor (an Ally of Brenda's). **GM Move:** The party has disadvantage on _all_ future Social Checks in the office until cleared.
        
- **The Sneaky Approach (Distraction):** Hack the terminal while someone distracts the rep.
    
    - **Group Action:** Leader makes a **Finesse Roll (13)**; Ally makes a **Presence Roll (13)**.
        
    - **Result:** Leader gains +/- 1 to their roll per ally's success/failure. **Success:** Access granted.
        
- **The "Hero" Approach:** Use a power (e.g., Mind Reading, Charm).
    
    - **Power Check:** Bard _Codex/Grace_ or Rogue _Midnight/Grace_ spellcast.
        
    - **Cost:** Mark **1 Stress** (Spell Cost) or spend **1 Hope** (Ability Cost).
        
    - **Consequence:** If the power use is loud/obvious, **Tick the Buzz Track**.
        
- **The "Brenda" Approach:** Threaten that Brenda will be unhappy.
    
    - **Presence Roll (16)**.
        
    - **Success w/ Hope:** HR panics and hands over the address immediately.
        
    - **Success w/ Fear:** HR hands over the address but calls Brenda to complain. **Tick the Buzz Track**.
        

**Result:** The team gets the address: "The Valley Glen" Apartments.

### **Scene 4: The Lair (A.K.A. Apartment 2B)**

Location: "The Valley Glen" Apartments.

Goal: Get inside without causing a scene.

The apartment has its blinds drawn, a faint _thump-thump_ sound inside, and the door is warm to the touch.

**Mechanics: Entry**

- **Locked Door:** **Finesse Roll (13)** (Lockpick) or **Strength Roll (16)** (Force).
    
    - **Failed Force Check:** The door flies off its hinges. **Tick the Buzz Track**.
        
- **Car Battery Doorbell:** The doorbell is wired to a car battery.
    
    - **Knowledge Roll (14):** **Failure** results in the player marking **1 Stress** from a minor electrical shock.
        

**Inside:** Gary sits on a "throne" made of hacked appliances, powered by the Omni-Java 9000. Vacuum cleaners patrol like dogs; toasters line the counters.

> "Shhh... shhh, don't listen to them, my beauty. They don't understand your grind settings. They treat you like a _tool_! 'Make me a latte, make me a dark roast.' But did they ever ask _you_ what _you_ wanted to brew? No!
_(He spins around to face the players)_
> Stay back! You’re just like Brenda! You think small. You see a coffee machine; I see a conductor of energy! I see the perfect fusion of bean and electron! When I worked there, they mocked me. They fired me just because I gave the printer a soul! But the Omni-Java... she speaks to me. We are going to build a new world! A world of perfect, high-pressure extraction! And if you try to take her... well, my toaster friends here are set to 'Bagel,' and they are feeling _very_ spicy."

### **Scene 5: The "Confrontation" (A.K.A. De-escalation)**

Location: Gary's Apartment. Time: 2:15 PM.

Goal: Subdue [[Gary Blevins]] and retrieve the Omni-Java 9000 (Tier 1 Artifact) intact.

**The Opposition: Gary's Appliance Army**

- **Gary:** Difficulty 13.
    
- **12 [[Vacuum Minion]]:** Difficulty 9.
    
- **[[Toaster Horde]]:** Difficulty 10.    

**Mechanics: Ways to Win**

- **Talk Him Down:** **Presence Roll (16)** (Persuasion/Empathy). Convince Gary he's a genius who deserves better. **Success:** Gary stands down and yields the machine.
    
- **Subdue Him (Non-Lethal):** **Strength or Agility Roll (14)**. **Success:** Gary is knocked unconscious; appliances stop.
    
- **Shut Him Down (Hacking):** **Knowledge Roll (18)**. Create a feedback loop. **Success:** All appliances shut down safely. **Failure:** **Tick the Buzz Track**.
    
- **Destroy Appliances:** Heroes can attack minions (defeated on any damage). **Warning:** Destroying an appliance (e.g., vaporizing a vacuum) is a policy-breaking offense. **Tick the Buzz Track** for every destroyed appliance.
