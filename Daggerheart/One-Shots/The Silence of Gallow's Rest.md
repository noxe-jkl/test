## ADVENTURE OVERVIEW

A century has passed since the God-King Othedias betrayed the Pantheon, leaving the Halcyon Domain eternally cursed. In this broken world, the gray ash of the Ashfell Mountains falls like a heavy shroud. Small communities survive only by the grace of Sacred Pyres. The PCs are survivors sent from the Celsian Athenaeum to deliver a Blessed Branch to Gallow’s Rest—a settlement whose light has flickered and died.

## ACT ONE: THE ATHENAEUM’S BURDEN

**The Setting:** The Celsian Athenaeum, a fortress of flickering candles and cold stone. **The Triangle:** Before starting, identify two PCs for specific ties to **Archivist Nycoril**:

1. **The Protégé:** Nycoril was your mentor. You know the weight of his expectations.
    
2. **The Exile:** Nycoril personally signed your exile papers years ago for a "heresy" you didn't commit.
    

**Narrative Description:**

> "The Celsian Athenaeum stands as a defiant candle against the overwhelming night. Outside, the heavy gray ash from the Ashfell Mountains falls in a rhythmic, deathly hush, burying the ruins of Amber Reach. Inside, Archivist Nycoril—a man who has spent a century recording the world’s end—is wrapping a silver-threaded bundle.
> 
> He secures the knot, the metallic thread catching the light. He looks at (The Protégé) with a mixture of pride and profound guilt, then shifts his gaze to (The Exile). 'The signals from Gallow’s Rest have ceased,' Nycoril whispers. 'If this fire fails, the Soul Blight will claim every life in that valley. You are the only survivors fast enough to reach them before the ash swallows the path entirely.'"

**The Tension:** Ask (The Protégé): _"Nycoril is entrusting you with the only relic that can save these people. Does this feel like a final test, or a suicide mission he’s too old to take himself?"_ Then ask (The Exile): _"Nycoril is the reason you lost everything. Why are you willing to risk your life for his mission now?"_

> **LORE MODULE: AMBER REACH & THE ATHENAEUM** **Success with Hope:** You recall that Amber Reach was once the glorious capital of the Halcyon Domain, filled with gothic towers and fortresses. The Celsian Athenaeum, originally a grand college and library, is one of the few places where hundreds of Enduring still work together to reclaim the city from the spirits and killers that haunt the ruins. **Success with Fear:** You remember that the Ashcall Quarter, where you currently stand, is one of the only "safe" zones left. Reclaiming the city is a slow, bloody process. For every street the Celsians take, two more are lost to the Damask Queens or the Umbra. The "reclamation" Nycoril speaks of often feels less like a crusade and more like a desperate, losing siege.

**TRANSITION: DEPARTURE**

> "The massive iron gates of the Athenaeum groan open, revealing a world of monochromatic gray. As you step out, the heavy silence of the waste swallows the sound of the city. You board the carriage, the silver-wrapped Branch tucked safely between you, and begin the long descent into the ash-fields."

## ACT TWO: THE ASH-CHOKED PATH

**The Setting:** The winding, desolate road through the Halcyon Outskirts.

**Narrative Description:**

> "The journey south is a grueling crawl through a world that has forgotten the sun. The wind howls off the Ashfell peaks, carrying a grit that tastes of ancient fire. You come across a derailed scout cart, half-buried in a drift of ash. There are no tracks leading away. A single, iron-cold fragment of a heavy blade is pinned to the driver’s seat. The silence here is heavy—not the absence of sound, but a physical weight."

**Foreshadowing Velk:** A survivor named **Tallis** is huddled beneath the cart. He mutters: _"The armored man... he didn't speak. He just walked through the fire and took the sparks. He carried a blade as wide as a coffin."_

**Pacing Move (The Group Action):** Navigate the ash drifts. Difficulty: 13.

- **Success:** They reach the destination efficiently.
    
- **Success with Fear:** They arrive, but the journey was taxing. Each PC marks **1 Stress** as the ash clogs their lungs.
    

> **LORE MODULE: THE SOUL BLIGHT** **Success with Hope:** You know that the Soul Blight is a mark upon every mortal soul in the Halcyon Domain. It ensures that upon death, the body rises as a corrupted revenant. However, those who live within the light of a Sacred Pyre believe that faith and grit can preserve the spirit's dignity until the body is properly laid to rest. **Success with Fear:** You recall the darker truth: if a risen body is destroyed, the soul doesn't move on—it withdraws into the Umbra, a dark ethereal mass. There, it seeks out others to haunt or merge with wandering beasts, contorting them into the nightmarish monsters that stalk the wastes. Even your death serves only to strengthen the storm.

**TRANSITION: THE BARREN WASTES**

> "Leaving the wreckage behind, the road levels out into a desolate stretch of cracked earth and waist-high ash drifts. The wind dies down here, replaced by an unsettling stillness. The ground beneath the carriage wheels feels soft and unstable, as if the landscape itself is waiting to shift beneath you."

## ACT THREE: THE STONY AMBUSH (COMBAT 1)

**The Setting:** A desolate, ash-heavy flatland of cracked stone.

**Narrative Description:**

> "As the carriage slows in the deep ash, the ground begins to vibrate. Without warning, the stony ground seems to bleed shadows. Figures draped in soot-stained rags emerge not from behind cover, but directly from the cracked earth and ash-drifts, as if the landscape itself is giving birth to killers. Leading them is an Ash-Cloaked Marauder. They raise a jagged, smoldering blade. 'Why prolong the inevitable?' they rasp. 'Give the Branch to the fire, and let the darkness take you peacefully.'"

**Adversaries (Tier 1):**

- **Ash-Cloaked Marauder** (Tier 1 Standard)
    
- **Soot-Stained Sentries** (Tier 1 Minions - 1 per PC)
    

**The Refraction Rival:** The Marauder focuses on their "mirror" PC.

- **Dialogue:** _"I used to believe in Nycoril's lies. But the ash is the only truth left. It covers the saint and the sinner just the same."_
    

> **LORE MODULE: THE CONGREGATION & THE DAMASK QUEENS** **Success with Hope:** You recognize the insignia of the Damask Queens—a rising faction of scoundrels and raiders from the debris-strewn Cinnabar Quarter of Amber Reach. While they are ruthless, they are mortals who value resources, and their expansion suggests they are building a society of their own, however brutal it may be. **Success with Fear:** You know the Queens are hedonic and ruthless. Their crossings with the Celsians have grown increasingly violent. The fact that their "agents" are now emerging from the stony earth this far from the city suggests they are using forbidden Umbra-touched rituals to move through the domain undetected.

**TRANSITION: THE FINAL STRETCH**

> "With the marauders dissolving back into the soot and wind, you push the horses toward Gallow's Rest. The gray ash begins to turn a sickly, bruised purple as you approach the settlement. The sun is setting, and the true darkness of the Umbra is beginning to bleed into the world."

## ACT FOUR: THE HOLLOW HEARTH

**The Setting:** The gates of Gallow’s Rest.

**Narrative Description:**

> "Gallow’s Rest is a ghost of a town. The gates swing on rusted hinges, and the central courtyard—once a place of warmth—is now a mound of cold, gray soot. There are no screams, only a low, rhythmic thrumming emanating from a massive cave mouth on the hill. Pyrekeeper Vora meets you at the gate, her skin the color of slate."

**Vora's Dialogue:**

> "Vora clutches a cold iron lantern, her eyes fixed on the cavern. 'You're too late for the village, but perhaps just in time for their souls,' she rasps. 'The armored sentinel came three days ago. He didn't come to pillage; he came to silence. He walked straight to the Sacred Pyre, and with one breath, he inhaled the flame. When the light died, he didn't strike my people down. He hummed a song—a deep, rhythmic vibration that resonated in our very bones.
> 
> My neighbors, my friends... they followed him like sleepwalkers. Hundreds of them, walking hand-in-hand into the sinkhole. I only stayed because the Pyre’s last ember held my heart in place. I am the tethered witness. That cave leads to the Fane—a natural cathedral that once served as a sanctuary for the Old World's secrets. Now, it is a tomb where the Sentinel keeps his vigil over my stolen people.'"

**The Stakes:** If any NPC (or PC) dies, the GM can spend **1 Fear** to raise them as a **Shambling Zombie**.

> **LORE MODULE: SACRED PYRES & OKROS** **Success with Hope:** You remember the village of Okros in the mountain-crowned region of the Idol Hollows. For over fifty years, its massive walls of melted shields have protected a Sacred Pyre whose flames climb above the tallest ramparts. It stands as a burning symbol of strength and safety, proving the Enduring can thrive even in the swampy flood plains. **Success with Fear:** You've heard that while Okros stands, many other settlements are failing. The "clouded skies" are growing darker with each passing generation. As the oracles imagine great alien minds glimpsing into this world from the cosmic veil, the light of the Pyres seems to be the only thing keeping those eyes from focusing entirely on the Halcyon Domain.

**TRANSITION: INTO THE EARTH**

> "Vora leads you to the edge of the massive sinkhole. 'The Fane isn't just a building anymore,' she says. 'The seismic events that birthed the Shalk Chasm tore the earth open here, too. The inner sanctum is now a cathedral of stone, deep beneath our feet. If you hear the thrumming, do not listen. Keep your eyes on the Branch.' You descend the damp, lightless steps into the yawning mouth of the cavern."

## ACT FIVE: THE TRIALS OF THE CAVERN (THE PUZZLE)

**The Setting:** A massive, echoing subterranean cavern with stalactites that look like frozen gothic arches.

**Narrative Description:**

> "The air in the cavern is freezing and smells of ancient dust. To reach the deepest sanctum, you must pass through a natural bridge of stone lined with cold iron braziers. This massive expanse of long-buried structures was uncovered by the same forces that created the Shalk Chasm. The air vibrates with a low, grinding sound—the sound of a greatblade being dragged across a stone floor far below."

**Trial 1: The Brazier of Mercy**

- **The Choice:** Spend **1 Hope** to comfort the spirit or roll **Presence (DC 13)**. Failure: The spirit shrieks (1 Stress to everyone).
    yp

**Trial 2: The Brazier of Resolve**

- **The Choice:** Walk through the ash-glass. Roll **Instinct (DC 14)**. On failure, choose: mark **1d4 Physical Damage** or mark **1 Stress**.
    

**Trial 3: The Brazier of Sacrifice**

- **The Choice:** A PC must mark **1 Hit Point** or narratively sacrifice a **valued memory**.
    

> **LORE MODULE: THE SHALK CHASM & ANCIENT VESTIGES** **Success with Hope:** You recognize these ruins as vestiges from _before_ the Old World. Brave Enduring have begun investigating the mysteries below the Shalk Chasm, hoping to recover ancient relics and tomes that might rebuke the soul blight or even commune with the absent gods. **Success with Fear:** You recall that the seismic event that birthed the Shalk Chasm also uncovered "banes" alongside the boons. Some of these subterranean catacombs weren't meant to be found; they contain remnants of God-King Othedias's clandestine machinations—secrets so dangerous they once enraged the Pantheon into enacting the Apostasy.

**TRANSITION: THE THRESHOLD**

> "As the third brazier ignites, the darkness at the end of the cavern retreats. The grinding sound stops. You step into a vast, hollow chamber where the ceiling is lost to shadow, and standing before a cold obsidian altar is a massive, armored figure draped in tattered mourning veils. This is Velk."

## ACT SIX: THE SENTINEL'S VIGIL (COMBAT 2)

**The Setting:** The cavern's heart, centered around a cold obsidian altar.

**Narrative Description:**

> "Velk stands motionless, his massive greatblade buried point-first in the cold ash of the altar. His armor is caked in centuries of dust, and his face is hidden behind a visor of black iron. He does not breathe. As you enter, he slowly raises the blade, the metal groaning. 'The light is a burden,' a voice rasps from within the helm, like stones grinding together. 'I will keep you from the pain of hope.'"

**Boss Mechanics:**

- **Velk (Tier 1 Solo)**
    
- **The Hunt is On:** Velk marks a "Prey." Attacks against them deal +2 damage.
    
- **Greatblade Sweep:** Spend a Fear to strike all targets within Melee range.
    

**The Big Swing:** A PC can surge forward to ignite the Branch instantly, but must sacrifice their connection to the light (permanent scar) or their life.

> **LORE MODULE: OTHEDIAS & THE GRAND ORDINANTS** **Success with Hope:** You know that Othedias was chosen by the Veiled Gods to be the bridge to divinity. He ruled a prosperous epoch before his fall. Some hermetic societies claim to hold tomes of his "great tragedy," believing his story contains a prophecy that could lead to the realm's salvation. **Success with Fear:** You remember the true horror: Othedias conspired with his Grand Ordinants and Aetherlords to shift the balance of celestial power. His blasphemy was so great it provoked cataclysmic retribution, ending the Old World and leaving the Halcyon Domain to wither under the Umbra. Standing before Velk, you are literally fighting a ghost of the man who destroyed the world.

## EPILOGUE: THE FIRST HOUR

**The Victory:** > "As Velk is finally laid to rest, his armor crumbling into rust and ash, you plunge the Blessed Branch into the heart of the altar. For a heartbeat, there is total silence. Then, a pillar of pure, blinding white radiance erupts, punching a hole through the ash-clouds of the Ashfell Mountains."
