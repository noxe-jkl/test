### Intro
Dust hangs in the air long before you see the town.

The road narrows as it winds into the Caradondo Mountains, pressed tight between jagged stone that rises like broken teeth on either side. The wind howls through the pass in long, hollow cries… and beneath it, something else—something deeper. A low, distant rumble.

As you round the final bend, Miquera comes into view.

A small settlement, little more than a single street carved into the mountain’s throat. Timber buildings lean into one another for support. A squat stone church stands at the far end, its bell tower reaching stubbornly skyward.

And the town is in chaos.

Smoke coils upward in thick black columns. Flames lick hungrily at a collapsing saloon. People run in every direction—shouting, screaming, hauling buckets, dragging the injured. The ground shudders beneath your feet, a violent tremor that sends loose stones clattering down the mountainside.

A crack splits the air like thunder.

High above, part of the church spire tilts dangerously, swaying as if it might tear free at any moment. Somewhere nearby, someone screams for help—then another voice, and another.

This is no accident.

Something is happening here.

And you have arrived just in time to be part of it.

### **Read-Aloud: Ikeri Comes Into View**

The world grows… quiet.

Not silent—but wrong. The wind dies. The fires seem to hesitate. Even the mountains hold their breath.

Then, from deep within the pass, comes a sound.

A long… low… agonized moan.

It echoes through the stone like something alive is buried inside the mountains themselves.

The ground trembles again—stronger this time. Pebbles dance. Cracks spider across the earth. Somewhere behind you, a building collapses with a dull roar.

And then you see it.

At first, it’s just movement—a shifting shape between the cliffs. Something impossibly large forcing its way through a gap too small to contain it.

Stone breaks.

Dust explodes outward.

And Ikeri emerges.

A colossal form, hunched and terrible—like a great ape carved from ruin and grief. Its head ends in a massive, cruel beak, scraping against the rock as it drags itself forward. Upon its back rests a slab of earth and stone—a mesa grown from its flesh, as though the land itself has fused with its body.

Its limbs move with awful weight, each step cracking the ground.

And it is not roaring in rage.

It is _moaning_.

A sound of endless pain. Of something ancient… broken… and unable to die.

It turns, slowly, toward Miquera.

Toward you.

And begins to advance.
