[[Orderborne]] [[Infernis]] **Class:** [[Guardian]] (Vengeance)

### Background

Eldric is the true-born heir of the noble [[Valemont]] family of [[Voldaen]], though he has been effectively disinherited. His grandfather, [[Count Allistar Valemont]], seized power through the treachery and murder of [[Duke Bran Hartwell]]. Eldric's mother, [[Matilda Hartwell]], died during his birth. Because of his [[Infernis]] appearance, [[Hilltop]] priests claimed he was a physical manifestation of his family's sins.

His father, [[Toren Valemont]], disinherited him to appease the nobility and the [[Hilltop]] priesthood, favoring his children with his second wife, [[Varelle Harrowfell]]—[[Cassira Valemont]] and [[Oswin Valemont]]. Despite this, Eldric is a celebrated knight and popular with his soldiers.

### Connections

- **Faction:** Loyal to [[Voldaen]], specifically his father [[Toren Valemont]], despite his stepmother's schemes.
- **Religion:** Respectful of [[Hilltop]] traditions, though their judgment of his birth is a point of tension.
- **The Party:** Accompanied [[Taylor]] to the summit to represent their nation.

- **Appearance:** Red skin, horns, and black eyes.

- **Experiences:** "Knight of Voldaen", "Charming".
