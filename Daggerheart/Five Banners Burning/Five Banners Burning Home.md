### 1. High Arbiter Carwen Grepetor (Hilltop Theocracy)

- **Faction:** [[Hilltop]]
    
- **Role:** High ecclesiastical diplomat representing the devout temple interests and divine authority of the Theocracy.
    
- **Appearance & Personality:** Severe, older, and deeply traditional. He is accustomed to the dry, warm high-altitudes of Hilltop and does not handle the damp cold well.
    
- **Current Status:** Chained to the wet volcanic stone in the Tide Vaults. The freezing salt-water is up to his chest. He is shivering violently, pray-muttering broken liturgies to his silent gods. He is absolutely horrified after witnessing Ariena execute Princess Taylor.
    

### 2. Cily Errer (Voldaen Monarchy)

- **Faction:** [[Voldaen]]
    
- **Role:** Senior diplomat sent to Parley Point to represent the interests of the Crown.
    
- **Appearance & Personality:** Exceptionally beautiful with long brown hair and striking hazel eyes. She traditionally wears elegant, flowing ruby-red silk robes and carries a defensive, polished wooden quarterstaff (both of which have been confiscated by the Iron Anchor).
    
- **Current Status:** Stripped of her fine robes and locked in the cage adjacent to High Arbiter Grepetor. She has spent the last two weeks trying to keep the older priest alive in the rising tides. She is screaming in raw terror through the iron bars at the party, completely traumatized by the sudden, brutal murder of Voldaen's princess.
    

### 3. Admiral Keegan Thorne (Jesthaen Republic)

- **Faction:** [[Jesthaen]]
    
- **Role:** Diplomat of the Jesthaen Naval Reconnaissance force, representing the young Republic's civilian-military council (RDC).
    
- **Appearance & Personality:** A weathered, salt-crusted military veteran with a missing finger and a sharp, disciplined gaze. He wears the simple, unadorned wool uniform of a Republic officer.
    
- **Current Status:** Held in a separate, deeper vault chamber. Unlike the others, Kroller has kept Thorne in a dryer cell to "negotiate" the terms of Jesthaen's surrender of Shimmer Lake's mineral shipping routes. If the party escapes the initial flood trap, Thorne is the key to dropping the harbor chains.
    

### 4. Academician Julian Vance (Polaris Magocracy)

- **Faction:** [[Polaris]]
    
- **Role:** Senior Logician and Magitech Theorist of the High Arcana.
    
- **Appearance & Personality:** Thin, incredibly pale, with brass ocular implants and copper wires running under his collar. He speaks in a rapid, clipped stammer and is constantly calculating his own survival odds. He has a severe, hacking cough brought on by the volcanic dampness of the vaults.
    
- **Current Status:** Held in a wet drainage alcove just above the cage. His brass implants are beginning to rust in the salty lake water, causing him agonizing magical backfires. He is frantic, uselessly rubbing a cracked magitech battery in his trembling hands to try and spark a simple warmth-charm.
    

### 5. Syndicate-Envoy Kassandra "Kass" Mercer (Armada)

- **Faction:** [[Armada]]
    
- **Role:** High Broker of the Hurricane Keep Mercantile Guilds.
    
- **Appearance & Personality:** Branded with a small, faded anchor on her jawline. She wears salt-stained silks under heavy, boil-hardened sea-leather. She has a voice like rough gravel and a deeply cynical, fearless attitude. She does not beg, cry, or pray—she looks for leverage.
    
- **Current Status:** Locked in the same cage as Grepetor and Cily. While Cily screams in horror, Kass is silently using a rusted iron nail to pry at a copper conduit pipe, trying to short-circuit the hydraulic gate lock. Her cold eyes are fixed on Ariena, instantly recalculating her survival strategy after witnessing the princess's execution.
