## 📦 Mission Overview

- **Objective:** Steal arcane material from the **Gilt Banner Compact** (Mid-Tier warehouse).
- **The Client:** [[Factor Caldris Kroller]].
- **Success:** Guard shifts "shift," logs "misfile," and diplomats become retrievable.
- **Failure:** The Anchor denies all knowledge of the party.
- **Constraint:** **Total Stealth.** No witnesses/evidence tying the theft to the Anchor.

---

## 📍 Location: The Cliffside Warehouse

**Vibe:** Volcanic rock, salt spray, verticality. Accessible via **Terrace Streets** (above) or **Sea Access** (below).

### Room 1: The Lantern Walk (The Approach)

- **Hazard:** Narrow walkway over black water. Signal lanterns flare **bright blue** if tampered with.
- **Security:** * 2 Patrols (crossing at the door).
    - Mercenaries on balconies above (watchful).
    - **The Door:** Iron-banded oak, sliding hatch, speaking tube to the interior.
- **Failure State:** If the alarm sounds, shutters drop and **murder slits** open for crossbow fire.

### Room 2: The Inventory Floor (The Puzzle)

- **The Ledger:** Uses shorthand codes. Without decoding, the wrong crate triggers a **Dead-Man Glyph**.
- **Crate Key:**
    - 🔴 **Red Stripe:** Powder kegs (Explosive).
    - 🔵 **Blue Stripe:** Bundled steel (Heavy/Loud).
    - 🟡 **Yellow Stripe:** Barrels of pitch (Sticky/Flammable).
- **The Target:** Look for "Special Handling," lighter-than-expected weight, arrived 3 nights ago.
- **Complication:** Two **Clerks** return unexpectedly mid-search.

### Room 3: The Suspended Cage (The Prize)

- **Setup:** The material is in an iron cage suspended over a shaft dropping to the lake.
    
- **Mechanics:** Controlled by a pulley-and-lock system on a raised platform.
    
- **The "Panic Button":** If the guard on the platform sees trouble, he **releases the cage** into the water.
    
- **Arcane Leak:** Faint environmental effects (static, cold, or whispers) hint at the material's nature.
    

### Room 4: Rival Interruption (The Scramble)

- **The Twist:** A third-party team of thieves breaches from the sea entrance below.
    
- **The Chaos:** * Vertical combat: Ropes, swaying cages, collapsing crates.
    
    - **Goal:** Not a kill-all fight, but a race to secure the cage/material before the rivals or Gilt Banner reinforcements arrive.
        

### Room 5: The Exit (The Chase)

- **The Alert:** Whistles blowing, lanterns flaring. The "Fortress" is waking up.
    
- **Escape Options:**
    
    1. **Cargo Lift:** High-speed descent under crossbow fire.
        
    2. **Rooftops:** Athletics/Acrobatics across terraces and rope bridges.
        
    3. **The Dive:** A literal leap of faith into a waiting skiff below.
        
    4. **The Blend:** (High Difficulty) Hiding the prize in a mundane cart and walking out.
        

---

## 💎 The Payoff

- **Delivery:** Caldris inspects the crate in the shadows.
    
- **The Result:** "You understand the value of quiet solutions."
    
- **In-Game Change:** The party gains immediate access to the **Tide Vaults** via a "maintenance" window.
