l**Government:** Young Republic
**Origins:** Anti-monarchist workers' revolution

### Overview

Fifteen years ago, groups of workers and anti-monarchists declared independence from [[Voldaen]]. After a bloody war, they established a republic governed by the **Republic Defense Council (RDC)**, a mix of civilians and military advisors.
Diplomat: Admeral Keegan Thorne

### Military Structure

- **High Marshall:** Oversees all branches.
- **Branches:** Land Forces, Naval Arm, and the Arcane Corps.
- **Arcane Corps:** Includes the Arcane Demolitions and Denial Division (ADDD), where [[Elias Hughes]] served.

### Political Landscape

- **Allies:** [[Armada]]; allies of convenience with [[Polaris]].
- **Enemies:** Bitter rivals of [[Voldaen]], who still view them as a breakaway province.
- **Territory Issues:** Strongly desire control over [[The Last Fortress]].
