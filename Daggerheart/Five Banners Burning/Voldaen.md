**Government:** Proud Monarchy 
**Legacy:** The original nation of [[Althas]]

### Overview

Once the sole banner of the continent under Queen Arvold, [[Voldaen]] has been shaken by the secession of [[Jesthaen]]. It remains a nation of traditional authority, chivalry, and noble houses, though its power is waning compared to the rising republics and magocracies.

### Noble Houses

- **House [[Valemont]]:** Currently ruling the duchy formerly held by House [[Hartwell]]. Includes [[Toren Valemont]], [[Eldric Valemont]], and [[Taylor]].

### Political Landscape

- **Allies:** Old allies of [[Hilltop]].
- **Enemies:** Ideological rivals of [[Polaris]]; considers [[Jesthaen]] to be illegitimate.
- **Conflict:** Seeking to reclaim [[The Last Fortress]] and maintain their traditional dominance.
