# Elias Hughes

**Player:** Jakk 
[[Warborne]] [[Human]] [[Wizard]] ([[School of War]])

### Background

Elias grew up in the years leading to the [[Jesthaen]] secession. A commoner with immense arcane talent, he was mentored by the arcanist [[Horin]], who taught him that magic requires ownership of its consequences. During the [[Jesthaen Secession War]], Elias became a Senior Arcanist-Captain in the **[[Arcane Demolitions and Denial Division]] (ADDD)**, specializing in breaking fortifications and psychological warfare.

After [[Horin]] was killed and his image twisted into political propaganda, Elias abandoned nationalistic allegiances. He now moves with quiet conviction rather than a flag.

### Connections

- **Faction:** Formerly high-ranking in the [[Jesthaen]] military; now an independent agent.
- **Mentor:** [[Horin]] (deceased).
- **Mission:** Sent to [[Parley Point]] to represent [[Jesthaen]] interests after the summit went silent.

### Traits & Abilities

- **Domain Effects:** [[Book of Illiat]], [[Book of Tyfar]].
- **Military Rank:** Senior Arcanist-Captain.
- **Ideology:** Precise, theatrical control of magic; rejects the "heroic" narratives of war.
