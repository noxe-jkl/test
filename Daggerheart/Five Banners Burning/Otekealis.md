## PART 1: THE APPROACH (The River View)

_Read or paraphrase as the party rounds the bend in the river:_

"The landscape here shifts. The lush banks of the river give way to a jagged stone shelf that rises like a hunched shoulder. Atop that shelf sits Otekealis.

It is a masterpiece of old [[Voldaen]] military engineering—a single, massive block of weathered gray granite that looks less like it was built and more like it was hewn directly from the cliff. Two identical, square towers flank a heavy central gatehouse. It is perfectly symmetrical, severe, and functional.

But the symmetry is broken by neglect. The banners are gone, leaving empty flagpoles that rattle like dry bones. The lower docks are half-submerged, and a single rusted chain still hangs across the water gate, preventing any large vessel from passing without a fight. It sits in a state of unnatural stagnation, a ghost of an empire that no longer reaches this far south."

### THE ATMOSPHERE

- **The Stagnation:** The iron is rusted but hasn't snapped; the wood is silvered but hasn't rotted. It feels like a place where time simply stopped 70 years ago.
    
- **The Stalemate:** It sits on the border of Nemeses ([[Voldaen]] and [[Jesthaen]]). Neither side occupies it, yet neither side will let the other have it. It is a monument to a war that ended in a truce, but never a peace.

## PART 3: THE 5-ROOM CRAWL

### ROOM 1: THE WATER GATE (The Threshold)

- **Visual:** A half-collapsed dock. The portcullis is jammed halfway. Above the arch, the Crown of [[Voldaen]] is carved into the keystone, though it has been pelted with stones and defaced.
    
- **The Discovery:** Inspecting the winch reveals the gears were jammed with iron spikes from _inside_ the gatehouse. This wasn't a siege from the river; it was a trap set to keep the garrison from fleeing.
    
- **Tension:** The party's boat is a sitting duck here. Anyone watching from the hills would see them clearly.


### ROOM 2: THE COURTYARD (The Killing Ground)

- **Visual:** A wide, paved yard. The twin towers face each other across the open stones.
    
- **The Discovery:** The courtyard is littered with hundreds of spent [[Crossbow]] bolts.
    
- **The Realization:** A DC 14 History or Perception check reveals that the bolts weren't fired at the gates. They were fired _from_ the tower battlements _at_ the other tower. The garrison didn't fight an invader; they spent seven days sniping each other across this 40-foot gap of stone.

### ROOM 3: THE CHAPEL OF QUEEN ARVOLD

- **Visual:** A small stone chapel built into the base of the North Tower. A statue of the first Queen, Arvold (the dwarven [[Seraph]]), stands at the altar.
    
- **The Discovery:** The statue's face has been chiseled away. On the walls, the original "Oath of the One Banner" has been defaced. Scratched over it are the names of the dead from both sides, listed together without rank or nation.
    
- **The Tension:** The silence here is heavy. The wind through the arrow slits sounds like low whispering.

### ROOM 5: THE SOUTH TOWER (Imperial Hold)

- **Visual:** The commander's quarters. High-vaulted ceilings and heavy oak furniture.
    
- **The Discovery:** A final, unsent dispatch on the desk. It wasn't a request for reinforcements. It was a standing order from the [[Voldaen]] capital to "Purge the provincial elements and raze the keep" if loyalty was ever questioned.
    
- **The Tension:** A loose floorboard hides a locked iron box. Inside is a list of names—soldiers from both sides who were planning to desert together to avoid the fight. None of them made it out.
