**The Factor of The Tally**

### 🕯️ The Presence

**Factor Caldris Kroller** does not impose; **Factor Caldris Kroller** calculates. Appearing as a study in banked heat, their skin is a deep ember tone that fades to ashen at the fingertips—the color of cooling coals. Their eyes are solid gold, reflective like coins in shallow water, and their horns are capped in matte iron rings that serve as literal weights for balance. In a display of absolute discipline, the tail of **Factor Caldris Kroller** is never seen. They sit with hands flat against a heavy ledger, smelling of clove smoke and fresh ink, turning pages with a precision that suggests every movement was decided hours ago.

### ⚖️ The Tally & Philosophy

As the architect of **The Tally**, **Factor Caldris Kroller** operates on the principle that loyalty is a variable, but incentives are constants. They do not seek to rule the Mid-Tier, but to maintain a "Controlled Imbalance" where no single faction becomes dominant. To **Factor Caldris Kroller**, every person in the fortress is either an asset or a debt. They never raise their voice, never threaten directly, and never ask a second time. If you are speaking to them, the outcome has likely already been recorded in their ledger; you are simply catching up to the math.

### ⚔️ Tactical Protocol (The Survivalist)

**Factor Caldris Kroller** is a social antagonist who views combat as a failure of logistics. They never fight fair and prioritize survival above all else.

- **The Retreat:** If reduced to **50% HP**, **Factor Caldris Kroller** disengages immediately via pre-planned escape routes.
    
- **Utility:** Deploys smoke vials to break line-of-sight and uses a concealed, precise utility wand to manipulate the environment (locking doors, tripping foes).
    
- **Architecture:** Prefers fighting in narrow halls where they can split the party and escape through hidden seams in the volcanic rock.
    

### 💬 Notable Refrains

- _"Chaos is expensive. I prefer a quiet, profitable friction."_
    
- _"I have already calculated the outcome. We are simply waiting for your participation to catch up."_
    
- _"Do not mistake a lack of a visible tail for a lack of balance."_

# FACTOR CALDRIS KROLLER
**Tier 3 Solo**

A clinical, dispassionate Infernis bureaucrat who views personal physical combat as a fundamental failure of logistics.

- **Motives & Tactics:** Depreciate player resources, control battlefield positioning, and avoid direct melee engagement.

- **Difficulty:** 18 | **Thresholds:** Major 22 / Severe 40
- **HP:**   | **Stress:** 6

- **ATK:** +6 | **Surgical Tectonic Shards:** Far | **3d10+8** magic 
    
- **Experiences:** _Surgical Seismic Restructuring_ (+4), _Ruthless Asset Liquidation_ (+3), _Predictive Risk Assessment_ (+3)
    

### FEATURES

#### PASSIVES

- **Relentless (3):** Kroller can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight him.
    
- **Gravity Wards:** While within Far range of Kroller, all targets have their flight speed reduced to 0 and safely descend to the ground.
    
- **Clinical Discipline:** Kroller is completely immune to the _Beguiled_ condition.
    
- **Controlled Accounting:** When Kroller takes physical or magic damage, reduce the final damage total by 6.
    
- **Managed Risk:** Whenever a PC rolls a failure with Fear, Kroller immediately clears 1 Stress or you gain a Fear.
    

#### ACTIONS

- **Tectonic Shift:** Mark a Stress to make a standard attack against all targets within Close range. On a success, targets take **3d8+3** physical damage and become _Vulnerable_.
    
- **Resource Depreciation:** Mark a Stress to force a target within Far range to make a Knowledge Reaction Roll (18). On a failure, the target must choose to either cross out 2 Armor Slots or mark 2 Stress.
    
- **Margin Call:** Force a target within Far range to make a Strength Reaction Roll (18). On a failure, the target becomes _Restrained_ by rising earth.
    

#### REACTIONS

- **The Closing Ledger:** Countdown (Loop 4). This Countdown activates when combat begins and advances on every action roll. When it triggers, all targets within Far range must make an Agility Reaction Roll (18). Targets who fail take **2d10** physical damage and are pushed to Far range.
    
- **Ash Escape:** When a target moves into Melee range of Kroller, he can mark a Stress to teleport to any point within Far range and become _Hidden_.
    

### FEAR FEATURES

- **Sovereign Liquidation:** Spend a Fear to force all targets within Far range to make a Knowledge Reaction Roll (18). Targets who fail take **3d10+5** magic damage and must immediately place one active domain card from their loadout into their vault.
    
- **Foreclosure:** Spend a Fear after a PC utilizes an Experience or spends Hope. The target loses 2 Hope and becomes _Vulnerable_.
