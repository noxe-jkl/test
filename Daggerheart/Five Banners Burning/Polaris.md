**Government:** Magocracy 
**Current State:** Magical Industrial Revolution

### Overview

A nation founded by scholars and mages who wished to push the boundaries of arcane knowledge beyond the traditions of [[Voldaen]]. They are currently undergoing a period of rapid technological advancement, particularly in the development of airships.

### Political Landscape

- **Allies:** Allies of convenience with [[Jesthaen]].  
- **Enemies:** Ideological rivals of [[Voldaen]].
- **Interests:** They desperately want the specialized materials found on the island of [[The Last Fortress]] to fuel their airship production.
