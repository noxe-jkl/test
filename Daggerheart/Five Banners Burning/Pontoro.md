[[Orderborne]] [[Firbolg]] [[Warlock]] ([[Pact of the Endless]])

### Background

Pontoro was once a fighter for the pirate navies of [[Armada]] during the rebellion. After the war ended, he felt unfulfilled and sought a more peaceful life, eventually becoming a sage or "wise hermit" figure. He spent twenty years in seclusion tending his garden until the state of the world forced him back into the spotlight.

### Connections

- **Faction:** Former fighter for [[Armada]]; currently acting as a guide for the [[Jesthaen]] diplomat.
- **The Party:** Provides guidance and wisdom, acting as a "sage" for the group.

### Traits & Abilities

- **Combat Style:** Melee-focused Warlock; utilizes a powerful charging attack.
- **Reputation:** Known as a wise man people seek out for advice on the mountain.
