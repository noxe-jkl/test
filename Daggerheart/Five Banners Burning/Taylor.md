[[Highborne]] [[Faun]] (Rabbit-Flavored) [[Seraph]] ([[Winged Sentinel]])

### Background

Taylor is a princess of [[Voldaen]], though she is far down the line of succession. Described as an "unhinged warrior princess," she spent the war years earning a reputation as a brutal warlord, often sent to sack and loot cities. Her relationship with her royal family is strained, as she feels undervalued and has dedicated herself entirely to the art of war.

### Connections

- **Faction:** Member of the [[Voldaen]] royal family.
- **Family:** Relationship with parents and siblings is "strained" but they are alive.
- **The Party:** Traveling with [[Eldric Valemont]].
- **Threats:** A potential target for the assassin [[Ariena Oakley]]

### Traits & Abilities

- **Personality:** Sassy, mysterious, and potentially "war crimes manifest."
- **Goal:** To prove her worth through military might since she will not inherit the throne.
