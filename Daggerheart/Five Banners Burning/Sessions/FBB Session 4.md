## Session Notes
The party witnesses their ships leaving (with the exception of Arienna's hidden ship), then are approached by Captain Holt. She declares that new information has come to light, and the ships have been sent away since they are no longer needed. She then declares that the party is responsible for the attack, and attempts to arrest the party. A battle ensues as the party fights back, and Captain Holt leaves the pitch when her mage ally is slain. The party is eventually all detained, but continue to fight, eventually able to turn the tide of the battle.
