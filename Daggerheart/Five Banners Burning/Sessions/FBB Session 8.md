## III. The Iron Anchor: Eyes in the Guildhall

The Iron Anchor (hired by Eldric’s step-mother via [[Factor Caldris Kroller]]) has "contractors" embedded in the **Tin-Sinker’s Guildhall** (the local thieves' guild).
**The Objective:** Monitor the river for anyone asking about the "Black Crate" or the missing diplomats. **The Encounter:**

- **Location:** _The Rusty Sluice_ (A tavern where **Zoog (Fungirl)** serves potent, fermented moss-ale)
- **The Rats:** Three mercenaries watching the door: **Brakka Two-Fang (Orc)**, **Wizzle (Goblin)**, and **Thimble (Halfling)**.
- **The Reveal:** If the party asks about the "Grey Ship," **Brakka** will stand up, blocking the exit, while **Wizzle** and **Thimble** slip into the shadows to prepare an ambush in the alley.

## IV. The Shadow Threat: The "Uncrowned"

A third faction has arrived in Nowjaw: **The Uncrowned**. They are radical Jesthaen separatists who want to assassinate [[Taylor]] to trigger an irreversible war
**NPC: Puck (Faun, She/They)**
- **Appearance:** Scars across her horns, wearing a tattered Republic cloak and a belt full of **Darksmoke Vials**.
- **Tactics:** They use hit-and-run strikes. **Puck** will coordinate rooftop snipers while the party is distracted by the town guard or the Anchor mercs.
- **Trigger:** They strike when the party is leaving the Chancellor's office, using the narrow streets to trap the group.
    

## V. Notable Locations & NPCs

### 1. The Chancellor’s Office

- **NPC:** [[Mayor Sebastian Osbert]] **(Human, He/Him)**
- **The Meeting:** Sebastian is a pragmatist. While he appreciates the order the Republic brings, he is tired of the violence. He is currently "Content" but wary of the rising tensions.
- **The Trade:** He will give the party a "Safe Passage" token to [[The Last Fortress]] IF they deal with **Puck's** cell of "Uncrowned" extremists, who are disrupting his tin exports and threatening the town's stability.

### 2. The Tin-Sinker’s Guildhall

- **Vibe:** Smells of sulfur and cheap ale.
- **The Hook:** **Grint (Goblin)**, a jittery informant, knows that a "Black Crate" was moved through the quarry tunnels three nights ago by a "Clank who didn't click" (referring to a different magitech signature than Vesper).

## VI. The Reason to Leave: "The Tally is Closing"

The party shouldn't stay long. Use these "Pressure Beats":

1. **The Recognition:** **Elion (Elf)**, a local artist, is seen sketching [[Taylor]]’s face onto a "Wanted" poster near the town square.
2. **The Sovereign’s Heart:** Vesper’s internal metronome starts ticking faster. She warns that a Polaris team led by **Brim (Clank)** is closing in on her signal.
3. **The Message:** **Nib (Clank)**, a small mechanical courier, delivers a cold, metal plate to Eldric with an engraved message from [[Factor Caldris Kroller]]: _"The Tally is being settled at the Tide Vaults. Late arrivals will be considered debts to be cleared."_

## VII. Combat Encounter: The Sluice Ambush

**Enemies:**

- **Brakka Two-Fang (Orc)** & **Thimble (Halfling):** Frontline bruisers for the Iron Anchor.
- **2x Uncrowned Snipers:** Hidden on the rooftops (use **Velma** and **Snag** as names).
- **Puck (Faun):** Uses smoke and poison to disorient the party.

**The Twist:** The Iron Anchor mercs (**Wizzle** and team) and the Uncrowned (**Puck** and team) are not allies. If the party starts a fire or causes enough chaos, the two groups will begin targeting each other to ensure they are the ones to claim the "prize."

## VIII. Ending the Session

The session ends with the party escaping on a tin-transport skiff piloted by **Plim (Ribbet)**, a frantic dockworker. As they race toward **The Last Fortress**, the rhythmic stamps of Nowjaw fade, replaced by the ominous, silent approach of a Grey Ship on the horizon.
