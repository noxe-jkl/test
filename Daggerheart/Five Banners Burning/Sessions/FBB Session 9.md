## II. Act 1: The Night at The Rusty Sleuth

The air in the Sleuth is thick with the smell of fermented moss-ale and desperation. **Gella ([[Fungril]])** is manning the front, watching the party with a mix of curiosity and boredom.

### The Fighting Ring (The "Tin-Mash")

The party needs [[Gold]]. **Brakka Two-Fang ([[Orc]])** is the matchmaker. He sees the "unusual" travelers and offers a high-stakes bout.


- **The Opponents:** **[[The Brown Skeleton Company]]** scouts—**Drogath ([[Orc]])** and **Kurn ([[Human]])**. They are tough, salt-crusted brawlers who fight with hooks and nets.
    
- **The Stakes:** Brakka offers 3 bags of [[Gold]] for a victory. If the party puts up their own [[Gold]]/items, they can reach the 6-bag goal in one night.
    
- **Mechanics:** Use this fight to test Level 3 survivability.
    
    - _Crowd Favor:_ If a player spends **Hope** to do something theatrical, the crowd tosses in "[[Nowjaw]] Tin" (temporary [[Armor]]).
        
    - _Crowd Fear:_ On a **Fear** roll, an observer from **The Uncrowned** tosses a **Darksmoke Vial** into the pit to see how the party reacts to blind [[Combat]].
        

## III. Act 2: The Shadow of Puck & The Uncrowned

After the fight, or during a quiet moment at the inn, **Puck ([[Faun]])**—the "glammed up" herbalist from earlier—approaches the group.

- **The Sincerity:** She is no longer acting "glammed." She is sharp, cold, and smells of poison.
    
- **The Reveal:** She is a leader of **The Uncrowned**. She knows exactly who **[[Taylor]]** is.
    
- **The Offer:** She wants the party to "send a message" to the Republic.
    
    - _The Job:_ Assassinate **Mayor Sebastian Osbert** tonight.
        
    - _The Incentive:_ She will provide the remaining [[Gold]] for the ship and a "[[Ghost]] route" out of the harbor that Mavik’s patrol boats can’t track.
        
    - _The Threat:_ If they refuse, she’ll tip off the central garrison that a [[Voldaen]] Warlord Princess is sleeping in the Sleuth.
        

## IV. Act 3: The Mayor’s Ploy

If the party goes to the "posh" district or seeks out **Mayor Sebastian Osbert**, they find a man who is "Content" but drowning in paperwork.

- **NPC:** [[Mayor Sebastian Osbert]] ([[Human]]).
    
- **The Counter-Offer:** Sebastian knows his town is infested with Iron Anchor "Rats" and Uncrowned "Shadows."
    
    - He offers a **[[Jesthaen]] Naval Letter of Marque** (legalizing their travel to [[The Last Fortress]]) if they expose the Uncrowned cell.
        
    - _The Catch:_ He can’t give them [[Gold]] directly without a paper trail, but he can "confiscate" the Iron Anchor’s local holdings and let the party "recover" them.
        

## V. Act 4: The Noon Deadline

As the sun rises, the "Pressure Beats" begin:

1. **The Recognition:** **Elion ([[Elf]])** is seen in the town square with a charcoal sketch of [[Taylor]]. The resemblance is striking.
    
2. **The Sentinel:** **Mavik ([[Human]])** has moved his patrol boat directly in front of the pirate’s pier. He is "inspecting" all outbound vessels for "[[Voldaen]] contraband."
    
3. **The Tally:** **Nib ([[Clank]])**—a tiny, whirring clockwork courier—finds **[[Eldric Valemont|Eldric]]**. It extends a metal arm holding a cold, etched plate.
    
    - _Message:_ "[[Factor Caldris Kroller]] notes your delay. Interest on the diplomats' lives is compounded hourly. The Tide Vaults flood at sunset. Do not be a bad investment."
        

## VI. [[Combat]] Encounter: The Sluice Breach

When the party heads to the docks to claim their ship, the factions collide.

- **Enemies:**
    
    - **The Anchor Rats:** **Wizzle ([[Goblin]])** and **Thimble ([[Halfling]])** leading 3 Mercs.
        
    - **The Uncrowned:** **Puck ([[Faun]])** and 2 Snipers on the crane-platforms.
        
- **The Layout:** A narrow pier over the tin-processing sluices. Shifting cargo crates provide cover.
    
- **The Twist:** If the party has the **[[Sovereign Vesper]]**, her "metronome" heart begins to pulse visibly. A [[Polaris]] tracking beam strikes the pier from the sky (Brim’s team), forcing everyone to [[Scramble]] for cover.
    

## VII. Ending the Session

The party boards the fast ship (piloted by **Plim - [[Ribbet]]**). As they break for the open water of Shimmer Lake, they see the **Grey Ship**—Holt’s vessel—emerging from the morning fog like a predator.
