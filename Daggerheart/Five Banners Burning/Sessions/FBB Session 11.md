## Session Notes
## III. Act 1: The Chamber of the Architect

The party is escorted past the Blackwater Docks and up the terraced stone steps of the Mercenary City. No weapons are confiscated—a detail that should make them deeply paranoid. They are led into the **Upper Bastion**, entering a circular stone solar smelling of dried clove smoke, sea salt, and cold ink.

### NPC: Factor Caldris Kroller (The Liquidator)

- **The Look:** Immaculate, ash-grey tailored robes with deep crimson lining. His skin is the color of cooling coals, his gold eyes look like freshly minted coins, and his obsidian horns are capped in matte iron rings.
    
- **The Demeanor:** He has "bloody hands so sticky sweet"—he acts entirely like a reasonable, polite public servant. He speaks in a soft whisper, "sweet as baby's breath," but carrying the "long, icy cold finger of death." His teeth are too perfect, white, and sharp when he smiles ("deceitful teeth").
    

## IV. Act 2: Face-to-Face (The Welcome Script)

_Kroller is sitting behind an unpolished basalt desk. He does not stand. He slowly closes a massive ledger, sliding a single iron-capped finger across the cover. When he speaks, his voice is a low, gentle whisper that easily cuts through the silence of the room._

> **"Sit down. Please. Let us dispense with the theatrical indignation.**
> 
> **You have spent fourteen days bleeding across Shimmer Lake, chasing a tragedy that you believe was born of ancient hatreds and broken treaties. It is a beautiful story. It keeps the recruitment offices full and the war furnaces burning. But it is a lie. A great, big, beautifully calculated lie.**
> 
> **The Five Banners are terrified of a tragedy. They are so consumed by the fear of what might happen that they never look closely at what is actually being done right in front of them. This war? It is a machine. A magnificent, self-sustaining machine. It is the tool we use to separate you. To make some of you afraid, to make others full of rage, to ensure that everyone feels they have been betrayed. Because when you are broken, when there is no give and take, you look to me to settle the accounts. And I make you pay. Oh, how we make you pay.**
> 
> **Nobody wins if everybody dies. I do not want a graveyard, travelers. I want a market. And a market requires controlled friction.**
> 
> **Which brings me to you. You are small. You are expendable. You do not belong to the grand armies or the noble houses. Yet, you have introduced continuous, messy noise into my ledger. You survived Parley Point. You broke Kathu. You arrived here thinking you were heroes in a tragedy. But you are simply bad arithmetic. A stray balance that must be brought to zero."**

### 💬 The Confrontation & The Trigger

If the players try to argue, threaten, or cast magic:

- Kroller smiles—a flash of sharp, deceitful teeth.
    
- _"You want to fight the machine? Go ahead. But remember... nobody wins if everyone dies. Especially those who think their wings make them exempt from the fall. Welcome to the show."_
    
- He presses a hidden iron pedal beneath his basalt desk.
    

## V. Act 3: "Here It Is, Surprise!" (The Snap-Trap)

The floor does not tilt; it simply vanishes.

### ⚙️ Countering the Seraph's Flight (Kroller's Precautions)

Kroller is an accountant of variables; he knows **Taylor** has wings. The trap is designed to actively neutralize her flight:

1. **The Weighted Iron Mesh:** Simultaneously as the floor drops, a massive, heavy iron-mesh net drops from the ceiling at double the speed of the fall. This physical weight is designed to drape over her wings, tangling her feathers and dragging her down with the rest of the group.
    
2. **Polaris Gravity-Dampening Runes:** The moment the pedal is pressed, the circular solar walls flare with violet light. Kroller has installed custom **Gravity-Dampening Wards** purchased from Polaris. For 3 rounds, all magical and natural flight speeds within the solar are reduced to 0.
    
3. **The Harpoon Snipers:** If Taylor somehow spends **Hope** or uses a special trait to try and defy both the net and the runes, hidden panels in the solar walls slide open. Three Iron Anchor guards with pneumatic harpoon-guns fire tethered steel claws. They do not aim to kill—they aim to snag her armor or limbs and winch her down into the chute with her friends.
    

### ⚙️ Mechanical Hazards of the Iron Drop:

1. **The Fall:** The stone floor beneath the party drops like a trapdoor. They slide/fall down a vertical, grease-slicked volcanic chute cutting straight down through the heart of the mountain.
    
2. **The Impact:** They plunge thirty feet directly into the pitch-black, freezing water of the **Tide Vaults**.
    
3. **The Cage:** They land inside a massive, rusted iron cage ($20\text{ ft} \times 20\text{ ft}$) submerged in chest-deep water. As they hit, a massive overhead iron grate slams shut above them with a deafening, hydraulic _CLANG_.
    
4. **The Disadvantage:** All non-magical torches, maps, and paper scrolls are instantly ruined by the black lake water.
    

## VI. Act 4: The Tide Vaults Climax (Ariena's Choice)

_The only light comes from faint, water-logged luminescent moss clinging to the volcanic stone. The air is freezing. The water is rising._

- **The Sight:** Across the flooded stone corridor, locked behind heavy, barnacle-encrusted iron bars, are the shivering, half-drowned missing diplomats: **High Arbiter Carwen Grepetor** and **Minister Cily Errer**. They are chained to the walls, the water already up to their shoulders.
    
- **The Audio:** A brass speaking tube in the ceiling of the cage crackles to life. Kroller's whisper echoes down, dry and sweet:
    
    > _"Ariena. Your twenty-four hours are concluded. The Executioners Guild has already logged your default. But I am a man of transactions. Your handlers are standing directly above your cage with crossbows. Push the Princess under the water. Hold her there until the bubbles stop. The moment the princess's ledger is closed, your cage door opens and your debts are erased. The rest of you may assist her... or you may drown together as collateral."_
