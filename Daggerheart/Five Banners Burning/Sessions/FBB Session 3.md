**Back to [[Five Banners Burning Home]]**
## I. Opening Beat: Evidence in Hand, Wake on the Water

**Start in motion.**

The party is standing among the bodies the goblins dragged aside for looting. Armor stripped. Pockets emptied. Bones cleaned with scavenger efficiency. They have finished with the goblins. There is nothing left to ask.

As they work, the water changes.

Describe the _wake_ before the ship. The way sound carries across Shimmer Lake. The low, disciplined creak of rigging.

A grey-hulled ship approaches. No banner. No heraldry. Perfect lines. Reefed sails. Armed, but not aggressively so.

This is not a warship.  
This is an intervention.

---

## II. The Note (Immediate Payoff)

The body of **Rowan Blackmere’s bodyguard** is among those pulled aside. He was killed cleanly, efficiently, and deliberately hidden just well enough to be missed unless someone was looking.

On his person is a folded parchment, oil-stained, written in a precise, practiced hand. Hilltop clerical shorthand marks the margins.

**Before reading it aloud:**  
Ask the table:

> “Who do you show this to?”

Then present the note.

### The Note (Verbatim)

> **“Blackmere is not to be harmed unless she speaks.**
> 
> **If she resists relocation, terminate her escort and proceed without delay.**
> 
> **The summit is already lost. What matters now is that no version of events emerges without our blessing.**
> 
> **The Iron Anchor has been paid to make this look like a republic sin.**
> 
> **Ensure the girl reaches the water alive. The rest are expendable.”**
> 
> — unsigned

**Clerical Analysis:**

- Pontoro immediately recognizes the margin marks as Hilltop cipher shorthand
    
- The language implies institutional authority, not mercenary planning
    
- “Our blessing” suggests a body that believes it has the right to define truth
    

This confirms the frame of Jesthaen—and introduces Hilltop as a controlling hand rather than a victim.

## III. The Grey Ship Makes Contact

The ship does not rush. It comes to anchor with professional precision.

A launch boat is lowered. Armed crew, controlled posture. No threats.

### What They Claim (Public Answer)

If asked directly who they serve:

> “We are an **Intercessory Vessel of Hilltop**, chartered under the authority of the Seeker Conclave to prevent escalation between the banners.”

This statement is true.

### What That Means (GM Truth)

- The ship answers to a _quiet sub-faction within Hilltop_
    
- They believe mortal stability is a sacred duty in the gods’ absence
    
- Truth is secondary to preventing war
    
- They are acting without broad public sanction, but with deep confidence
    

They are not villains. They are convinced they are right.

---

## IV. NPC: Captain Seraphine Holt, “The Quiet Oar”

**Name:** Captain Seraphine Holt

**Affiliation:** Hilltop — Seeker Conclave (Intercessory Branch)

**Appearance:**

- Human woman, late 40s
    
- Weathered face, iron-grey hair braided tight at the nape
    
- Simple, immaculate coat with no insignia
    
- Carries no visible holy symbol
    

**Demeanor:**

- Calm, measured, unhurried
    
- Never raises her voice
    
- Maintains steady eye contact
    
- Speaks as if every word has already been weighed and approved
    

**Reputation (If Known):**

- Called _“The Quiet Oar”_ among Hilltop circles
    
- Where she goes, crises stop escalating
    
- No one ever seems to prove wrongdoing afterward
    

### Captain Holt’s Core Belief

> “Peace is not born from truth. It is born from restraint.”

She genuinely believes:

- Jesthaen is being framed
    
- Revealing that truth _now_ will cause more bloodshed than silence
    
- The party is standing at a historical inflection point they may not survive
    

### Holt’s Opening Line

> “You’ve done well. Better than most. Which means you’re now holding something sharp enough to cut the continent.”

### What Holt Wants

She will pursue these in order:

1. **Delay** — no public statements yet
    
2. **Control** — Hilltop coordinates the narrative
    
3. **Custody** — bodies, evidence, and especially the note
    

She never demands. She _offers_.

### Holt’s Pressure Lines

Use these sparingly:

- “If you speak now, people will die who never chose this.”
    
- “History will be written with or without you. I am offering to keep you alive in it.”
    
- “You are correct about Jesthaen. That does not make you safe.”
    

### Insight / Magic Reads

- Absolute sincerity
    
- No personal ambition
    
- A woman who has buried truths before and slept afterward

## V. The Core Conflict of the Session

The players already believe Jesthaen is being framed.

The real question is now:

**What happens if the truth comes out _now_?**

Holt will openly acknowledge:

> “Yes. The Republic is being framed.”

And then counter:

> “And if that truth ignites war tonight, was it worth being right?”

This is not a debate about facts.  
It is a debate about timing, cost, and authority.

---

## VI. Escalating Pressure (Use As Needed)

- A Hilltop messenger arrives _not_ aligned with Holt’s orders
    
- Word spreads that Jesthaen scouts are already being blamed publicly
    
- The Grey Ship begins quietly removing bodies unless stopped
    
- Rowan Blackmere’s status becomes time-sensitive (confirmed alive / lost at sea)
    

Each pressure should respond to player action.

---

## VII. Ending the Session

End without resolution.

Strong ending beats:

- The Grey Ship departs with or without something important
    
- The party must decide whether to release, hide, or weaponize the note
    
- A public accusation begins forming that they could interrupt—or exploit
    

### Final Question (Ask One Player)

> “If you speak now, people will die.
> 
> If you wait, the truth may be buried.
> 
> Which cost are you willing to pay?”

Their answer determines Session 4.

---

## GM Reminder

Do not plan outcomes.  
Track reactions.

If you feel stuck, ask:

> “Who benefits if this stays quiet—and what would they do _right now_?”

Then act.
