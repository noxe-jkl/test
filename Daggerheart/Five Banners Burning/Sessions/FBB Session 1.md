# Session 0.5: The Call to Action

**Timing:** Exactly 14 days after the summit at [[Parley Point]] began. The silence has lasted for 10 days.

### **The Scene: The War Room of Hurricane Keep**

**The Atmosphere (The "Mise-en-scène")**
_(Begin with a low, steady voice to set the mood. Engage the senses before the characters speak.)_

The War Room isn't a place of comfort; it’s a stone throat that swallows the sound of the ocean below. High, narrow windows look out over the churning grey peaks of the sea, and the glass rattles with every hammer-blow of the waves.

Inside, the air is thick—heavy with the **smell of old, dry parchment** and the **sharp, cold tang of oiled steel** from the suits of armor standing like silent ghosts in the corners. The only light comes from the flickering braziers and a single shaft of grey morning light that falls across a massive oak table.

**The Setup (Spotlight & Fill)**

Standing in that light is **Toren Valemont**. He looks like a man whose dynasty is fraying at the edges; his hair is thinner than last you saw him, his shoulders hunched under the weight of his mantle. His fingers, stained with ink and age, trace the jagged, ink-drawn coastline of **Shimmer Lake** as if he’s trying to hold the land together by touch alone.

---

### **The Briefing**

**Toren:** _(He finally looks up, his eyes rimmed with red from lack of sleep. He looks at [[Taylor]], then [[Eldric Valemont|Eldric]].)_

> "The silence from **Parley Point** is deafening. Our diplomats, our cousins... they haven't sent word in ten days. If this were a simple delay, a bird would have flown. If this were a disagreement, we would hear the shouting from here."

_(He reaches into the shadows of the table and pushes a small, ornate birdcage across the wood. It’s empty, the door swinging open with a **sharp, metallic creak**.)_

> "The messenger hawks we sent four days ago returned empty-handed—or didn't return at all. The winds are fair; there is no weather to blame. Only malice."

_(Toren leans forward, his hands flat on the table. This is the "Key Light"—the high-contrast truth of his character.)_

**Toren:** "I know what the court is saying. I hear them through the walls. They’re screaming for [[Jesthaen]] blood. They think I’m hesitating out of weakness."

_(He lowers his voice, a rare moment of raw honesty.)_

"But my towers... the coastal signal lights at the Parley Point pavilion? They went dark ten days ago. Not a spark since. We cannot survive another decade of war, but we cannot survive being blind either."

---

### **The Command**

_(He stands tall, regaining his kingly mask. He looks at Taylor first.)_

**Toren:** "**[[Taylor]]**, you are to be the **Eyes of the Crown**. Go there. See what they have hidden in the dark. Bring me the truth before the court forces my hand into a war we cannot win."

_(He turns to [[Eldric Valemont|Eldric]], his gaze turning hard and cold.)_

"And **[[Eldric Valemont|Eldric]]**... you are the **Shield**. But a shield is also a heavy weight to drop on an enemy's throat. If you find proof of treachery—if the Republic has broken the peace—set aside your 'honor.' Strike them first. Strike them hard enough that they don't have a second breath to ask for terms."

### **RP Opportunity**

- **To [[Taylor]]:** As your father speaks, you feel the "weight of the peace" settling on your shoulders. The court outside these doors wants a warlord, but the man in front of you is asking for a spy. **How do you respond to this duality?**
    
- **To [[Eldric Valemont|Eldric]]:** Toren’s instruction is a direct order to abandon the knightly code you’ve lived by. He’s asking for a preemptive execution if the Republic is involved. **How does it feel to have your father give you permission—or a command—to be dishonorable?**

### **The Scene: The RDC Command Center**

**The Atmosphere (The "Mise-en-scène")**

Unlike the salt-sprayed heights of Voldaen, the RDC Command Center is a **spartan, windowless bunker**. The air is stagnant, vibrating with the **rhythmic, frantic scratching of a dozen quills** from clerks in the shadows. It smells of **industrial oil, cold stone, and stale coffee**.

**Magister General Valerius** sits behind a massive slab of unpolished granite. The light here doesn't flicker; it’s the steady, humming glow of mag-lamps that cast harsh, unforgiving shadows. **Pontoro**, you stand in the corner—your robes and your "sage" presence feel like a splash of color in a world of grey and iron.

**The Entrance (The Spot Light)**

The heavy iron-bound doors groan open. The scratching quills stop for a heartbeat.

**Elias Hughes** enters. You aren’t in the flamboyant robes of the High Academies; you wear the practical, singed leather and reinforced gear of the **ADDD**. Your boots strike the stone with a rhythmic, military precision that the clerks recognize—the walk of a man who has stood on both sides of a collapsing wall.

**Magister General Valerius** looks up from a slab of granite. He doesn't smile, but there is a glimmer of recognition in his eyes.

---

### **The Briefing**

**Valerius:** _(His voice is dry, like grinding stones.)_

> "Hughes. I heard you’d traded your commission for 'conviction.' I’m glad to see you still answer a priority summons. We have a situation that requires a specialist who understands the... structural nature of power."

_(He slides a thin, leather-bound dossier across the stone toward Elias.)_

> "Magister Thorne—our lead diplomat—is ten days overdue. The **Voldaen** are already moving scouts to the border. They’re looking for a reason to claim we sabotaged the talks. But the silence is universal. No hawks, no riders, no survivors."

_(He leans forward, his shadow stretching long across the map.)_

> "The signal lights at the Parley Point pavilion went out ten days ago and never came back on. **Elias**, I need you on the ground. If that structure is standing, I need you to find Thorne. But if it isn't... I need a man who can read the ruins. I need to know if it was a collapse, or if it was _taken_ down."

---

### **The Hidden Agenda (The Subtext)**

_(Valerius stands and walks to a tactical slate. He taps a series of marks on the border.)_

**Valerius:** "Our border scouts spotted smoke from the site a few days ago—the exact moment the pavilion went dark. Since then, the Voldaen military posture has shifted to 'Aggressive.' They aren't mourning a loss; they're positioning for a strike."

_(He walks over to Elias, lowering his voice. He’s speaking to the "Specialist," not the "Independent.")_

"Elias... the court is asking for a diplomatic solution, but I’ve seen this before. If you find so much as a scorched timber or a cracked foundation, I want you to find the signature of the magic that caused it. Don't let the Voldaen 'peace-keepers' sweep the evidence under the rug. If they’ve started this, I want the proof in my hand before the first stone falls."

---

### **RP Opportunity**

- **To Elias:** Valerius is being careful with his words, but he’s sending an **Arcane Demolitions** expert to a disappearance. He isn't asking you to find a missing person; he's asking you to be ready to conduct a "post-mortem" on a crime scene he expects to find. **How do you feel about being recruited for your "destructive" skill set before a single fact has been confirmed?**
    
- **To Pontoro:** You see the way Valerius’s hand hovers over the military markers on the map. He mentions the Voldaen scouts moving, but he doesn't mention that his own scouts are doing the same. **As a reader of hearts, do you sense that Valerius is genuinely worried about Thorne, or is he simply waiting for a "justifiable" reason to go to war?**

### **The Scene: The Salty Dog Tavern**

**The Atmosphere (The "Mise-en-scène")**

_(Speak in a low, conspiratorial hush. The noise of the tavern should feel like a wall of static protecting this table.)_

The Salty Dog is a place where the ceiling is so low it feels like the weight of the city is pressing down on your head. The air is a thick, grey soup of **acrid pipe smoke and the sour, heavy stench of spilled ale**.

Shadows here aren't just lack of light; they feel physical. You sit in a corner booth, the wood sticky under your palms, while a courier from the **Executioners Guild** slides into the seat across from you. He doesn't look at you. He keeps his hood low, his eyes fixed on the dirty rim of a glass he isn't drinking from.

**The Setup (The "Tension")**

Without a word, he pulls a **heavy, cold iron key** from his coat. He doesn't hand it to you. He just taps it rhythmically against the table—_clink, clink, clink_. The sound is a sharp, metallic reminder of a specific lock on a specific cell that haunts your sleep.

---

### **The Briefing**

**The Courier:** _(His voice is a dry rasp, barely audible over the tavern’s roar.)_

> "The Guild is sweating, Oakley. Ten days of silence from the summit. It’s making the masters nervous. But where there’s silence, there’s opportunity."

_(He slides a small, folded square of parchment across the sticky wood.)_

> "The **Voldaen** princess is heading to the ruins to 'investigate.' The Guild knows her route. More importantly, they know she’s the next name you’re going to cross off."

_(You unfold the paper. On the front is a charcoal sketch of **Taylor**. You flip it over. Your breath hitches. It’s a child’s drawing—a messy, yellow sun and a stick-figure Faun, the lines shaky and innocent.)_

> "Finish the job at **Parley Point**. If she dies in the wreckage, we call it a Jesthaen accident. If she survives the ruins, you make sure she doesn't survive the journey home. Do this, and the doors stay open. You might even get another hour to... visit."

---

### **The Hidden Agenda (The "Hook")**

_(The courier finally looks up, and for a split second, you see the cold, hollow emptiness in his eyes.)_

**The Courier:** "Don't get sentimental, Oakley. Gain her trust. Become her shadow. Let her believe she’s found a friend in the dark. It’ll make it that much easier when the time comes."

_(He leans in, his voice dropping to a jagged whisper.)_

"Fail this, and we stop feeding the residents of that cell. We have plenty of other names on the list. Don't let yours—or theirs—be next."

# Session 1: The Scars of [[Shimmer Lake]]

### **I. Arrival: The Charred Jewel**

**The Atmosphere** The grand pavilion, once a sprawling masterpiece of glass and cedar, is now a blackened ribcage sinking into the shallows of Shimmer Lake. The silence is absolute, broken only by the rhythmic _slap_ of water against scorched marble. The air carries the heavy, greasy scent of **wet soot and cold iron**.

As the Voldaen and Jesthaen vessels converge, the tension is visible in the distance kept between the hulls. On the docks, a third, smaller craft sits—the neutral mark of a hired scout—bridging the gap between the two approaching powers.

**The Witnesses** At the edge of the tree line, a small group of **Firbolgs** moves through the mist. They are silent, their large hands covered in ash as they sift through the perimeter debris. They don't look like warriors; they look like a search party that has spent all night looking for a heartbeat they haven't found.

### **II. Exploration: The Anatomy of a Blast**

**The Structural Evidence** Under the skeletal remains of the central dome, the damage tells a specific story. A massive support beam lies shattered on the floor. The wood isn't just burned; the grain has been **pulverized from the inside out**.

Near the base of the pillar, there is a distinct **white-grey residue** and the sharp, chemical tang of **Republic Blasting Powder**. The placement is curious—the charges were set high and exposed, rather than tucked into the structural joins. It is a loud, messy display that ensured the building would collapse, but only after it had burned long enough for the signature of the powder to be unmistakable.

**The Diplomatic Table** In the center of the wreckage, the massive stone table where the peace was to be signed remains mostly intact. The chairs are overturned, but there are no signs of struggle near the seats. Most notably, the high-backed chairs reserved for the diplomats are **empty of remains**. There are no ceremonial robes, no signets, and no bone ash.

The floor around the table is scuffed with heavy boot-tracks that cut through the soot—lines that suggest heavy weight was dragged toward the water’s edge, away from the center of the explosion.

---

### **III. The Evidence of Treachery**

**The Guard Detail** Near the entrance, the remains of the guard detail lie in the ash. Despite the explosion, their armor is largely intact. They weren't killed by the blast. Their wounds are precise—**clean punctures in the gaps of their plate** and slit throats. These were close-quarters executions, carried out by someone already inside the perimeter.

One of the bodies, dressed in a **Jesthaen Republic uniform**, lies facedown. When turned over, the uniform's fit is noticeably wrong—the tunic is slightly too large, and the rank pins are pinned upside down. Tucked into the man’s belt is not a Republic sidearm, but a heavy, rusted boarding hatchet.

**The Iron Anchor** Discarded near the shoreline is a piece of heavy leather gear—a mercenary’s pauldron. Scratched crudely into the hardened surface is a symbol: **an Iron Anchor wrapped in a jagged, broken chain**.
