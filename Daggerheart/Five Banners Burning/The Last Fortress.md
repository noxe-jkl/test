## The Layers

### 1. The Blackwater Docks (Lowest Tier)

- Long piers reinforced with iron brackets.
    
- Mooring posts carved from shipwreck hulls.
    
- Cranes powered by counterweights lift cargo directly into cliffside openings.
    
- Sea caves yawn beneath the docks — only visible at low tide.
    

This is loud, active, and public. Mercenaries walk openly armed.

---

### 2. The Mid-Tier (The Mercenary City)

Carved terraces stacked into the rock.

- Stone staircases worn smooth by boots.
    
- Overhanging balconies strung with signal lanterns.
    
- Taverns built half into the cliff face.
    
- Narrow alleys where you can’t see the sky.
    

Iron Anchor headquarters — **The Anchor House** — is here. Not grand. Just solid. Practical. Reinforced.

There is prosperity here.

But it’s transactional.

No one stays who can’t pay.

---

### 3. The Upper Bastion (Old Fortress Core)

Ancient stonework.

- Walls thicker than three men laid shoulder to shoulder.
    
- Arrow slits shaped for older wars.
    
- Inner courtyard open to the sky.
    
- Watchtowers with braziers always lit.
    

This is where discipline lives.

Iron Anchor officers sleep here.  
Gray Ship detainees are processed here.  
Orders are given here.

Below it lie the Tide Vaults.

---

### 4. The Tide Vaults (Hidden Core)

Sea caves hollowed and reinforced.

- Iron bars sunk directly into stone.
    
- Floor channels designed to flood cells at high tide.
    
- Chains fixed into rock faces.
    
- The constant drip of water.
    

When the tide rises, the sound becomes thunder.

This is where the diplomats are held.

And where secrets drown easily.
