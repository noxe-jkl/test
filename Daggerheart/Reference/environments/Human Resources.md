# THE HR OFFICE

_**Tier 1 Social**_ _A fortress of bureaucracy, beige carpet, and passive-aggressive signage._ **Impulses:** Obstruct with paperwork, delay the inevitable, enforce policy

> **Difficulty:** 12
> 
> **Potential Adversaries:** HR Reps (Socials), Manager Brenda

## FEATURES

_**Red Tape - Passive:**_ The environment is stifling. Physical and Power-based checks made in this environment have **Disadvantage** unless the PC first succeeds on a Presence Roll to navigate the bureaucracy.

_How does the paperwork literally get in your way? What mundane question does the receptionist ask that completely derails your momentum?_

_**"Did You Fill Out Form 27-B?" - Action:**_ The GM introduces a bureaucratic hurdle. A PC must make a Knowledge Roll to recall the correct procedure or produce the correct ID. On a failure, the party must mark a total of 2 Stress (distributed as they choose) as they are forced to wait in the lobby.

_What obscure rule is cited to stop you? Why is your ID badge technically invalid right now?_

_**Code of Conduct - Reaction:**_ When a PC fails a roll with Fear, the HR Rep notes a violation in their permanent file. Tick the **Buzz Track** down by 1.

_What minor infraction did you just commit? Who noticed it and began furiously typing?_
