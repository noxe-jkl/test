# APARTMENT 2B (THE LAIR)

_**Tier 1 Traversal/Event**_ 
_A smoggy, run-down apartment crowded with animated junk and wired with traps._ 
**Impulses:** Defend the genius, shock intruders, animate the inanimate

> **Difficulty:** 12
> **Potential Adversaries:** Gary Blevins, Vacuum Minions, Toaster Horde

## FEATURES

_**Live Wires - Passive:**_ The floor is covered in cables and stripped copper. Any PC moving further than Close range in a single turn must make an Agility Roll (12). On a failure, they take 1d6 magic damage (shock) and stop moving.

_Where are the sparks flying from? Which innocuous appliance is actually a conduit for high voltage?_

_**The Omni-Java Power Source - Action:**_ Gary draws power from the coffee machine. Spend a Fear to heal Gary for 1d4 HP or clear all his Stress. If the machine is damaged or unplugged, this action cannot be taken.

_How does the coffee machine glow when Gary draws on it? What terrifying noise does it make?_

_**Appliance Riot - Reaction:**_ When a PC rolls with Fear while inside the apartment, a nearby mundane object (blender, lamp, fan) springs to life and attacks. The PC must mark 1 Stress or 1 HP (player's choice) from a minor scrap.

_What object betrays you? How does a ceiling fan become a weapon?_
