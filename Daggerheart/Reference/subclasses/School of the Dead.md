# SCHOOL OF THE DEAD

Gain power through grave means

## SPELLCAST TRAIT

Knowledge

## FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Grave Secrets:_** You have delved into forbidden scripts, allowing you to take Dread domain cards equal to half your level (rounded up)

**_Grim Harvest:_** When an adversary within Far range is defeated, you may mark a Stress to either gain a Hope or clear a Hit Point.

## SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Raise the Dead:_** When an Adversary is defeated within Far range, you may spend 2 Hope to animate them as a **Thrall**. While active, you may command the Thrall to move. You may make a **Spellcast Roll** to strike an Adversary, dealing d8 magic damage using your Proficiency. The Thrall has 10 Evasion and is destroyed if it takes any damage, or if a new Thrall is animated.

## MASTERY FEATURES

**_Veil Walker:_** You exist between worlds, granting you +1 **Evasion** and the ability to move through solid objects and Adversaries during your movement.

**_Reaper's Toll:_** When you or your **Thrall** take damage from an Adversary, you may place a token on this card (max 3). Make a **Spellcast Roll** and spend all tokens on this card to deal 2d12 magic damage per token. If this attack defeats the target, clear Stress equal to tokens spent.
