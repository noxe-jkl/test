# DEMIGOD

Demigods are creatures whose veins flow with the blood of the gods. They carry the weight of divine expectation and power, often manifesting as exceptional talent or a presence that commands attention.

## TRANSFORMATION FEATURES

_**Ichor of the Gods:**_ Your advantage die is always a d10 instead of a d6.

_**Weight of Divinity:**_ When you roll a failure with Fear, you must mark a Stress or give the GM an additional Fear.
