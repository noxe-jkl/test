# SHAPESHIFTER

Shapeshifters are creatures that can change their physical form. Their identity is fluid, allowing them to adapt to any environment or social situation by mirroring the shapes of others.

## TRANSFORMATION FEATURES

_**Change Shape:**_ During a long rest, you can spend a downtime move to swap your current ancestry with another. When you do, describe how your appearance changes.

_**Only Skin Deep:**_ When you Change Shape, you only gain the benefit of one of the ancestry's features, which you select when you choose the ancestry. You can spend a downtime move to switch which of your ancestry's features you gain the benefit of.
