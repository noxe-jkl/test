**_Tier 1 Skulk_** _A prowling hunter, like a slinking mountain lion, with a slate-gray stone body._ **Motives & Tactics:** Defend territory, isolate prey, stalk

> **Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 7/13 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3 **ATK:** +1 | **Bite and Claws:** Melee | 1d8+2 phy **Experience:** Stonesense +2

## FEATURES

**_Stonestrider - Passive:_** The Stonewraith can move through stone and earth as easily as air. While within stone or earth, they are Hidden and immune to all damage.

**_Rocky Ambush - Action:_** While Hidden, mark a Stress to leap into Melee range with a target within Very Close range. The target must succeed on an Agility or Instinct Reaction Roll (13) or take 1d8 physical damage and become temporarily Restrained.

**_Gravelly Roar - Action:_** Mark a Stress to unleash a disorienting roar. All targets within Very Close range must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll (12) or become Vulnerable until they next act.

**_Momentum - Reaction:_** When the Stonewraith makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
