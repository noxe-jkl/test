# DAMASK AMBUSHER

**_Tier 1 Skulk_**

_A hardened cutthroat and thief who hunts for the Queens._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Evade, hide, ambush, pilfer

> **Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 6/11 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3 
> **ATK:** +1 | **Long Knife:** Melee | 1d6+3 phy 
> **Experience:** Stealth +2

## FEATURES

**_Backstab - Passive:_** When the Ambusher succeeds on a standard attack that has advantage, they deal 1d8+6 physical damage instead of their standard damage.

**_Cloaked - Action:_** Become Hidden until after the Ambusher's next attack. Attacks made while Hidden from this feature have advantage.
