# MINOR OUTER REALMS THRALL

***Tier 3 Minion***
*A vaguely humanoid form stripped of memory and identity.*
**Motives & Tactics:** Destroy, disgust, disorient, intimidate

> Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: None | HP: 1 | Stress: 1
> ATK: +2 | Claws and Teeth: Very Close | 9 phy

## FEATURES

**_Minion (9) - Passive:_** The Thrall is defeated when they take any damage. For every 9 damage a PC deals to the Thrall, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

**_Group Attack - Action:_** Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Outer Realm Thralls within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 9 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
