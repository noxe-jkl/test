# CRAG-CLAMBERING CRAB

***Tier 1 Standard***
*A large, territorial crustacean with a shell that looks like weather-worn rock. They move with an unsettling, sideways scuttle and clatter their stony claws aggressively when approached.*
**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, defend territory, grapple, swarm

> **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5/10 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2
> **ATK:** +1 | **Pincer Snap:** Melee | 1d6+2 phy
> **Experience:** Camouflage +2

## FEATURES

***Rocky Camouflage - Passive:*** When not moving, the crab is difficult to distinguish from the surrounding rocks. The first attack made against a camouflaged crab that has not yet acted in combat has disadvantage.

***Aggressive Scuttle - Action:*** The crab can move up to Very Far range and make a Pincer Snap attack as a single action.

***Claw Lock - Reaction:*** When the crab makes a successful Pincer Snap attack, it can mark a Stress to latch onto its target. The target is **Restrained** until they or an ally succeed on a Strength roll (Difficulty 12) to pry the crab off. The crab cannot attack another target while it is latched on.
