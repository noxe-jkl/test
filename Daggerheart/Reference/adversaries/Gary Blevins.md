# GARY "THE JANITOR" BLEVINS

_**Tier 1 Solo**_ 
_A disgruntled ex-custodian with low-level technopathy, sitting on a throne of hacked appliances._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Prove his genius, protect the machine, animate appliances, shock intruders

> **Difficulty:** 16 | **Thresholds:** 8/15 | **HP:** 9 | **Stress:** 4
> **ATK:** +2 | **Live Wire Lash:** Close | 1d10+2 mag
> **Experience:** Technopathy +3

## FEATURES

_**Relentless (2) - Passive:**_ Gary can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight him.

_**Technopathic Shield - Passive:**_ While Gary is within Close range of an electrical appliance (or the Omni-Java 9000), he has Resistance to Physical Damage.

_**Surge - Action:**_ Mark a Stress to overload a device near a target within Close range. The target must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (13) or take 2d8+1 direct magic damage and become **Stunned** (cannot act until they clear a Stress).

_**Animate Clutter - Action:**_ Spend a Fear to bring 1d4 nearby appliances to life. These act as **Vacuum Minions** or **Toaster Hordes** (GM choice) and immediately take the spotlight to attack.

_**Feedback Loop - Reaction:**_ When Gary takes Magic damage, he can mark a Stress to redirect half the damage to a random Minion or Appliance within Close range.
