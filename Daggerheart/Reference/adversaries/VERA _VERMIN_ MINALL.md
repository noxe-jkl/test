# VERA 'VERMIN' MINALL

**Tier 2 Solo**
This pastry chef owns Iced Delights, as well as a profitable fight club called the Baker’s Dozen. Vera is tall and slim with greasy, greying hair. A dark, leather apron dusted with powdered sugar covers her crimson clothing.
**Motives & Tactics**: Guard her secrets, win at all costs, summon minions, shift to gain an advantage.

> Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 12/24 | HP: 8 | Stress: 5
> ATK: +5 | Silver Dagger: Melee | 2d10+4 phy
> Experience: Leadership +3, Intimidation +2, Dirty Fighter +2

## FEATURES

**_Relentless (2) - Passive:_** The Wererat can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn4. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them5.

**_Pounce - Action:_** Vera leaps at a target. Make an attack against a target within Close range. On a success, deal 2d10+2 physical damage and the target must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll or be knocked prone, becoming Vulnerable until they next act.

**_I'm Not Who You Think I Am - Action:_** Vera reveals her wererat form. Spend 2 Fear to immediately transform. Her claws become her new standard attack (see below). Until the end of the scene or until she marks her last HP, Vera gains a +2 bonus to her Evasion and a permanent +2 bonus to her damage thresholds. She also immediately clears all Stress.

**_Scurry - Action:_** Vera scurries away. Mark a Stress to become Hidden from all adversaries. This move can only be used in an area with a large number of places to hide, such as a crowded room, sewer, or forest.

**_Call for Reinforcements - Action:_** Spend a Fear to have 1d4 of Vera's underlings enter the fight from a hiding spot nearby. Use the Tier 2 Minion stat block for each underling.

**_Vicious Bite - Reaction:_** When Vera makes a successful attack, you can spend a Fear to have her bite the target instead. The attack deals an additional 2d6 direct physical damage.
