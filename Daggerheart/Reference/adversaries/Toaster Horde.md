# TOASTER HORDE

_**Tier 1 Horde (3/HP)**_ 
_A battalion of angry chrome toasters launching searing-hot bread._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Burn, launch projectiles, surround

> **Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 6/11 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 2
> **ATK:** +1 | **Burnt Toast Volley:** Far | 1d8+2 mag (Fire)

## FEATURES

_**Horde (1d4+1) - Passive:**_ When the Toaster Horde has marked half or more of their HP, their standard attack deals 1d4+1 magic damage instead.

_**Pop-Up Scare - Action:**_ Mark a Stress to launch a synchronized volley of toast. All targets within Close range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll or become **Vulnerable** until their next turn.

_**Overheat - Reaction:**_ When the Horde takes Melee damage, mark a Stress to deal 1d6 direct magic damage (heat) to the attacker.
