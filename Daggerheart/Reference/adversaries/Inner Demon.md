# INNER DEMON

***Tier 2 Solo***  
*A demonic manifestation of dark inner thoughts.*  
**Motives & Tactics:** Exploit fears, weaken resolve, take control

> **Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 13/26 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 5  
> **ATK:** +3 | **Psychic Lash:** Very Close | 2d6+7 mag  
> **Experience:** Manipulation +3

## FEATURES

***Mental Game - Passive:*** The Demon has advantage against targets that have marked more Stress than the Demon.

***Bitter Memories - Action:*** Mark a Stress and choose a point within Close range. All targets within Very Close range of that point must make a Presence Reaction Roll. Targets who fail have their spirits weighed down with resurfaced painful memories and must decrease their Hope Die by one step (such as from **d12** to **d10**) until they succeed with Hope or the Demon is defeated.

***Possess - Action:*** Spend a Fear to attempt to take control of a PC's body within Melee range. The target must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll or become *Possessed* by the Demon until they succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll to end the possession. Attacks made against the *Possessed* target damage the target, after which they can attempt a new Instinct Reaction Roll to end the possession. Spotlight the *Possessed* target instead of the Demon. Roll a **d20** instead of Duality Dice to make an attack, and if you use an ability that costs Hope, spend Fear instead.

***Guilt Trip - Reaction:*** When the Demon takes Major or greater damage, the attacker must mark a Stress.
