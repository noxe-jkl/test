#### ONE-SHOT • WEIRD WESTERN, COSMIC HORROR

The party is hired in the desperate Brink-Town of [[Last Stop]] to investigate a surge in [[Tearror]] activity. The source appears to be a nearby precursor ruin, where a strange energy pulse has been detected. The trail will lead them into a confrontation with a cult of [[Khol Seekers]] attempting to tear a hole in reality and summon a being of pure darkness.

---
## Starting the Adventure

Read the following aloud to your players:

>"The air in Last Stop's only saloon, 'The Glimmer,' is thick with synth-ale fumes and quiet desperation. It's a town clinging to the Twilight Line, the ever-shifting border between the blinding glare of the Glint and the absolute, liquid darkness of the Khol. An old prospector named Joric, his face a roadmap of hard years, waves you over. His voice is a low gravel. 'I got a job. Something's stirring out at the Whispering Monolith. The Tearrors are getting bold, and there's a... pulse. A light where there shouldn't be one. Find out what it is, stop it, and I'll make you rich in energy cells. Or you'll die. 'Bout a fifty-fifty shot on Kestrel.'

Last Stop is on edge. The "nights," when the town faces the cosmic ocean of the Khol, have been growing longer, and the attacks by Tearrors—creatures of nightmare born from the dark—have become more frequent and coordinated. Joric, a grizzled scavenger, has connected the attacks to a strange, rhythmic pulse of light emanating from a nearby precursor ruin known as the Whispering Monolith.

Unbeknownst to the townsfolk, a cell of Khol Seekers calling themselves the Penitent Hand has taken up residence in the ruin. Led by a charismatic zealot, they believe the Khol is not an end, but a beginning. They are using precursor technology to amplify a ritual, attempting to summon a powerful Tearror they believe will grant them communion with the Khol itself. This ritual is poisoning the land and drawing lesser Tearrors to it like moths to a dying flame.

Important Characters

- **JORIC**: A human male, 68 years old (use Commoner stats). Joric is a grizzled, pragmatic prospector who hires the party. He's seen too much to believe in anything but survival. 
>"Joric's face is weathered leather, his eyes squinting as if perpetually staring into the Glint. His hands, scarred and calloused, never stray far from the laser pistol on his hip."
    
- **THE PENITENT HAND**: A human male, 34 years old (use Secret-Keeper stats). He is the fanatical leader of the Khol Seekers and genuinely believes the Khol offers salvation.
>Dressed in robes the color of a starless night, his face is serene, almost beatific. His eyes, however, hold the chilling emptiness of the void he worships."
    

Key Locations

- **THE GLIMMER SALOON**: "A dusty, low-ceilinged bar lit by flickering energy lamps. The patrons are a mix of weary prospectors and nervous settlers. The air smells of ozone and cheap synth-ale." This is the social heart of Last Stop, where deals are made and rumors are traded. Joric will provide the party with a crude map and an advance of a few energy cells.
    
- **THE WHISPERING MONOLITH**: "A single, colossal spire of black, unknown material juts from the cracked earth, humming with a sickly purple light. Around its base, lesser precursor structures lie in ruins. The very air feels thin and wrong here." An ancient precursor site, now the location of the cult's ritual. The humming is a sign of its active, and dangerous, power.
    

Suggested Story Flow

GET THE JOB → CROSS THE BRINK → INVESTIGATE THE RUIN → STOP THE RITUAL → COLLECT THE BOUNTY

Secrets and Clues

1. The Khol Seekers believe their ritual will save Kestrel by merging it with the Khol, ending its slow death.
    
2. The Penitent Hand wears a precursor artifact, a "Focusing Crystal," which is amplifying the ritual. It can be repurposed for a laser weapon.
    
3. The "pulse" of light is the Monolith discharging energy, a safety protocol against overload caused by the ritual.
    
4. The Tearrors are not being controlled by the cult; they are simply drawn to the Khol energy like sharks to blood.
    
5. A failed Instinct roll while crossing the Brink reveals a recently abandoned prospector's camp, torn apart by something unnatural.
    

Quest Rewards

Joric will pay the party 2 Bags of Gold (in energy cells and salvage) for stopping the ritual. If The Penitent Hand is defeated, his Focusing Crystal can be recovered. It can be attached to a laser weapon to grant it the Kholbane feature: When damaging a Tearror or Khol-Touched creature, add an additional d6 to the damage roll.

Adventure Difficulty (Level 2-3)

- EASIER: Remove one Khol Fanatic from the final encounter. The summoned Tearror could be a Tier 1 Bruiser like a Cave Ogre, re-flavored.
    
- HARDER: The Penitent Hand has already Imbued one of the Khol Fanatics with Blasphemous Might at the start of the fight. The Summoning countdown starts at 4 instead of 6.

### ACT ONE: THE LAST STOP SALOON

Read the following aloud:

The Glimmer Saloon is the only real gathering place in the dusty Brink-Town of Last Stop. Joric the prospector sits at a corner table, nursing a murky drink. He gestures you over. "Heard you were looking for work. Good. Most folks are looking for a hole to hide in these days." He slides a data-slate across the table, showing a crude map leading to a jagged spire a few hours into the Khol-facing badlands. "The Whispering Monolith. Used to be quiet. Now... it's screaming. Tearrors are crawling all over the Brink since it started. I need you to go there, find the source of that pulse, and shut it down. Permanently. I'll give you five handfuls of cells now, and a bag each when you get back. If you get back."

Joric can provide the following information if asked:

- The pulse is a rhythmic flash of purple light, visible only when the Khol is at its deepest.
    
- The Tearrors are more aggressive and organized than he's ever seen.
    
- He suspects someone is "poking something they shouldn't" out at the monolith.  
    A PC can attempt to negotiate for a better advance with a Presence Roll (12). On a success, Joric grudgingly adds another handful of cells.
    

### ACT TWO: ACROSS THE BRINK

The journey to the Whispering Monolith takes the party across the shifting Twilight Line. The landscape is a stark mix of harsh light and deep shadow, filled with strange, crystalline flora and the petrified bones of giant, unknown creatures.

Read the following aloud:

Leaving the relative safety of Last Stop, you enter the strange, perpetual twilight of the Brink. To one side, the endless, blinding Glint casts razor-sharp shadows. To the other, the Khol waits, a silent, liquid ocean of pure black. The path on Joric's map winds through a canyon of wind-carved rock and petrified trees. The air grows cold, and an unnerving silence presses in as you move deeper into territory claimed by the night.

The party must navigate the treacherous terrain. Ask one player to take the lead and make an Instinct (Navigate) Roll (14).

- Success with Hope: The party finds a safe, quick path, arriving at the monolith feeling confident.
    
- Success with Fear: They find a path, but it takes them through a region heavily tainted by the Khol. Gain a Fear. Each character must make a Strength Reaction Roll (12) against the chilling influence. On a failure, they gain one Khol-Stain (see Kestrel Custom Mechanics below).
    
- Failure: The party gets lost, stumbling into a Tearror nest. They are ambushed by a number of Khol-Shades equal to the number of PCs + 1. After the combat, they find their way, but each character gains one Khol-Stain from the intense exposure.
    

KESTREL CUSTOM MECHANICS: KHOL-STAIN

When a character gains a Khol-Stain, they mark it on their sheet (max 3).

- 1 Stain: The character suffers disadvantage on Presence rolls when interacting with those untainted by the Khol. They might hear faint whispers or see fleeting shadows.
    

Khol-Shade

- Tier 1 Minion
    
- A fleeting silhouette of pure darkness, barely humanoid, with grasping tendrils.
    
- Motives & Tactics: Extinguish light, instill fear, drag prey into shadows
    
- Difficulty: 9 | HP: 1 | Stress: 1 | ATK: -2
    
- Shadow Tendril: Melee, 1 physical damage (fear)
    
- FEATURES:
    

- Minion (3) - Passive: The Khol-Shade is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to the Khol-Shade, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.
    
- Fear Eater - Passive: When a PC within Very Close range rolls with Fear, the Khol-Shade may immediately move into Melee range with that PC.
    

### ACT THREE: THE WHISPERING MONOLITH

The party arrives at the precursor ruin to find the cult's ritual underway. Set up the scene with the Monolith at the center, the cultists arranged around it. The Penitent Hand stands before the Monolith, channeling energy.

Read the following aloud:

You arrive at a clearing dominated by a colossal, humming monolith of smooth black stone. The air crackles with energy. Around its base, robed figures chant in a dissonant chorus. This is the source of the pulse—a ritual. At its center, a man in darker robes, The Penitent Hand, gestures towards the monolith, drawing ribbons of purple energy from it. The ground itself feels wrong, tainted. You must stop this before they complete whatever they've started.

This final encounter uses the Cult Ritual environment. Your goal is to defeat The Penitent Hand, who is the ritual leader. Doing so will disrupt the ritual and end the encounter. If The Summoning countdown reaches 0, a Whispering Horror appears, and the fight becomes much more deadly.

CULT RITUAL (RE-FLAVORED)

- Tier 2 Event
    
- A Khol Seekers cult assembles around a precursor monolith, their bonfire burning with a sickly purple light.
    
- Impulses: Profane the land, unite the Mortal Realm with the Khol
    
- Difficulty: 14
    
- Potential Adversaries: Khol Seekers (The Penitent Hand, Khol Fanatics, Khol Initiates)
    
- FEATURES:
    

- Desecrated Ground - Passive: The Khol's influence seeps into this place. Reduce the PCs' Hope Die to a while in this environment.
    
- Blasphemous Might - Action: The Penitent Hand diverts some of the ritual's power. Choose one Khol Fanatic to become Imbued until they're defeated. An Imbued adversary gains advantage on all attacks and deals an extra damage.
    
- The Summoning - Reaction: Countdown (6). When the PCs enter the scene, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC rolls with Fear. When it triggers, summon a Whispering Horror within Very Close range of the ritual's leader. If The Penitent Hand is defeated, the countdown ends.
    
- Complete the Ritual - Reaction: If The Penitent Hand is targeted by an attack, a Khol Initiate within Very Close range of him can mark a Stress to be targeted by that attack instead.
    

Adversary Tactics:

- The Penitent Hand will stay near the Monolith, using his actions to command his forces and cast spells. He will use Blasphemous Might on a Khol Fanatic early in the fight.
    
- Khol Fanatics will engage the PCs directly, trying to protect their leader. They will use their Shadow Stepper ability to flank and isolate party members.
    
- Khol Initiates will chant and use the Complete the Ritual reaction to absorb attacks meant for their leader. They will use Group Attack if multiple PCs get close.
    

The Penitent Hand

- Tier 2 Leader (re-flavored Secret-Keeper)
    
- A seasoned leader with a direct channel to the whispers of the Khol.
    
- Motives & Tactics: Amass great power, plot, take command
    
- Difficulty: 16 | Thresholds: 13/26 | HP: 7 | Stress: 4 | ATK: +3
    
- Khol-touched Staff: Far, magic damage
    
- FEATURES:
    

- We are the Void's Chosen - Action: Mark a Stress to spotlight allies. Attacks they make while spotlighted in this way deal half damage.
    
- The Khol Demands It - Action: Spend a Fear to summon Khol Initiates, who appear at Close range.
    
- Momentum - Reaction: When The Penitent Hand makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
    

Khol Fanatic (x2)

- Tier 2 Skulk (re-flavored Cult Fang)
    
- A professional killer-turned-cultist, granted power from the shadows.
    
- Motives & Tactics: Capture sacrifices, isolate prey, rise in the ranks
    
- Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 9/17 | HP: 4 | Stress: 4 | ATK: +2
    
- Jagged Precursor Knife: Melee, physical damage
    
- FEATURES:
    

- Shadow Stepper - Passive: Mark a Stress to move from one shadow to another within Far range.
    
- Isolate the Unbeliever - Action: Mark a Stress to cause a target within Melee range to make an Instinct Reaction Roll (15). On a failure, the target must mark 2 Stress and is teleported with the Fanatic to a shadow within Far range, making them temporarily Vulnerable.
    

Khol Initiate

- Tier 2 Minion (re-flavored Cult Initiate)
    
- A low-ranking cultist in simple robes, eager to gain power from the Khol.
    
- Motives & Tactics: Follow orders, gain power, seek forbidden knowledge
    
- Difficulty: 13 | HP: 1 | Stress: 1 | ATK: +0
    
- Ritual Dagger: Melee, 5 physical damage
    
- FEATURES:
    

- Minion (6) - Passive: The Initiate is defeated when they take any damage. For every 6 damage a PC deals, defeat an additional Minion within range.
    
- Group Attack - Action: Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Initiates within Close range. Those Minions move into Melee range and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 5 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
    

Whispering Horror (Summoned)

- Tier 1 Solo (re-flavored Minor Demon)
    
- A crimson-hued creature from the Khol, consumed by rage against all light.
    
- Motives & Tactics: Act erratically, corral targets, relish pain, torment
    
- Difficulty: 14 | Thresholds: 8/15 | HP: 8 | Stress: 4 | ATK: +3
    
- Claws of Nightmare: Melee, physical damage
    
- FEATURES:
    

- Relentless (2) - Passive: The Horror can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.
    
- All Must Fall - Passive: When a PC rolls a failure with Fear while within Close range of the Horror, they lose a Hope.
    
- Khol-Fire - Action: Spend a Fear to rain down purple hellfire within Far range. All targets must make an Agility Reaction Roll (14). Targets who fail take magic damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.
    
- Momentum - Reaction: When the Horror makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

### EPILOGUE: A MOMENT OF LIGHT

If the party defeats The Penitent Hand before the countdown ends:

With the death of their leader, the ritual collapses. The purple energy sputters and dies, plunging the monolith into silence for the first time in weeks. The remaining cultists scatter into the darkness, their will broken. As the unnatural pressure in the air dissipates, you see the first harsh rays of the Glint beginning to crawl over the horizon. You've held back the night, at least for now.

The party can recover the Focusing Crystal from The Penitent Hand's body. Upon returning to Last Stop, Joric pays them their due, nodding with grim respect. The Tearror attacks lessen, and the town breathes a collective sigh of relief.

If the Whispering Horror is summoned:

Even with the cult leader fallen, the damage is done. A tear in the world rips open, and a creature of pure nightmare, all claws and whispers, steps through from the Khol. The ritual was a success. You may have won the battle, but the Whispering Monolith has now become a permanent source of terror on the Brink.

If the party defeats the Horror, they return as grim heroes, but the monolith remains a dangerous, Khol-touched site. If they flee, they return to Last Stop with a dire warning. The town must now live in the shadow of a far greater threat, and Joric's payment feels like cold comfort. Either way, the story of Kestrel continues.
