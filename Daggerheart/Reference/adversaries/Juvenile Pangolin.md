# JUVENILE PANGOLIN

**_Tier 2 Skulk_** _A smaller, quicker pangolin, about the size of a large dog. Its scales haven't fully hardened, making it more agile but also more vulnerable._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Deceive predators, locate food, sneak past threats

> **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 8/16 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2
> **ATK:** +1 | **Claws:** Melee | 2d4 phy 
> **Experience:** Sneak +3, Locate +2

## FEATURES

**_Skittish Retreat - Reaction:_** When a creature moves into Melee range of the Juvenile Pangolin, you can mark a Stress for it to immediately move up to Close range.

**_Fragile Body - Passive:_** When this adversary takes Major or greater damage, it is defeated.
