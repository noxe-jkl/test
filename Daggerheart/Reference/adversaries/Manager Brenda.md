# MANAGER BRENDA

_**Tier 1 Social**_ 
_The team’s boss. Her suit is immaculate, her stare is withering, and her only power seems to be "Weaponized Disappointment."_ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Intimidate subordinates, meet deadlines, avoid the audit, delegate

> **Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 5/10 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 5
> **ATK:** -2 | **Withering Glare:** Close | 1d6+2 mag
> **Experience:** Middle Management +3

## FEATURES

_**Weaponized Disappointment - Passive:**_ When a PC fails a roll against Brenda, they must mark a Stress. If they roll with Fear, they must mark 2 Stress.

_**"My Office. Now." - Action:**_ Brenda singles out a target within Close range. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (13) or become **Vulnerable** and unable to move away from Brenda until they succeed on a Social check or the scene ends.

_**The Audit is Coming - Action:**_ Spend a Fear to remind the team of the looming deadline. All PCs within Earshot must make a Knowledge Reaction Roll. On a failure, they lose a Hope. On a success, they mark a Stress.

_**Corporate Policy - Reaction:**_ When a PC attempts to use a power or ability that would cause property damage or draw attention (ticking the Buzz Track), Brenda can immediately make a Social attack against them. On a success, the PC takes the damage as Stress and their action has **Disadvantage**.
