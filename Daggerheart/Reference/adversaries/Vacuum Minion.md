# VACUUM MINION

_**Tier 1 Minion**_ 
_A sentient canister vacuum that hungers for ankles._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Trip, suction, obstruct

> **Difficulty:** 10 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 0
> **ATK:** -1 | **Suction Hose:** Melee | 2 phy

## FEATURES

_**Minion (4) - Passive:**_ The Vacuum is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Vacuum, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

_**Trip Cord - Passive:**_ PCs moving through a group of Vacuum Minions treat the area as Difficult Terrain.

_**Group Attack - Action:**_ Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Vacuums within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
