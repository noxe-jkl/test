# SPECTRAL BEAST

***Tier 2 Standard***
*A ghostly, skeletal version of a woodland creature, glowing with blue fire and dripping with liquid mana.*
**Motives & Tactics:** Harass, obey summoner, terrifying

> **Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
> **ATK:** +2 | **Ethereal Claws:** Melee | 2d8+3 phy

## FEATURES

***Ghost - Passive:*** The Spectral Beast has resistance to physical damage. It can mark a Stress to move up to Close range through solid objects.

***Spirit Bond - Passive:*** While within Close range of The Starving Lure, the Spectral Beast gains advantage on attack rolls.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When this adversary makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

***Dissipate - Reaction:*** When the Spectral Beast is defeated, it explodes in a burst of cold mana. All targets within Very Close range must make an Agility Reaction Roll (14). Targets who fail take 8 magic damage.
