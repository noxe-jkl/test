# CAVE PANGOLIN

**_Tier 2 Bruiser_** _A large, powerfully built pangolin the size of a small boulder. Its body is covered in thick, overlapping scales of rock-like keratin, and it moves with surprising speed when it curls into a ball._ 
**Motives & Tactics:** Dig, locate threats, protect territory, roll into enemies

> **Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 7 | **Stress:** 3 
> **ATK:** +2 | **Claws:** Melee | 2d8+2 phy 
> **Experience:** Dig +3, Protect +2

## FEATURES

**_Armored Carapace - Passive:_** When this adversary takes physical damage, reduce it by 3.

**_Rolling Charge - Action:_** Mark a Stress to curl into a ball and roll in a straight line up to Far range. Make an attack against all targets in its path. Targets the pangolin succeeds against take 2d8+3 physical damage and are knocked back to Close range.
