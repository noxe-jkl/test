# THE STARVING LURE

***Tier 3 Solo*** 
*A geodesic sphere of cracked crystal, hovering over a pit of liquid mana. It pulses with a frantic, rhythmic thrumming. It is a furnace running on fumes, tearing holes in reality to find fuel.* 
**Motives & Tactics:** Consume magic, stabilize, survive

> **Difficulty:** 18 | **Thresholds:** 20/35 | **HP:** 10 | **Stress:** 6  
> **ATK:** +3 | **Mana Tether:** Far | 3d10+3 magic dmg

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Lure can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

***Arcane Vacuum - Passive:*** The chamber smells of ozone and roasting spices. When a PC casts a spell or uses a magic ability within Close range, they must mark a Stress. If they cannot, the Lure clears 1 HP.

***Unstable Core - Action:*** Mark a Stress. The Lure vents excess energy. All PCs in Very Close range must make a Strength Reaction Roll (16) as gravity distorts. Targets who fail take 2d8+4 physical damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Summon Remnant - Action:*** Spend a Fear. The Lure pulls a memory from the stone. Summon a [[Spectral Beast]] (Tier 2 Standard), which appears at Close range.

***Primal Hunger - Reaction:*** When the Lure takes damage, you may spend a Fear to force the attacker to make an Instinct Reaction Roll (16). On a failure, they are overcome with a sudden, debilitating starvation and become Vulnerable until their next action.
